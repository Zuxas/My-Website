Chapter 01

DECK
IDENTITY

"You're not trying to outgrind them. You're trying to reduce their library to six cards and then mill them out in two attacks."

Dimir Excruciator won Pro Tour Lorwyn Eclipsed 2026 in Christoffer Larsen's hands, going 15-4 (78%). The deck is a combo-control shell built around a two-card instant-kill sequence: Doomsday Excruciator + Restless Reef.

Doomsday Excruciator is a 7/6 Demon with flash and flying. When it enters the battlefield, both players exile cards from the top of their library until each player has exactly six cards remaining. After Excruciator resolves, Restless Reef — a land that becomes a 1/2 creature when it attacks — mills the opponent for 4 cards. They now have 2 cards left. One more draw step kills them. Alternatively, you cast Insatiable Avarice targeting them, forcing them to draw 3 cards from a 2-card library — they mill themselves out immediately.

The supporting cast keeps you alive long enough to assemble the combo. Deceit is both a Distress (discard a nonland) and a bounce spell in one card — and Superior Spider-Man copies Deceit on ETB, giving you a second copy for four mana total. Requiting Hex and Bitter Triumph handle early threats. Harvester of Misery sweeps small creature boards. Winternight Stories discards to put Excruciator in the GY for Superior Spider-Man to copy via discard.

The Two Kill Lines

Kill Line 1 — Reef Attack
Excruciator → Restless Reef Attacks → Insatiable Avarice
Cast Excruciator (both players go to 6 cards). Restless Reef attacks → opponent mills 4 → they have 2 cards. Cast Insatiable Avarice targeting opponent (draw 3) → they draw 2 then mill 1 with empty library → they lose. Full sequence costs 7 mana total across two turns (5 for Excruciator + 2 for Avarice, with Reef attacking for free).
Kill Line 2 — Superior Spider-Man Copy
Discard Excruciator → Superior Spider-Man Copies It
Use Winternight Stories or Bitter Triumph to discard Doomsday Excruciator to your GY. Cast Superior Spider-Man ({2}{U}{B}) — it ETBs and copies Excruciator's enter trigger using its "if you cast it" clause. Both players exile down to 6. Continue to Line 1. This line costs only 4 mana for the "Excruciator effect" and leaves mana for Avarice the same turn.

Register Conditions

Register When
Midrange Is Dominant
Your disruption (Deceit, Intimidation Tactics, Requiting Hex) is excellent against creature and midrange strategies. The combo kills efficiently enough that midrange decks can't race you once Excruciator resolves. Best when the field is Dimir Midrange, Bant Rhythm, and Mono-Green.
Be Cautious When
GY Hate and Flash Creatures Are Prevalent
Soul-Guide Lantern exiles your GY (prevents the Spider-Man copy line). Torpor Orb stops all ETB triggers including Excruciator's exile and Deceit's discard. Flash creatures answering your Excruciator at instant speed are also problematic. Verify the field doesn't have widespread Torpor Orb before registering.

Chapter 03

GAME
FLOW

"Turns 1-4: disrupt and stabilize. Turn 5-6: Excruciator. Turn 6-7: Reef attacks, Avarice kills."
Turns 1–3
Disrupt and Survive
T1-2: Requiting Hex and Intimidation Tactics remove their early threats. Deceit on T2 strips their best card while leaving a 1/1 body. Stock Up on T2-3 digs for your combo pieces. Goal: be at a comfortable life total with their hand emptied or key threats removed by T4.
Turns 3–4
Set Up the Discard Line
Winternight Stories on T3 gives you a discard outlet — use it to bin Doomsday Excruciator from hand. This enables the Superior Spider-Man line on T4: cast Spider-Man ({2}{U}{B}), copy Excruciator's trigger. Both players exile to 6 cards while you have mana up for Insatiable Avarice. Alternatively, cast Insatiable Avarice on T3 to draw 3 and find Excruciator.
Turn 4–5
Execute the Combo
Cast Doomsday Excruciator ({3}{B}{B}) or use Spider-Man to copy the trigger. Both players exile down to 6. On your next turn, Restless Reef attacks (flip it into creature mode) → mills them for 4 → they have 2 cards. Cast Insatiable Avarice targeting opponent ({1}{B}: draw 3) → they draw 2 then mill with empty library → they lose immediately.
Alternate Win
Quantum Riddler Clock
When the combo is disrupted (Excruciator countered, Spider-Man killed), Quantum Riddler from the SB serves as a 4/4 that surveil 2 + draws a card every time it attacks. With 2+ instants/sorceries in GY, Riddler generates 1+ card advantage per turn. In long attrition games, Riddler is your backup kill condition — it threatens lethal in 5 attacks and generates overwhelming card advantage.

Mulligan Framework

Keep: Any 7 with 3-4 lands + disruption (Requiting Hex, Deceit) + any combo piece (Excruciator, Spider-Man, or Insatiable Avarice). Three lands + Deceit + Stock Up is a strong keep regardless of combo pieces — you'll find them.

Mulligan: Hands with 0-1 lands. Hands with Excruciator but no black sources (5 {B}{B} to cast). Hands with 4+ copies of the same card. Any hand that can't interact on T1 or T2 vs aggro.

Chapter 04

CORE
ENGINES

"Superior Spider-Man makes the combo cost 4 mana instead of 5. That one mana lets you hold Insatiable Avarice on the same turn."
Combo Engine
Doomsday Excruciator + Restless Reef
Excruciator ETB: both players exile until each has exactly 6 cards remaining. This is symmetrical — you also go to 6. Plan your hand carefully. Restless Reef attacks as a 1/2 and mills 4. After one attack, opponent has 2 cards. Insatiable Avarice (draw 3 targeting them) = they draw 2, try to draw a 3rd from empty library = they lose.
Copy Engine
Superior Spider-Man + Winternight Stories
Discard Excruciator with Winternight Stories. Cast Superior Spider-Man ({2}{U}{B}) — it copies Excruciator's enter trigger because Spider-Man preserves "if you cast it" clauses. Both players exile to 6, leaving you with {B} or {1}{B} for Insatiable Avarice in the same turn. The copy line executes the full combo in one turn for 6 total mana.
Disruption Engine
Deceit + Superior Spider-Man
Deceit ({1}{B}) is both Distress (target opponent discards a nonland) and a mini-bounce (return a nonland permanent with MV ≤ 2 to hand) on one card. Superior Spider-Man copies Deceit's ETB — so casting Spider-Man with Deceit in GY effectively gives you a second Deceit effect plus a 4/4 body for {2}{U}{B}. This is why Spider-Man is a 4-of: it's both the combo enabler AND a tempo engine.

Mana Sequencing

Critical
You Need 5 Black Mana for Excruciator
Doomsday Excruciator costs {3}{B}{B}. With 11 Swamps + 4 Watery Grave + 4 Gloomlake Verge (enters untapped with Swamp/Island), you reliably hit double black by T4-5. Don't try to cast Excruciator on T4 with only 1 basic Swamp in play — verify your black mana sources before committing.
Critical
Restless Reef Needs to Not Have Summoning Sickness
Restless Reef is a land that becomes a 1/2 creature when you activate its ability. It has summoning sickness the turn it enters. Play Restless Reef BEFORE casting Excruciator — ideally on T3-4, well before the combo turn. Reef that entered the same turn as Excruciator cannot attack that turn, breaking the two-turn kill.
Chapter 05

LINES &
TACTICS

"After Excruciator, count your six remaining cards. Know exactly which are combo pieces before attacking with Reef."
Line 01 — Critical: Excruciator Is Symmetrical
Doomsday Excruciator exiles both players' libraries down to 6. YOU also go to 6 cards. Before casting Excruciator, look at your hand and calculate: after exile, what 6 cards do you want in your library? Stock Up (look at top 5) before Excruciator lets you order your library favorably. Ideally your bottom 6 contains Insatiable Avarice and multiple land drops. Never cast Excruciator with Insatiable Avarice in your library unless it's one of the top 6 — otherwise it gets exiled.
Line 02 — Critical: Superior Spider-Man Preserves "If You Cast It" Clauses
Doomsday Excruciator's exile trigger says "when this creature enters the battlefield, if you cast it." Superior Spider-Man copies that trigger even though you didn't "cast" the copy — because Spider-Man specifically preserves "if you cast it" clauses from the original. This is the core rules interaction that makes the copy line work. Note: if Excruciator enters via another copy effect that doesn't have this clause, the exile trigger won't trigger.
Line 03 — Critical: Insatiable Avarice Targets the Opponent to Kill
Insatiable Avarice ({1}{B}: target player draws 3 cards) is the finisher. After Reef mills them to 2 cards, target the OPPONENT with Avarice. They draw 2 cards (now 0 in library), attempt to draw the 3rd from an empty library, and lose. Do NOT target yourself. Also note: Avarice can be cast proactively in the early game targeting YOURSELF as a 3-card draw for {1}{B} — this is correct when you're digging for Excruciator.
Chapter 06

ROLE
ASSIGNMENT

"You're always the control deck pretending to be combo. They have to respect the combo even when you're just casting removal."
Primary Role
Reactive Control
Answer their threats with Requiting Hex and Deceit. Use Harvester of Misery to sweep small boards. Use Bitter Triumph as flexible removal. Build toward the combo with Stock Up and Insatiable Avarice. Don't rush the combo — stabilize first, kill second.
vs Aggro, Midrange
Secondary Role
Disruptive Combo
Strip their hand with Deceit + Superior Spider-Man before executing the combo. Empty their hand of interaction before casting Excruciator. Prioritize Intimidation Tactics to exile their key threat before they can cast it — proactive exile is better than reactive removal.
vs Control, Mirror
Backup Role
Quantum Riddler Grind
When Excruciator is answered or the combo is disrupted, shift to Riddler beatdown. Riddler + Deceit bodies + Harvester tokens close games in 4-6 turns. Deadly Cover-Up as Surgical Extraction disrupts their engine. You can win fair games — you just prefer not to.
Backup Plan

SIDEBOARD
GUIDE

"Torpor Orb stops their ETBs AND your own — don't board it if you still need Excruciator's exile trigger."
Priority 1
Duress ×4
Your most versatile SB card. Strips countermagic before Excruciator, removes planeswalkers, hits Monument to Endurance, removes High Noon. Board in every non-aggro matchup. The 4-of is justified — you want multiples in the hand before committing to the combo.
Priority 2
Quantum Riddler ×3
Backup win condition when Excruciator is repeatedly answered. A 4/4 that generates card advantage every attack (surveil 2 + draw 1) is excellent in attrition matchups. Board in: Dimir Midrange, Jeskai Control, mirror. Provides a threat they must answer immediately.
Priority 3
Torpor Orb ×2
Stops ALL ETB triggers — including Excruciator. WARNING: If you board in Torpor Orb, you cannot use Doomsday Excruciator (no ETB). The Orb is for matchups where you plan to win through fair creature beatdown (Riddler, Deceit bodies) and want to shut down their ETB-based engines (Mono-Green Landfall's Icetill combo, Bant Rhythm's Gene Pollinator). Do NOT board Torpor Orb in matchups where you're still combo-killing.
Priority 4
Shoot the Sheriff ×2 + Cruelclaw's Heist ×1
Shoot the Sheriff is removal for non-outlaw creatures that Requiting Hex doesn't cover (MV 3+). Board in vs large-creature strategies. Cruelclaw's Heist is targeted exile — removes their key permanent (Monument to Endurance, Restless Reef in mirror, key enchantments) and lets you steal it. One-of flex slot.

Chapter 09

HEURISTICS

  1. 01Play Restless Reef early — before it matters. Reef needs to be in play without summoning sickness before the combo turn. Play it on T3 or T4 even if you don't need the mana. A Reef that can't attack the turn after Excruciator resolves breaks the two-turn kill.
  2. 02Excruciator is symmetrical — you also go to 6 cards. Before casting Excruciator, use Stock Up or Insatiable Avarice to order your library. Your 6 remaining cards should include Insatiable Avarice and at least 2 lands.
  3. 03Superior Spider-Man copies Excruciator's trigger via "if you cast it" preservation. Discard Excruciator with Winternight Stories, cast Spider-Man — Spider-Man's ETB copies the exile trigger. This is the 4-mana combo line that leaves mana for Insatiable Avarice on the same turn.
  4. 04Insatiable Avarice targeting the opponent is the kill — not targeting yourself. After Reef mills them to 2 cards, Avarice forces them to draw 3 from a 2-card library. Target the opponent. Using Avarice on yourself in the combo turn draws you cards but doesn't kill.
  5. 05Torpor Orb kills your own combo. Never have Torpor Orb in play if you're planning to win through Excruciator. The Orb stops Excruciator's ETB exile trigger. Only board Orb in configurations where you're winning through Quantum Riddler beatdown.
  6. 06Deadly Cover-Up is also Surgical Extraction. After killing their Monument to Endurance, use Deadly Cover-Up — exile all remaining copies from their hand, library, and GY. In the Izzet Lessons matchup, this removes all 4 Monuments permanently after resolving one.
  7. 07Deceit targets nonland permanents with MV ≤ 2 for the bounce mode. The bounce mode returns a permanent opponent controls with MV ≤ 2 to their hand. Requiting Hex (0 MV), Stormchaser's Talent (2 MV), Artist's Talent (2 MV), Deep-Cavern Bat (2 MV) are all bounce targets. Use this to buy a turn against their key early permanents.
  8. 08Stock Up digs 5 deep — use it the turn before combo to order your library. Stock Up (look at top 5, put 2 in hand, 3 on bottom) lets you arrange what 3 cards are at the "top" of your library when Excruciator fires. Put the cards you need in the 6 remaining (Avarice, lands) at the bottom of the 5 rather than burying them under the cards you want.
Chapter 10

TUNING
NOTES

Core — Never Cut
Restless Reef + Excruciator + Spider-Man
The combo requires both Excruciator (or Spider-Man as the copy) and Restless Reef. 4 Reef ensures you always have it in play. 3 Excruciator + 4 Spider-Man gives 7 ways to execute the exile effect. Don't touch these numbers.
Flex Slot
Archenemy's Charm: 1-2
Flexible removal — modal spell that handles different threats. The single copy in Larsen's list reflects that Requiting Hex and Bitter Triumph cover most removal needs. Run 2 in aggro-heavy metas where the extra flexible removal matters. Cut to 0 vs pure combo where removal is dead.
Adjust For Meta
Winternight Stories: 2-3
2 is Larsen's number. Run 3 in metas where the Spider-Man copy line is your primary combo path (vs lots of removal answering Excruciator directly). Winternight discards for multiple turns and generates value beyond just enabling Spider-Man — the life gain from the Stories side is real vs aggro.

Know Cold
The Kill Sequence Step by Step
1. Restless Reef in play without summoning sickness. 2. Discard Excruciator (Winternight) OR cast Excruciator directly. 3. Spider-Man copies OR Excruciator resolves → both players exile to 6. 4. Reef attacks → mills 4 → they have 2 cards. 5. Cast Insatiable Avarice targeting them → draw 3 → they lose. Know every step before sitting down.
Know Cold
Torpor Orb Interaction
Torpor Orb stops ALL ETB triggers including Excruciator's exile and Deceit's discard and Harvester of Misery's sweep. If Torpor Orb is in play (yours or theirs), none of these work. Know which cards in your deck are ETB-dependent before boarding Torpor Orb. In the Orb configuration, you win through Riddler and Deceit bodies only.
