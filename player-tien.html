The Player

How long have you been playing?
Since Neon Dynasty — February 2022. Less than four years ago. What's happened since then would take most players a decade.
What got you into competitive play?
Couldn't lose at the kitchen table. His buddies told him he needed a format with real restrictions. He picked Modern. He proceeded to learn the game much faster than most people do and immediately wanted to grind. That's the whole story.
"I kept beating him. He bought me a hat."
— ON HOW HE MET ZUXAS

Results

Proudest result?
Winning his first RCQ after playing for just over a year. Then making it a pattern — he's qualified for every competitive season he's cared about since, in both Modern and Pioneer. He has not failed to qualify in any season he's genuinely tried. That's not a streak that happens by accident.

The Game

How do you think at the table?
Four questions, in order: What do I die to. How do I win. What does a winning game look like. How do I thread the needle to steal the match. He knows his decklist cold and can reconstruct a majority of his opponent's list from memory before seeing a card. The game is already partially solved by the time he sits down.
"I know when I 'have' to go for the win vs when I'm allowed to take a turn to sculpt more."
What do most players get wrong?
Pressure points. Most players don't have a clear model for when the situation demands they go for it versus when they have margin to play for a better position. Tien does. He knows which turns are free and which aren't — and that awareness creates decisions his opponents never see coming.
The Deck He Always Returns To
Mono-U Belcher — since MH3. Consistent combo that uses interaction and free spells to win on turn 4, but can drop into a draw-go control game when needed. "Consistent combo with a lot of interaction and free spells." The flex between combo and control within the same 75 is exactly the kind of ambiguity he navigates best.
Hardest Matchup He Learned to Navigate
Not answered directly — but his framework says it all. Every matchup that used to beat him became a solved problem through the same process: identify the pressure point, map the winning line, thread the needle. The process scales to any matchup.

Keep the streak. He hasn't failed to qualify for a competitive season yet — and based on everything above, that's not changing anytime soon.
