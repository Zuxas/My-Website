SECTION QUALITY BAR (from Goryo's Vengeance standard):
  Identity:  Oracle text verified. Edge cases include non-obvious rules only.
  Gameplan:  Mulligan is card-grid-3 with specific hands, not vague conditions.
  Engines:   Priority order explicit. Speed math includes exact mana costs.
  Lines:     3 priority lines at top. Edge cases under category headers.
  Roles:     Switching signals are explicit triggers, not vibes.
  Matchups:  Every matchup has point edges. SB note explains the logic.
  Tuning:    Register conditions are specific (%, meta density), not generic.
  Prep:      Print button prints only the SB matrix on 1 page portrait.
