Chapter 01

DECK
IDENTITY

"You play control until you're ready. Then you discard Magma Opus, target the Treasure token with Indomitable Creativity, Gearhulk enters, flashes Opus back for free. Their board is gone, you have a 5/6 and four mana worth of effects. The game ends."

Izzet Creativity is Pioneer's premier combo-control hybrid. Unlike pure aggro or pure control, it operates from a reactive position until it assembles the two-card combo — then transitions instantly into an overwhelming tempo advantage. The combo is elegant: discard Magma Opus to create a Treasure token (via Fable of the Mirror-Breaker's Chapter 2 or Prismari Command), then target that non-creature token with Indomitable Creativity. Since Torrential Gearhulk is the only creature in the deck, Creativity always hits it. Gearhulk's ETB flashes back Magma Opus from the GY for free — dealing 4 damage, tapping four permanents, drawing two cards, and making a Treasure. For {3RRR} you generate a free 8-mana spell and a 5/6 creature.

The deck's pre-combo phase looks like a standard Izzet control build: Fiery Impulse and Divide by Zero answer early threats. Make Disappear and Prismari Command provide permission and card selection. Fable of the Mirror-Breaker generates Treasure tokens (combo enablers), loots away Magma Opus into the GY, and threatens to transform into Reflection of Kiki-Jiki for an additional win condition. The deck rewards patient play and punishes opponents who tap out at the wrong moment.

Why Izzet Creativity At 3%

The Combo
Combo That Doesn't Lose to Removal
The Treasure token created by Fable or Prismari Command is not a creature — it can't be targeted by creature removal or killed in combat. Indomitable Creativity targeting a Treasure is effectively uncounterable by creature-based hate. Your combo piece is a non-creature artifact that removes itself when the combo fires. They need to counter Indomitable Creativity itself or have the Gearhulk exiled — not interact with the setup.
The Control Shell
Fully Interactive Until You Go Off
Unlike Lotus Field or Greasefang that are racing on a linear path, Creativity plays real control Magic until it fires. Fiery Impulse, Make Disappear, Divide by Zero, and Prismari Command all answer threats, buy time, and enable the combo simultaneously. You never feel like you're doing nothing — every spell serves double duty as interaction and setup.
The Power
Magma Opus Is an 8-Mana Spell for {3RRR}
Magma Opus at full cost is unplayable at {6UUR}. Cast for free via Gearhulk it's the most broken spell in Pioneer. Dealing 4 damage, tapping four opponent permanents, drawing 2 cards, and making a Treasure while deploying a 5/6 body in one action creates an insurmountable tempo advantage against virtually every deck. Opponents who survive need to deal with both the Gearhulk AND the aftermath.

Chapter 03

GAME
FLOW

"T1-T4: play control, answer threats, set up Fable. T5: fire the combo. They die on T6-7."
Early (T1–T3)
Control Mode
Answer early threats: Fiery Impulse kills MV-1 creatures (spell mastery gives 3 damage by T2). Divide by Zero bounces any nonland permanent. Make Disappear is your soft counter — {1U} Mana Leak. Don't deploy Fable yet unless you're sure the board is safe — an early Fable getting answered wastes a critical combo enabler. Play reaction, not action.
Setup (T3–T4)
Fable + Magma Opus in GY
T3: Deploy Fable of the Mirror-Breaker. T4 upkeep: Chapter 2 — discard Magma Opus, draw a card, create a Treasure. This puts Opus in your GY and creates the non-creature target for Creativity. Alternatively: Prismari Command on T3 discards Opus and makes a Treasure in one shot. You need Magma Opus in GY + a non-creature token/artifact on board + {3RRR} available.
Combo (T5)
Indomitable Creativity → Gearhulk → Magma Opus
Cast Indomitable Creativity targeting the Treasure (or any other non-creature token). Reveal library until you hit a creature — always Torrential Gearhulk (only creature). Gearhulk ETB: cast Magma Opus from GY for free. Tap four opponent permanents (shut down their attacks), deal 4 damage, draw 2 cards. The game is over functionally — they have a tapped board and you have a 5/6 with two cards up.
Mulligan
Need: Lands + Interaction + Fable/Prismari Path
Keep: 3 lands (including at least one red source for Creativity), 1-2 interaction pieces, and either Fable or Prismari Command. Mulligan: fewer than 2 lands, 0 interaction (you die to aggro before comboing), or a hand with 2+ Magma Opus (dead cards until Fable arrives). At 6: 3 lands + Make Disappear + Fiery Impulse is keepable. At 5: 3 lands + Fable is fine — you find interaction naturally.
Chapter 04

CORE
ENGINES

"Fable creates tokens AND loads GY AND transforms into a win condition. Every chapter does something."
Engine 1 — The Setup
Fable of the Mirror-Breaker
Chapter 1: create a 2/2 Goblin Shaman that makes a Treasure when it attacks. Chapter 2: discard and draw — put Magma Opus in GY and draw a card, OR discard dead cards and draw gas. Chapter 3: transform into Reflection of Kiki-Jiki — copy any nonlegendary creature you control when it enters. Reflection of Kiki-Jiki copying Torrential Gearhulk creates a 5/6 haste that flashes back another Magma Opus. Fable alone can win the game if Creativity never fires.
Engine 2 — The Combo
Indomitable Creativity → Gearhulk
Indomitable Creativity ({X}{R}{R}{R}): for each target artifact/creature you control, exile it and reveal the top of your library until you find an artifact or creature with the same MV or less. Since Gearhulk is the only creature, X=1 always hits it. The Treasure token is the correct target — it can't be killed by creature removal, making the combo resilient. Gearhulk is a 5/6 that flashes back any instant or sorcery from your GY for free on ETB.
Engine 3 — The Control Package
Make Disappear + Divide by Zero
Make Disappear: {1U} counter unless they pay {2} or you sacrifice a creature/artifact. Casualty 1 mode sacrifices the Goblin Shaman token from Fable to hard-counter anything. Divide by Zero: {1U} bounces any nonland permanent with MV 3 or less, then learn (access your lesson cards or draw). Flexible answers that double as card selection in the mid-game. Together they answer any permanent type without spending real resources.
Chapter 05

LINES &
TACTICS

"Bait them into tapping out before you fire Creativity. The Treasure target is removal-proof. Creativity at instant speed if possible."
Line 01 — The Perfect Creativity Turn: Bait Before Firing
The best Indomitable Creativity turns happen when opponents have tapped out. The sequence: end of their turn, cast Prismari Command (they might counter it, burning countermagic). Untap. Discard Magma Opus to Fable Chapter 2. Cast Creativity targeting the Treasure. They have no mana open to counter it. When you have Magma Opus in hand and a Fable in play, don't immediately fire — wait for a turn where they tap out for a big spell or you have enough mana to hold up Make Disappear as backup.
Line 02 — Make Disappear Casualty: Sacrifice Goblin Shaman for Hard Counter
Make Disappear normally costs {1U} and is a Mana Leak (counter unless they pay {2}). But with Casualty 1: sacrifice a creature with power 1 or greater to copy the spell — the copy counters unconditionally. The Goblin Shaman token from Fable Chapter 1 (2/2) satisfies Casualty 1. Sacrifice it: two copies of Make Disappear trigger, but Casualty copies are hard counters. Use Casualty specifically for critical spells (Teferi, Greasefang ETB trigger, Collected Company, Emergent Ultimatum). Regular Make Disappear is fine for early drops.
Line 03 — Targeting Your Own Permanents With Creativity for Removal
Indomitable Creativity can target "any number of target artifacts and/or creatures you control." This means you can target your Goblin Shaman token with Creativity — it gets exiled and you hit Gearhulk. Alternatively, if you have a Gearhulk in play already and want another one, target the Shaman to get a second Gearhulk. Two Gearhulks = two free Magma Opus casts = eight 4-damage hits, tap four permanents twice, draw four cards. The game ends immediately.
Line 04 — Reflect Kiki-Jiki Copying Gearhulk: The Backup Win
Fable Chapter 3 transforms into Reflection of Kiki-Jiki — whenever a nonlegendary creature enters under your control, you may create a token copy of it with haste that's exiled at EOT. When Torrential Gearhulk enters (via Creativity or hardcast), Reflection immediately copies it — a 5/6 haste token that also triggers Gearhulk's ETB: cast another instant/sorcery from GY for free. The haste copy attacks and deals combat damage before being exiled. This double-Gearhulk line generates two free Magma Opus casts in one turn even without a second Creativity.
Chapter 06

ROLE
ASSIGNMENT

"You play control until T5. From T5 onward you're the combo deck. Know when to switch."
vs Aggro
Answer Everything, Then Combo
Against RDW and Selesnya: use ALL your removal to stay alive. Fiery Impulse + Volcanic Spite kills most threats. Brotherhood's End (SB) sweeps the board. Once stabilized at 6+ life, fire the Creativity combo — Magma Opus taps their remaining threats and the Gearhulk closes. Don't rush the combo while you're dying to their board.
vs Midrange
Tempo Them Out
Against UR Cutter and Dimir Midrange: Divide by Zero bounces their key threats (Vivi Ornitier, Floodpits Drowner). Make Disappear counters their setup spells. Your Fable of the Mirror-Breaker out-values their mid-range plays. Fire the Creativity combo when they're tapped out after deploying a threat — ideally the turn after they play their most impactful card.
vs Control
Bait Counters, Then Resolve Creativity
Against UW Control: their Dovin's Veto counters Indomitable Creativity (it's a noncreature spell they can counter). Bait their veto on Fable or a draw spell. Then fire Creativity when their countermagic is spent. Hullbreaker Horror (SB) is your best game 2 threat — it can't be countered by spells and bounces any permanent on each spell you cast.

P1 — vs Control
Hullbreaker Horror ×2
7-mana 7/8 with flash. Can't be countered by spells. Whenever you cast a spell, you may return target spell or permanent with MV ≤ 7 to its owner's hand. Against UW Control: Hullbreaker entered at flash speed bounces Teferi before it untaps. Every spell you cast after bounces another permanent. They can't keep anything in play. The uncounterable clause bypasses Dovin's Veto. Hard cast on T7 with mana from Magma Opus Treasures.
P2 — vs Aggro Boards
Brotherhood's End + Anger of Gods
Brotherhood's End ({1RR}): deals 2 damage to each creature and each player, OR 3 damage to each artifact. Sweeps RDW's one-drops including Eidolon. Anger of the Gods ({1RR}): deals 3 damage to each creature with toughness 3 or less AND exiles them. No GY recursion. Hits Thalia, Llanowar Elves, Voice of Resurgence, Monastery Swiftspear, Kellan. Anger specifically prevents CoCo recursion via Extraction Specialist. Both cost {1RR} — playable on T3 after setting up Fable.
P3 — vs Greasefang
Rending Volley ×2
{R} instant: destroy target white or blue creature. Can't be countered. Hits Greasefang (white/black 3/3 — wait, Greasefang is a rat which is BW). Actually Rending Volley hits white creatures — relevant vs Selesnya Company (Thalia, Voice of Resurgence, Knight of Autumn) and UW Control threats (Brimaz, Wandering Emperor tokens). For Greasefang specifically, Fiery Impulse at 3 damage (spell mastery) hits the 3/3. Rending Volley is your {R} instant-speed answer to white threats.
P4 — vs Draw/Midrange
Narset, Parter of Veils
3-mana planeswalker: static ability — opponents can't draw more than one card per turn. Shuts off Treasure Cruise (a {U} spell drawing 3 becomes draw 1), Behold the Multiverse, Consider, Opt. Against UR Cutter and Arclight Phoenix, Narset counters their entire card advantage engine. Deploy alongside interaction — they must answer Narset before drawing any cards. Her -2 looks at top 4 cards and keeps a noncreature/land card.
Chapter 09

HEURISTICS

"Bait their countermagic. Use Creativity into empty mana. Fable Chapter 2 is how Opus enters the GY."
  1. 01Fable Chapter 2 is how you discard Magma Opus — use it immediately when available. Chapter 2's "discard a card and draw a card" is the primary way to put Magma Opus into your GY without paying its full 8-mana cost. Use this ability the turn it becomes available. Don't wait to find Creativity first — get Opus in the GY, then find Creativity.
  2. 02The Treasure token is removal-proof — target it with Creativity, not the Goblin Shaman. Tokens from Fable Chapter 1 (the 2/2 Goblin Shaman) can be killed by removal. The Treasure token is an artifact — no creature removal hits it. Always target Treasures with Creativity. If you target Shaman and they kill it in response, Creativity has no legal targets and fizzles.
  3. 03Fire Creativity into empty mana — never into open countermagic if possible. Indomitable Creativity can be countered by Dovin's Veto, No More Lies, or any hard counter. Wait for turns where they've tapped mana to deploy a threat or answer your spell. The best Creativity turns come when you bait their interaction and then fire with no mana available to respond.
  4. 04Make Disappear Casualty 1 on the Goblin Shaman = hard counter. Sacrifice the 2/2 Shaman from Fable Chapter 1 to Casualty 1 on Make Disappear — the copy counters unconditionally. Reserve this for must-counter spells: Teferi, Greasefang, Emergent Ultimatum. Normal Make Disappear handles everything else.
  5. 05Magma Opus tap-four targets: hit their best four permanents, not random ones. When Magma Opus resolves, tap up to four permanents. Target: the four creatures threatening to kill you next turn, their mana sources (especially if they need specific colors for an answer), or key planeswalkers before they can activate. Don't tap random lands — tap the blockers, attackers, or resources that matter.
  6. 06Reflection of Kiki-Jiki + Torrential Gearhulk = two free Magma Opus casts. When Fable transforms into Reflection of Kiki-Jiki and you play Gearhulk (via Creativity or hardcast), Reflection immediately creates a haste token copy. The copy triggers Gearhulk's ETB — a second free Magma Opus. Plan for this line when Fable has transformed — a single Creativity generates two free Opus casts in that turn.
  7. 07Divide by Zero bounces any nonland MV 3 or less — don't forget the Learn effect. Divide by Zero also lets you Learn: look at your sideboard and add a card to your hand. Your sideboard has Mascot Exhibition (tokens), Teachings of the Archaics (copy), Environmental Sciences (lands + life). In the mid-game when you're stable, using Divide by Zero defensively and taking a lesson card adds significant value.
  8. 08Prismari Command has four modes — use all of them depending on the situation. Prismari Command: (1) deal 2 damage to any target, (2) counter target artifact or enchantment spell, (3) discard and draw, (4) create a Treasure. In aggro matchups: 2 damage mode kills a one-drop + 3 from impulse. In combo setup: discard Magma Opus and draw + create Treasure. Against Damping Sphere or Lotus Field: counter the artifact spell. Know all four modes before tapping mana.
  9. 09Hardcasting Torrential Gearhulk at 6 mana is fine in long games. Don't treat Gearhulk as only a Creativity target. In long control games with 6 mana available, hardcasting Gearhulk and flashing back any instant or sorcery for free is a legitimate play. Flash back Make Disappear for a free counter. Flash back Fiery Impulse for free removal. A hardcast Gearhulk at 6 mana is still a 5/6 with an ETB.
  10. 10Indomitable Creativity X=2 can target two non-creature tokens for two Gearhulks. If you have two Treasure tokens (or a Treasure and the Goblin Shaman), Creativity for X=2 targets both non-creature artifacts and hits two Gearhulks from the library. Wait — there's only one Gearhulk in play at a time, but if you have 2 Gearhulks in the library, X=2 finds both. More commonly: X=2 targeting a Treasure and an opponent's permanent removes their threat while getting your Gearhulk.
Chapter 10

TUNING
NOTES

"4 Gearhulk, 4 Magma Opus, 4 Fable are the core. Interaction count adjusts to meta speed."
Lock
4 Torrential Gearhulk + 4 Magma Opus
The combo requires Gearhulk in the library and Opus in the GY. 4 of each maximizes Creativity reliability (always hits) and ensures Opus is available for the flashback every game. Never cut any.
Lock
4 Fable of the Mirror-Breaker
The primary Opus discard outlet AND the primary Treasure generator AND an alternate win condition. Fable is the reason the deck functions. 4 copies ensures you draw it consistently by T3.
Flex 3–4
Indomitable Creativity
3 is reference. 4 in metas where firing the combo ASAP is critical. 3 in metas where the control shell can win without Creativity (via Reflection of Kiki-Jiki or Hullbreaker). The 3 copies ensure you draw it without the risk of redundant copies in the combo window.
Flex 3–4
Fiery Impulse + Volcanic Spite
7 total removal spells is correct in most metas. In heavy aggro metas, go to 8-9. In control-heavy metas, trim to 5-6 and add more permission. The ratio between removal and counters adjusts to the expected threats.
Flex 2–4
Make Disappear
4 in permission-heavy metas where you need redundant counters. 2-3 in aggro metas where you'd rather have more removal. Make Disappear is weakest against decks that can pay the {2} (control) and best vs combo and midrange.
Meta Call
Creature Lands
Hall of Storm Giants (3/3 ward when attacking) and Den of the Bugbear (Goblin + Haste) provide resilient win conditions that bypass Creativity entirely. In control-heavy metas where Creativity gets countered, running 2-3 creature lands gives you alternate pressure that works through their interaction.
Chapter 11

EVENT
PREP

"Know when to fire the combo. Know what they can counter. Bait before firing."
Know Cold
The Bait-Before-Fire Protocol
Against any deck with countermagic: (1) cast something expendable at end of their turn — Prismari Command, Behold the Multiverse, Divide by Zero. (2) Watch if they counter it. (3) If they counter: their mana is spent, untap, fire Creativity. (4) If they don't counter: the card drew you into Opus/Creativity, proceed. Never fire Creativity into open blue mana without knowing their hand or having bait resolved.
Know Cold
Magma Opus Four-Tap Priority
When Magma Opus taps four permanents, it's often the most important decision in the game. Default priority: (1) their active threats attacking you next turn, (2) mana that enables their key answer, (3) planeswalkers before next activation, (4) anything with a relevant activated ability. Never tap random basics — tap the four things that win them the game if left untapped.
Verify
SB for Expected Field
Heavy control (UW Control): Hullbreaker × 2 + Disdainful Stroke × 2 + Negate. Heavy aggro (RDW, Selesnya): Brotherhood's End + Anger of Gods. Heavy GY combo (Greasefang, Phoenix): Rending Volley + Narset. Confirm 15 cards map cleanly to expected matchups. Creativity's SB is highly flexible — 12 of 15 slots flex.
