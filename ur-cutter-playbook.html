Chapter 01

DECK
IDENTITY

"You are the most popular deck in Pioneer for a reason. Every cheap spell is a Monk. Every Monk has prowess. You kill on turn 3 and you can't be hated out."

Cori-Steel Cutter is the engine. The equipment's Flurry ability reads: whenever you cast your second spell each turn, create a 1/1 white Monk token with prowess, then you may attach the Cutter to it. Every cantrip past the first is a free 1/1 that grows with every subsequent spell. A hand with Cutter plus five cheap spells presents a T3 kill — not an exaggeration.

Pioneer gives this strategy tools Standard never had. Treasure Cruise for {U} after filling your graveyard with cantrips draws three cards and refuels the engine from empty. Consider adds a fourth instant-speed cantrip that also mills one — more Delve fuel. Slickshot Show-Off from the sideboard closes games where the creature plan is disrupted. The deck is faster than Greasefang and less fragile than Phoenix.

The key insight: Cori-Steel Cutter triggers on your second spell each turn — not just your turn. Opt and Consider at instant speed mean you can cast one on their end step and trigger Cutter during their turn, untapping with an additional Monk already in play. The Monks multiply faster than opponents expect.

Why UR Cutter Is #1

Speed
Kills Before Hate Matters
T1 Stormchaser's Talent or Vivi Ornitier. T2 Cori-Steel Cutter + cantrip = Monk token immediately. T3 three spells = three prowess triggers + new Monk. The damage output by turn 3 forces opponents to have interaction or die, and the deck runs enough cheap threats that any single answer isn't enough.
Resilience
No Graveyard Dependency
Unlike Izzet Phoenix, you don't need to recur anything. Cutter generates new threats from nothing. Getting hit by Unlicensed Hearse or Soul-Guide Lantern does nothing to you. Greasefang hate (Grafdigger's Cage, Rest in Peace) is completely dead against this deck. Your opponents' hate packages are misconfigured.
Reach
Treasure Cruise Refuels
When your hand empties at 8 life, the opponent thinks they stabilized. Then you Treasure Cruise for {U} and draw three more spells. The Delve clock means your GY isn't wasted — every cantrip discarded or milled by Consider is mana for the refuel. Opponents can't durdle past your initial burst and survive the second.

UR Cutter vs Izzet Phoenix

UR Cutter (This Deck)
Non-GY Aggro
Proactive threats that don't need the GY. Cutter generates tokens from spells cast. More resilient to GY hate. Faster kill clock. Worse against lifegain strategies. Weaker to board wipes — Monks are 1/1s until pumped.
Izzet Phoenix
GY Combo
Arclight Phoenix recurs from GY every turn with three instants/sorceries. Slower kill but more inevitability in long games. Demolished by GY hate. Better against board wipes (Phoenix comes back). Same cantrip/Cruise shell.

Key Card Notes

The Engine
Cori-Steel Cutter — Flurry
Equipment with Flurry: whenever you cast your second spell each turn, create a 1/1 white Monk creature token with prowess, then you may attach Cutter to it. The token immediately has the equipment and will grow with every additional spell. Crucially: triggers any turn — yours OR opponent's. Cast Opt on their end step after already casting a spell earlier and you get a free Monk that untaps ready to attack. Equip cost {1}{R} to move to an existing creature.
The Refuel
Treasure Cruise — Draw 3 for {U}
Each cantrip you cast or mill via Consider fills your GY. By turn 4–5, Treasure Cruise costs {U} and draws three cards. This is Pioneer's best card advantage spell in this shell. Critical rule: Delve exiles cards from YOUR GY to pay for generic mana costs. Each exiled card = {1} paid. With 7 cards in GY, Treasure Cruise costs {U}. Counts from the GY only — no GY, no discount. Don't over-Delve early if you want a second Cruise later.
The Cantrips
Opt + Consider + Sleight of Hand
12 cantrips is not an accident. Every cantrip is: (1) a Cutter trigger if it's your second spell, (2) a prowess pump on Vivi/Stormchaser, (3) Delve fuel for Treasure Cruise, (4) card selection to find your kill. Opt and Consider are instant-speed — they trigger Cutter on your opponent's turn. Sleight of Hand is sorcery — cast it on your main phase before combat. Always count your cantrips: two = Cutter trigger, three = two triggers, etc.
The Closer
Vivi Ornitier + Stormchaser's Talent
Vivi Ornitier (FINAL FANTASY): when it enters or whenever you draw your second card each turn, it gets +1/+1 permanently. Combined with Treasure Cruise (drawing 3 = triggering Vivi twice), a freshly cast Vivi can be a 3/3 or larger immediately. Stormchaser's Talent: creates a 1/1 Otter with prowess for {U}, then grows it whenever you draw your second card each turn (+1/+1 permanently). Both scale with the cantrip engine.
Chapter 03

GAME
PLAN

"T1 threat. T2 Cutter + cantrip = first Monk. T3 three spells = board full of pumped Monks. They die."
Ideal Curve
T1–T3 Kill Setup
T1: Stormchaser's Talent ({U}) — Otter token in play. T2: Cori-Steel Cutter ({1}{R}) + Opt ({U}) — Cutter Flurry triggers, second Monk created, Cutter attaches to it. Two creatures attacking for 2+2 before pumps. T3: Sleight of Hand + Consider + Fiery Impulse — each spell pumps all creatures +1/+1 via prowess. Three spells = four triggers total (three prowess on each creature). Board presents 12+ damage.
Without Cutter
Pure Prowess Beats
Even without Cutter, Vivi Ornitier and Stormchaser's Talent are strong standalone threats. Vivi grows permanently every time you draw your second card per turn — a Treasure Cruise immediately makes Vivi a 3/3. Chain cantrips freely. Stormchaser's Otter also grows permanently. The creatures are not just Cutter payoffs — they win independently if Cutter is removed or countered.
Mulligan
Need Threat + Cutter + Cantrips
Keep: any hand with a T1–T2 threat (Stormchaser/Vivi), Cori-Steel Cutter, and 2+ cantrips, on 2 lands. Mulligan: all lands and no spells, all spells and no lands, no Cutter and no way to find it quickly. At 6: keep threat + Cutter + 1 cantrip on 2 lands. At 5: need Cutter + 2 spells + 2 lands — anything less is too slow.
End Game
Cruise Into the Win
When your hand empties after the initial burst, check GY count. 7 cards = Treasure Cruise for {U}. Draw three, reload, attack again. The second wave of spells pumps your existing board AND creates more Monks via Cutter. This is why the deck beats fair midrange: they answer your initial threats and then die to the reload. Keep 2 lands up post-combat for Cruise + one more spell.
Chapter 04

CORE
ENGINES

"One Cutter. Twelve cantrips. Four Treasure Cruises. The math always works out."
Engine 1 — Primary
Cutter Token Factory
Every spell past the first each turn = new 1/1 Monk with prowess + Cutter attached. A turn with four spells creates three Monks (second, third, fourth spell each trigger). Each Monk pumps +1/+1 for every additional spell cast that turn. With three existing Monks and three spells: 9 prowess triggers across the board. The math compounds fast.
Engine 2 — Refuel
Delve Loop
12 cantrips fill your GY every game. Consider mills one additional card on resolution. By T3–T4 you routinely have 5–8 cards in GY. Treasure Cruise for {U}–{2}{U} draws three new spells. Those three spells create more Monks, fill the GY further, and enable the next Cruise. You can cast two Cruises in a game and win from an empty hand.
Engine 3 — Permanent Growth
Vivi + Stormchaser Scaling
Vivi Ornitier grows permanently when you draw your second card per turn. A Treasure Cruise drawing three cards triggers Vivi twice in one spell (you draw cards 1, 2, 3 — second draw triggers, done). A single Cruise can put +2/+2 on Vivi permanently. After 2–3 Cruises, Vivi is a 5/5 or larger. Stormchaser's Otter scales the same way. These aren't 1/1 jokes late in the game.
Chapter 05

LINES &
TACTICS

"Cutter triggers on their turn too. Always count your spells across the full turn cycle."
Line 01 — Cutter Flurry Triggers on ANY Turn — Sequence Instant-Speed Cantrips Strategically
Cori-Steel Cutter's Flurry triggers whenever you cast your second spell each turn — this includes your opponent's turn. The correct sequence when you have Cutter in play: cast one spell on your main phase (Sleight of Hand), pass the turn. On their end step, cast Opt or Consider (second spell of the turn) — Flurry triggers, new Monk created. Untap with one MORE creature than they knew about. This is not a corner case — it's the baseline play pattern every turn once Cutter is active.
Line 02 — Treasure Cruise Delve: Exile YOUR Graveyard, Not Theirs
Delve exiles cards from YOUR graveyard only. You choose which cards to exile when you cast the spell. Don't exile cards you might need later (a second Cruise, a Vivi you want back via some other effect). The exile is part of casting the spell — if Cruise is countered, the exiled cards stay exiled. Plan: exile cantrips and lands, preserve any copies of Treasure Cruise in your GY for sequencing purposes (though you can only legally have 4 in the deck). Most importantly: don't over-exile early — leave enough in GY for a second Cruise next turn.
Line 03 — Stormchaser's Talent Otter Grows on Your SECOND Draw, Not All Draws
Stormchaser's Talent reads: "whenever you draw your second card each turn, the Otter gets +1/+1 permanently." This triggers once per turn — the second draw only. If you draw a card for your draw step (first draw) then cast Opt (second draw) — Otter trigger. If you then cast Consider (third draw) — no additional trigger that turn. Treasure Cruise counts as: draw 1 (no trigger), draw 2 (TRIGGER: Otter +1/+1), draw 3 (no trigger). So one Cruise = one Otter trigger. Two Cruises in a turn = two Otter triggers = +2/+2 total.
Line 04 — Cutter Equip vs Creating New Monks: Choose Wisely
When Cutter's Flurry triggers, you MAY attach it to the new token — it's optional. Sometimes you don't want to move Cutter. Example: Cutter is on a Vivi Ornitier that's a 4/4. A new 1/1 Monk is created. If you move Cutter to the Monk, your 4/4 Vivi loses the equipment. Usually: keep Cutter on your largest creature. Move it only if the new Monk is going to take lethal damage in combat or if the largest creature is going to be removed. The 1/1 Monk attacks anyway via its own prowess — it doesn't need Cutter attached to be threatening.
Chapter 06

ROLE
ASSIGNMENT

"You are always the aggressor. Every opponent is on a clock. The question is just how fast."
vs Combo
Race — All In
Against Greasefang, Lotus Field, Niv: you are faster. Go all in on the clock. Don't hold up Spell Pierce preemptively — cast it as another spell for Cutter triggers and save counters only if their combo is resolving. Goldfishing at maximum speed is correct. A turn-3 kill doesn't care about Parhelion II.
vs Midrange
Race With Disruption
Against Rakdos Midrange: apply pressure while holding Into the Flood Maw for their key threat. Don't let Thoughtseize strip Cutter — lead with a creature threat if you have both. After the first wave is answered, Treasure Cruise reloads. Their removal is 1-for-1 and you have twelve cantrips — eventually you run them out of answers.
vs Control
Slickshot Plan B
Against UW Control: they sweep your Monks. Board in Slickshot Show-Off. Plot it early, resolve it after their sweeper, attack for lethal with the Plot attack triggers. Into the Flood Maw bounces their Teferi before they can -3. Force them to have every answer — you have more threats than they have answers in post-board games.

P1 — vs Control/Sweepers
Slickshot Show-Off ×3
Plot Slickshot for its plot cost, exile it face down. After their sweeper resolves (or at any convenient moment), pay {1}{R} to deploy it without casting from hand. It attacks and gets +2/+0 for each instant/sorcery you cast this turn. Board in against: UW Control, Esper, any deck with Anger of the Gods or Extinction Event. Unlicensed Hearse ×2
Artifact that exiles X cards from any GY (X = number of creature types among exiled cards). Against Greasefang: activate immediately to exile vehicles and Greasefang from their GY. Spell Pierce ×2 + Annul ×2

HEURISTICS

"Count your spells before every combat. Know exactly how much damage your board will deal."
  1. 01Cutter triggers on their turn — use instant-speed cantrips at end of step. Cast one spell on your main phase, pass. On their end step, cast Opt or Consider. Cutter triggers for the second spell. Untap with one more Monk than they planned for.
  2. 02Count GY before your main phase every turn. 7 cards = Treasure Cruise for {U}. Knowing exactly when Cruise becomes one mana tells you when to switch from aggression to refuel mode. Don't cast Cruise at {3}{U} when you could wait one turn for {U}.
  3. 03Lead with a creature before playing Cutter vs discard. If they have Thoughtseize, showing Cutter gives them the choice. Deploy Vivi or Stormchaser first. They discard something else, then you play Cutter safely next turn.
  4. 04Don't move Cutter off your largest creature unless that creature is dying. The Flurry Monk is a new 1/1 with prowess — it doesn't need Cutter to be good. Keep Cutter on your highest-power creature. Moving it to a 1/1 Monk means your 4/4 Vivi stops generating new threats.
  5. 05Vivi Ornitier gets +1/+1 on your SECOND draw each turn, once per turn. Your draw step = draw 1 (no trigger). Cast Opt = draw 2 (TRIGGER: +1/+1). Treasure Cruise = draw 1, draw 2 (TRIGGER: +1/+1), draw 3 (no more triggers). Understand the cap so you don't over-value multiple draw effects on the same turn.
  6. 06Fiery Impulse kills more with Spell Mastery — always check. Fiery Impulse deals 2 damage normally, 3 damage if you have 2+ instant/sorcery cards in your GY (Spell Mastery). By T2 you almost always have 2+ instants/sorceries in GY from cantrips. Assume Impulse kills 3-toughness creatures in most situations.
  7. 07Otawara, Soaring City bounces permanents at instant speed for free (channel). Channel Otawara: return target nonland permanent to hand for {3}{U} (reduced by {1} per legendary creature you control). With two legendary creatures, costs {1}{U}. Use on their planeswalker or key threat in response to removal on your creature. Don't cycle it as a land without evaluating the channel option first.
  8. 08Into the Flood Maw is tempo, not card advantage. It bounces their creature and cycles for a card. Use proactively on their key blocker before your alpha, or defensively on their Kalitas/Trespasser before combat. The looting (discard a card, draw a card) happens on resolution — plan what you'll discard to fill your GY faster.
  9. 09Slickshot Show-Off attacks as if it had flying. When it attacks, it gets +2/+0 for each instant or sorcery you've cast this turn. Cast three instants in your pre-combat main, attack with a 7/2 flier. Plot it safely before playing it — the plot lets you deploy at any sorcery speed moment without tapping mana, past sweepers.
  10. 10Annul is a hard counter only for artifacts and enchantments. Don't try to Annul Thoughtseize (sorcery). Annul hits: Cori-Steel Cutter (artifact equipment), Stormchaser's Talent (enchantment), Hearse (artifact), Collected Company is an instant — doesn't hit. Know the card type before boarding Annul in.

TUNING
NOTES

"4 Cutter + 12 cantrips + 4 Cruise is the core. Flex everything else."
Lock — Never Cut
4 Cori-Steel Cutter
The entire engine. Four copies ensures you find it by T2 consistently. Running fewer makes the deck dramatically worse — you become just a mediocre Prowess deck rather than the format's dominant aggro-combo strategy.
Lock — Never Cut
12 Cantrips (4+4+4)
Opt + Consider + Sleight of Hand. The Delve fuel, the Cutter triggers, the card selection. Any cantrip cut reduces the consistency of Treasure Cruise AND the Cutter token engine simultaneously. 12 is the right number.
Lock — Never Cut
4 Treasure Cruise
The refuel that makes the deck unbeatable in attrition. Four copies ensures you draw it after the first burst. Running fewer means opponents stabilize and you can't reload. The best card advantage spell in this shell by a massive margin.
Flex 0–4
Into the Flood Maw
2 main is standard. In aggro-heavy metas, cut entirely — it's slow for creature racing. In midrange/control metas, run 3–4. The loot + bounce is excellent tempo vs Rakdos and control. Also fills GY for Delve.
Flex 1–3
Slickshot Show-Off (SB)
3 copies is correct for any meta with UW Control or significant sweeper strategies. If your meta is creature-heavy (RDW, Selesnya), trim to 1–2 and add more removal. Slickshot is your Plan B, not your Plan A.
Register When
Any Non-Hate Meta
UR Cutter is correct whenever the meta isn't specifically targeting it with sweepers and counterspells. If UW Control is dominant (above 15% meta), consider switching to a more resilient strategy. Otherwise, register Cutter — 23% meta share doesn't happen by accident.
Rule 01
Cutter Crew Commitment
You crew Cori-Steel Cutter only when you have a plan. Either: (1) you have pump spells to protect the attacker, or (2) Cutter's attack is lethal this turn. You never crew Cutter into unknown information — you check their mana and calculate whether they can block or remove your crewing creature before committing.
Rule 02
Slickshot Plot Timing
You plot Slickshot Show-Off at their EOT, not during your main phase. Plotting at their EOT: you plot for {1}{R}, exile Slickshot face-down, then your next turn chain cantrips (each gives +2/+0) before deploying Slickshot from exile. You time the plot to align with your highest-spell-density turn.
Rule 03
Free Spell Identification
