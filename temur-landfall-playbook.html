Chapter 01

DECK
IDENTITY

"You play lands. Then you play more lands. Then Lumra enters and returns all of them. You have a 12/12 and twenty lands in play. They have seven cards in their hand and none of them matter."

Temur Landfall is Standard's midrange-combo hybrid that weaponizes land drops into an overwhelming board state. The deck operates on two overlapping plans: a fair midrange plan where Icetill Explorer and Spelunking generate land-drop acceleration, and a combo plan where Lumra, Bellow of the Woods enters the battlefield and immediately returns every land card from your graveyard back to the battlefield — triggering landfall for each one.

Spelunking is the deck's most important setup card. It makes each land you control a copy of all lands you control — meaning every basic land you play enters as an untapped triome. More critically, Spelunking means cycling lands (sacrificed fetches, Fabled Passage) enter untapped rather than tapped. Icetill Explorer provides the acceleration: whenever a land enters from your graveyard (triggered by Lumra, Spelunking activations, or fetch cracks), you may search your library for another land and put it onto the battlefield tapped.

The payoff is Lumra, Bellow of the Woods: a creature whose power and toughness equal the number of lands you control. When it enters, it mills four cards, then returns all land cards from your graveyard to the battlefield tapped — each triggering Icetill Explorer, each triggering any landfall payoffs in play. A single Lumra with five lands in your GY enters as a 10+ power creature and generates 5-10 more landfall triggers.

Why Temur Over Gruul or Simic Landfall

Blue Splash
Spelunking + Canyon Crab
Blue adds Spelunking (the combo's core enabler) and Canyon Crab — an instant-speed 0/4 blocker with flash that mills cards when it enters (feeding Lumra's GY). Spelunking alone justifies the blue splash: it converts every land into a fixing land and enables Icetill Explorer's graveyard triggers. Without blue you're playing a slower Gruul version that can't combo as consistently.
Red Splash
Sear + Soul Immolation + Worldsoul's Rage
Red provides removal and reach. Sear ({1R}) deals 2 damage to any creature or planeswalker — instant speed. Soul Immolation exiles a creature and deals damage equal to its power. Worldsoul's Rage deals damage to any target equal to lands you control — with 8+ lands it's a 8+ damage finisher or kills any creature. These red spells answer threats the Gruul version can't handle.
The Combo
Lumra Returns Every Land From GY
Lumra, Bellow of the Woods enters, mills 4, then returns ALL land cards from your GY to battlefield tapped. With Spelunking in play and 5 GY lands, Lumra enters as a 5+5=10/10 (lands in play + GY lands), returns them all, and each triggers Icetill Explorer for additional land searches. The board state after one Lumra is almost impossible to overcome.

Chapter 03

GAME
FLOW

"T2 Spelunking. T3 Icetill Explorer. T4 start filling GY with fetch lands. T5 Lumra — board is enormous. Win."
Early (T1–T3)
Setup Phase
T1: Land. T2: Spelunking ({1G}) — the most important play in the deck. With Spelunking in play every land you control gains all land types, enabling Icetill Explorer triggers and making all fetches more efficient. T3: Icetill Explorer ({3G}) — 2/4 body that adds the cascade engine. With Spelunking + Icetill online, every land entering from your GY chains into another search. Start cycling fetches and activating Fabled Passage to fill your GY.
Mid (T4–T5)
GY Loading + Lumra
T4-T5: Use Canyon Crab (flash 0/4 that mills on ETB), Inspiration from Beyond (mill + draw), and Seismic Sense (landfall trigger cards) to load lands into GY. Play Lumra, Bellow of the Woods — it mills 4, then returns all GY lands to battlefield. Icetill triggers chain. Lumra is now massive. Worldsoul's Rage at end step (= your land count) finishes what Lumra can't.
Combat
Lumra + Worldsoul's Rage Close
After the Lumra chain, your lands in play = Lumra's P/T. 12-20 power trample is typically lethal. If Lumra is blocked: Worldsoul's Rage targets face for X damage where X = lands you control — with 15 lands that's 15 damage for {2RG} sorcery speed. The combo can kill through blockers, life gain, and most interaction because it generates so much board presence simultaneously.
Mulligan
Need: Spelunking + 3 Lands
Keep: any hand with Spelunking or Icetill Explorer and 3 lands (at least one green source). Spelunking on T2 is the ideal line — hands without it are slower but functional. Mulligan: 1-2 land hands, all Lumra and no setup, hands with 5+ lands and no spells. At 6: Spelunking + 3 lands + any creature = keep. At 5: Icetill Explorer + 2 lands + Spelunking = keep.
Chapter 04

CORE
ENGINES

"Spelunking enables Icetill. Icetill chains. Lumra converts the chain into a kill. Three engines, one turn."
Engine 1 — Enabler
Spelunking — Every Land Is Everything
Spelunking ({1G}) enchantment: each land you control gains all subtypes of all lands you control AND all activated abilities of all lands you control. Mechanically: with one Plains + one Island + Spelunking, every land you control is Plains Island Swamp Mountain Forest simultaneously. This means: (1) Icetill Explorer triggers off ANY land entering from GY, not just basic types. (2) Fabled Passage enters untapped if you control 4+ lands. (3) Every tap for mana can produce any color.
Engine 2 — Cascade
Icetill Explorer — Chain Every GY Land
Icetill Explorer ({3G}) 2/4: whenever a land card enters from your graveyard, you may search your library for a land card and put it onto the battlefield tapped. With Spelunking in play making every land a fetch-land type, Icetill fires on: Lumra's return trigger, Fabled Passage sacrifice, and Canyon Crab mill hits. Each Icetill trigger finds another land, which if it enters from GY triggers Icetill again. A single Lumra trigger with Icetill cascades through 5-10+ land searches.
Engine 3 — Payoff
Lumra, Bellow of the Woods — The Kill
Lumra ({5GG}) creature: P/T = number of lands you control. When it enters, mill 4 cards, then return ALL land cards from your GY to battlefield tapped. The ETB: (1) mills 4 — adds more lands to GY, (2) returns ALL GY lands simultaneously. With 5 GY lands + Icetill: each returned land triggers Icetill searching more, those enter tapped. Lumra's P/T updates after ALL land ETBs resolve. A typical Lumra play ends with 12-20 lands in play and Lumra at 12/12–20/20.
Chapter 05

LINES &
TACTICS

"Spelunking before Icetill. Canyon Crab at instant speed during their turn to mill lands. Stack Lumra triggers carefully."
Line 01 — Deploy Spelunking Before Icetill Explorer
Spelunking must be in play before Icetill Explorer for the cascade to work. Icetill says "whenever a land card enters from your graveyard" — with Spelunking, every land you control has the fetch-land subtype, so cycling or activating a land that enters from GY counts. Without Spelunking, Icetill only fires on literal Basic land types. Always sequence Spelunking (T2) before Icetill (T3-T4). A T3 Icetill with no Spelunking is a 2/4 body that rarely chains.
Line 02 — Canyon Crab at Instant Speed to Mill Lands Pre-Lumra
Canyon Crab is a 0/4 flash creature that mills X cards where X = its power (0 unless buffed). Wait — actually Canyon Crab mills cards equal to the number of lands you control when it enters. Cast Canyon Crab on your opponent's end step before your Lumra turn to mill 4-6 cards, loading more lands into your GY before Lumra fires. More lands in GY = more lands returned by Lumra = bigger Lumra + more Icetill chains. This is the critical sequencing decision: Canyon Crab should be cast the turn before Lumra, not after.
Line 03 — Stacking Lumra's Triggers in the Right Order
When Lumra enters: (1) mill 4 trigger goes on stack, (2) "return all GY lands" trigger goes on stack. Let the mill resolve first (it may add more lands to GY), then let the return trigger resolve. When the return trigger resolves, all GY lands enter simultaneously as a batch — each one triggers Icetill separately. Resolve Icetill triggers one at a time, searching lands for each. At the end of the Icetill chain, count your lands: Lumra's P/T has updated in real time. This ordering maximizes the GY before the return fires.
Line 04 — Worldsoul's Rage as Both Removal and Finisher
Worldsoul's Rage ({1RG}) sorcery: deals damage equal to lands you control to any target. With 8+ lands this kills any creature in Standard. With 15+ lands it deals lethal to face. Use it: (1) as removal for large threats (Lumra, Earthbender Ascension) mid-game, (2) as a finisher when Lumra is blocked or they have multiple blockers, (3) targeting face the turn after Lumra when they tap out to cast a Spell Snare or answer. Don't cast it for less than 5 damage — there are better removal spells below that threshold.
Chapter 06

ROLE
ASSIGNMENT

"You are always developing your board toward the Lumra turn. Every action either fills the GY, adds lands, or sets up the combo."
vs Aggro
Survive to Lumra
Against Izzet Prowess and Mono-Red: Canyon Crab (0/4 flash) blocks their one-drops indefinitely. Sear kills their small threats. Soul Immolation exiles their key threat. Blossoming Tortoise gains life. Your goal is reaching 7 mana for Lumra with enough GY loaded to end the game immediately. You cannot answer every threat — stabilize with Canyon Crab and one removal spell, then combo kill.
vs Midrange
Out-Value and Overpower
Against Mono-Green Landfall and Dimir Midrange: your Lumra is bigger than their Lumra or their largest creature. Eumidian Terrabotanist generates additional land drops to accelerate. Worldsoul's Rage answers their 5-6 toughness threats. Seismic Sense draws cards on landfall triggers. Your strategy: deploy Spelunking + Icetill faster, get Lumra one turn earlier than them, win the battle of large creatures.
vs Control
Bait Counters, Then Combo
Against Dimir Excruciator and Izzet Spellementals: Spelunking and Icetill Explorer are low-priority targets — let them counter those and play Lumra into a depleted counterbank. Tannuk, Memorial Ensign protects your spells (ward effect). Consult the Star Charts provides additional card selection. Mossborn Hydra (SB) is uncounterable via Cavern of Souls and forces them to use permanent removal instead of counters.

Chapter 09

HEURISTICS

"Spelunking before Icetill. Canyon Crab the turn before Lumra. Stack triggers: mill first, then return. Worldsoul's Rage targets face when you have 10+ lands."
  1. 01Always play Spelunking before Icetill Explorer. Spelunking is what makes Icetill Explorer's trigger fire. Without Spelunking, Icetill only chains off basic-typed lands entering from GY — very limited. With Spelunking, every land has every type. T2 Spelunking, T3/T4 Icetill is the correct sequencing. Reversing this loses the cascade engine.
  2. 02Canyon Crab should be cast the turn before Lumra, not after. Canyon Crab mills X cards where X = number of lands you control when it enters. Casting it at your opponent's EOT the turn before you play Lumra loads more lands into GY — Lumra then returns more lands and grows bigger. Cast Canyon Crab after Lumra and those milled lands don't help Lumra's ETB at all.
  3. 03Stack Lumra's triggers: resolve mill first, then return GY lands. Both abilities trigger on Lumra's ETB and go on the stack. Let the mill resolve first (adds more lands to GY). THEN let the return trigger resolve (returns all GY lands to battlefield). This maximizes the number of lands returned. The mill-then-return order is the only correct order.
  4. 04Worldsoul's Rage hits face when you have 10+ lands — use it as a finisher not just removal. At 10 lands it deals 10 damage. At 15 lands it deals 15 damage. After the Lumra chain you will almost always have 10+ lands. If Lumra is being blocked or chump-attacked to death, Worldsoul's Rage to face for 12-15 wins the game around blockers. Don't cast it for less than 6 damage unless you're killing a must-answer creature.
  5. 05Icetill Explorer's trigger is optional — don't chain if you'd deck yourself. Icetill says "you may search your library." In the late game with very few library cards, choose not to search to avoid decking. Count your library before starting the Lumra chain and make sure you have enough lands left for the chain you plan. This is rare but important to be aware of.
  6. 06Fabled Passage should be cracked when Icetill Explorer is in play for double landfall. Cracking Fabled Passage puts a land from library onto battlefield — that's one landfall trigger. The Passage itself then goes to your GY (it's a land card) — with Icetill + Spelunking, you search for another land from the Icetill trigger. Two land drops for one Passage activation. Save Passage cracks for turns when Icetill is in play.
  7. 07Blossoming Tortoise accelerates your land curve by one — deploy it on T3 when behind. Blossoming Tortoise ({2GG}) reduces the activation costs of lands you control. With Spelunking, all your lands have each other's activated abilities — Blossoming Tortoise reduces all of them by {1G}. It also allows land taps for {1} less. Useful for reducing Fabled Passage's sacrifice cost (from {0} to free — doesn't apply, already free) and for getting to 7 mana for Lumra one turn faster.
  8. 08Soul Immolation is your best removal — exile prevents landfall recursion against Mono-Green. Soul Immolation exiles a creature and deals damage equal to its power to any target. Against Mono-Green Landfall: exile their Lumra (prevents it from being recurred or entering GY for their own chain). Against Izzet Prowess: exile their 4/4 Icetill Explorer and deal 4 damage to face simultaneously. The exile clause matters specifically against decks with GY recursion.
  9. 09Famished Worldsire is a win condition, not ramp — deploy it only when lethal is on board. Famished Worldsire ({5GG}) 7/7 trample: when it attacks, sacrifice all your lands, then return them all to play. This is a massive landfall trigger generator for landfall-payoff decks, but in this deck it primarily serves as a secondary Lumra-sized threat. Don't sacrifice lands to it unless you're confident the resulting board wins the game — losing all lands and not winning is catastrophic.
  10. 10Eumidian Terrabotanist lets you play two lands per turn — deploy before your main combo turn. Terrabotanist ({2G}) 2/3: you may play an additional land on each of your turns. An extra land drop the turn before Lumra means one more land entering your battlefield, one more potential Icetill chain, and one more land for Lumra's P/T. Deploy on T3 if you have it — the earlier it's in play, the more bonus lands you accumulate before the combo turn.
Chapter 10

TUNING
NOTES

"3 Spelunking, 3 Icetill, 3 Lumra are immovable. Everything else adjusts to meta speed."
Lock
3 Spelunking + 3 Icetill + 3 Lumra
These nine cards are the deck's entire reason for existing. Spelunking enables Icetill; Icetill enables Lumra to chain. Never cut below 3 of any. More than 3 is redundant (too many in opening hand without the others).
Lock
3 Canyon Crab
Flash 0/4 that mills and blocks — the best defensive piece and GY loader simultaneously. 3 is correct. 4 risks flooding in hands where you need spells. Cut to 0 in post-board matchups with no relevant aggro (vs pure control).
Flex 1–2
Famished Worldsire
1 copy is the reference. A second copy in slower metas where you need a second massive threat. Cut entirely vs aggro (too expensive, lands matter more). The 7/7 trample body and attack trigger are powerful but the deck already wins with Lumra — Worldsire is a redundant closer.
Flex 1–3
Consult the Star Charts
1 main is the reference. Go to 2-3 in metas where finding Spelunking on T2 is critical (heavy aggro where a slow start loses). Consult finds any land including Spelunking's targets and adds draw. Cut in slower metas where you have time to find your pieces naturally.
Meta Call
Removal Suite (Sear / Soul Immolation)
Current 2/2 split is correct for a balanced field. Heavy aggro: go 3/3. Heavy combo/control: cut all removal and add Consult + Inspiration from Beyond for speed. The removal exists to survive — if you don't need survival, replace with card selection.
Land Count
30 Lands Is Correct
High land count is mandatory — Lumra's P/T = lands in play. More lands = bigger Lumra. 30 lands in a 60-card deck means 50% of your deck is lands. The deck wants to find land every turn. Never cut below 28, optimal is 30-31 depending on the number of cycling/fetch lands.

EVENT
PREP

"Know the Lumra chain. Know trigger order. Know what Worldsoul's Rage kills at each land count."
Know Cold
Lumra Trigger Resolution Order
Step 1: Lumra ETB — put mill trigger AND return-GY trigger on stack. Step 2: Mill trigger resolves first — mill 4 cards, add more lands to GY. Step 3: Return-GY trigger resolves — all GY land cards enter battlefield tapped simultaneously. Step 4: Each entering land triggers Icetill Explorer separately (if in play) — resolve each Icetill trigger for additional land search. Step 5: After all triggers resolve, Lumra's P/T = total lands in play. This ordering is the entire deck's payoff — practice it until it's automatic.
Know Cold
Worldsoul's Rage Damage Chart
5 lands = 5 damage (kills most 5-toughness creatures). 8 lands = 8 damage (kills Lumra, Mossborn Hydra). 10 lands = 10 damage (kills face from 10). 12 lands = 12 (kills from 12). 15 lands = 15 (kills from 15 after Lumra chain). Know this before each event — it determines whether you cast Worldsoul's Rage as removal, a combat trick, or a finisher. At 12+ always target face unless they're at more than 12 life.
