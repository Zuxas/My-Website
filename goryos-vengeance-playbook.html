Chapter 01

Deck
Identity

"Two mana instant. Legendary creature from the graveyard. Haste. Exile at end of turn — unless you Ephemerate it first. Then it stays forever. Then Ephemerate rebounds. Then it enters a THIRD time. Atraxa reveals 30 cards. Griselbrand draws 21. Pick your poison."

Goryo's Vengeance is Modern's fastest reanimator combo. The namesake card — Goryo's Vengeance ({1}{B}, instant) — returns a legendary creature from your graveyard with haste, but exiles it at end of turn. The trick: Ephemerate ({W}) blinks the creature before the exile trigger, creating a new game object that no longer has the "exile at EOT" clause. The creature stays permanently. Ephemerate then rebounds next upkeep for a THIRD ETB trigger. With Atraxa, Grand Unifier, that's three "reveal top 10, take one of each card type" triggers — you've seen 30 cards and taken up to 24. With Griselbrand, that's a 7/7 flying lifelink that draws 7 cards per 7 life paid.

The deck backs this up with Psychic Frog (discard fatties to GY while growing), Solitude (free removal that also combos with Ephemerate), and a full Esper interaction suite. When the combo is disrupted, you pivot to fair Frog beats or Solitude + Ephemerate value. This is not a glass cannon — it's a combo deck with a legitimate control backup plan.

Oracle-Verified Edge Cases

Goryo's + Ephemerate — Why the Exile Clause Disappears
Goryo's creates a delayed trigger: "Exile [this creature] at the beginning of the next end step." This trigger tracks the specific GAME OBJECT that entered from the GY. When Ephemerate exiles and returns the creature, it's now a NEW game object. The delayed trigger from Goryo's still tries to exile the old object — which no longer exists on the battlefield. The trigger does nothing. The creature stays permanently. This is the fundamental interaction that makes the deck work.
Ephemerate Rebound — Fires Even If You Didn't Cast It From Hand the First Time
WRONG — rebound ONLY works if you cast Ephemerate from your HAND. "If you cast this spell from your hand, exile it as it resolves. At the beginning of your next upkeep, you may cast this card from exile." If Ephemerate is cast from the graveyard (flashback, Snapcaster) or from exile, it does NOT rebound. From hand = 2 blinks. From anywhere else = 1 blink. This matters for sequencing — always cast Ephemerate from hand when possible.
Goryo's Vengeance Targets LEGENDARY Creatures Only
Goryo's says "target legendary creature card." It cannot reanimate: Psychic Frog (not legendary), Emperor of Bones (not legendary), Archon of Cruelty (not legendary), Quantum Riddler (not legendary). It CAN reanimate: Atraxa (legendary ✓), Griselbrand (legendary ✓), Ulamog (legendary ✓), Solitude (NOT legendary — Solitude is just a creature). The legendary restriction means your GY targets are limited to your 5-9 legendary fatties.
Solitude Evoke + Ephemerate — Response Window
Evoke Solitude (exile a white card from hand): Solitude enters → ETB exile trigger + evoke sacrifice trigger both go on the stack. You control both triggers and choose the order. Stack the sacrifice trigger BELOW the exile trigger. Exile trigger resolves first → remove their creature. Then before sacrifice resolves → cast Ephemerate targeting Solitude → Solitude blinks → returns as new object → sacrifice trigger tries to find the old Solitude → it's gone → sacrifice fizzles. Solitude stays. Second ETB fires → exile another creature. Rebound next upkeep → third ETB.
Atraxa ETB — "One of Each Card Type" Means Up to 8 Cards
Atraxa reveals top 10 and you take one card of each TYPE: artifact, battle, creature, enchantment, instant, land, planeswalker, sorcery = 8 types. Realistically you take 4-6 cards per trigger (not every type is represented in top 10). With 3 triggers (Goryo's + Ephemerate + Rebound): you see 30 cards deep and take 12-18 cards total. You're refilling your hand multiple times over. The remaining cards go to the BOTTOM of library in random order — not the graveyard.
Griselbrand — Pay 7 Life Is an Activated Ability, Not a Trigger
"Pay 7 life: Draw seven cards" is an activated ability. You can activate it ANY NUMBER of times as long as you have life to pay. With lifelink: Griselbrand attacks for 7 → gain 7 life → you now have enough life to activate twice (pay 14 total). At 20 starting life with no damage taken: pay 7 (13 life) draw 7, pay 7 (6 life) draw 7 = 14 cards drawn. After attacking and gaining 7: pay 7 more draw 7. The Griselbrand player's life total IS their hand size.

Chapter 03

Gameplan

"Get a legendary creature into the graveyard. Cast Goryo's Vengeance. Cast Ephemerate. Win the game."

The T2 Kill Line

Turn 1
Psychic Frog
{U}{B}: Frog enters. Opponent's end step: discard Atraxa to Frog → Frog gets +1/+1 counter, Atraxa goes to GY. You now have the reanimation target ready.
Turn 2
Goryo's Vengeance
{1}{B} instant: Atraxa enters from GY with haste. ETB #1: reveal top 10, take 4-6 cards. Now hold priority — cast Ephemerate ({W}). Atraxa blinks. ETB #2: reveal top 10, take 4-6 more cards. Atraxa stays permanently.
Turn 3
Rebound + Attack
Upkeep: Ephemerate rebounds from exile for free. Atraxa blinks AGAIN. ETB #3: reveal top 10, take 4-6 MORE cards. Attack with 7/7 flying vigilance deathtouch lifelink. You've drawn 12-18 extra cards and have an unkillable threat. Game over.

Alternative Lines

The Griselbrand Line
Draw Your Entire Deck
Goryo's → Griselbrand enters with haste. Attack for 7 lifelink (back to 20). Pay 7 life → draw 7. Pay 7 → draw 7. You've drawn 14 cards and have a 7/7 flying lifelink. Find Ephemerate in those 14 cards → blink Griselbrand → stays permanently. Alternatively: just hold all the answers you drew and win next turn.
The Fair Plan
Frog + Solitude + Ephemerate
When combo is disrupted: Psychic Frog grows every turn via discard, draws cards on hit. Solitude evoke + Ephemerate = exile 3 creatures for free. Quantum Riddler draws cards on ETB. You're an Esper control deck with a combo finish that can pivot entirely to fair Magic and still win through card advantage.

Mulligan Guide

Keep: Goryo's + Ephemerate + discard outlet (Frog or Atraxa in hand). Frog + legendary target + 2 lands. Solitude + Ephemerate + interaction + lands. Mulligan: Atraxa/Griselbrand in hand with NO way to discard them and NO Goryo's. Multiple legendary targets with no Goryo's or Frog. One-land hands without Frog.

Chapter 05 — Lines & Tactics

Lines &
Tactics

Goryo's at Opponent's End Step — Maximum Haste Value
Goryo's is an INSTANT. Cast it at their end step → creature enters with haste → "exile at beginning of the NEXT end step" = YOUR end step (a full turn away). You untap with the creature, attack with haste, and THEN it would exile at your end step. This gives you a full attack step. Without Ephemerate: you still get one full turn of attacks. With Griselbrand: attack for 7 lifelink + pay 7 draw 7 + pay 7 draw 7 before the exile triggers. You don't always need Ephemerate to get value.
Quantum Riddler + Ephemerate — Draw Engine Without Combo
Quantum Riddler: {3}{U}{U} 4/6 Sphinx. ETB: draw a card. Warp {1}{U} (enters as 1/1). Ephemerate on Quantum Riddler = blink → second ETB → draw again. Rebound → third ETB → draw again. 3 cards drawn from Ephemerate on a Riddler. When you don't have a legendary target in GY, Ephemerate + Riddler is your card advantage engine. Warp Riddler for {1}{U} → 1/1 that draws a card → Ephemerate for 2 more draws.
Ulamog, the Defiler — Cast Trigger vs ETB
Ulamog's "exile top half of opponent's library" is a CAST trigger, not ETB. Goryo's Vengeance puts Ulamog onto the battlefield — it was never CAST. So the half-library exile does NOT happen off Goryo's. What you DO get: a 7/7 with ward (sacrifice 2 permanents), +1/+1 counters equal to highest MV in exile, and annihilator X. If opponent has exiled cards with high MV (from Solitude, Prismatic Ending, etc.), Ulamog enters huge. The annihilator is real — they sacrifice X permanents per attack.
Atraxa Reveals Go to BOTTOM of Library, Not Graveyard
Atraxa: "Put the rest on the bottom of your library in a random order." Cards you don't take go to the BOTTOM — not the graveyard. This means: Atraxa does NOT fuel your GY (unlike looting effects). It also means cards you pass on are effectively gone for the rest of the game (bottom of a 40+ card library). Choose carefully — you only get one shot per reveal.
Solitude's Exile — Opponent GAINS Life Equal to Creature's Power
"That creature's controller gains life equal to its power." When you exile their 5/5, they gain 5 life. This is NOT free removal — there's a real cost. Against aggro decks at low life: be careful exiling their big threats because the life gain might give them extra turns. Against combo: the life gain is irrelevant. Calculate whether the life gain matters before deploying Solitude on curve.
Psychic Frog Discard Timing — End Step for Maximum Safety
Discard Atraxa to Frog during opponent's end step. They can respond with Surgical Extraction targeting Atraxa in GY — but they only have the end step to do it. Your untap is next, then your main phase: cast Goryo's. If they don't have instant-speed GY hate ready at that exact moment, you're safe. Never discard on your own turn if you can wait — give them the minimum window to respond.

How Fast Can This Deck Kill?

Frog T1 → Goryo's T2

T3: Ephemerate rebounds → ETB #3. Attack again. Game over.

Thoughtseize T1 → Setup T2 → Goryo's T3
T1: Thoughtseize — strip their interaction, gain information.
T2: Psychic Frog OR Roiling Dragonstorm. If Frog: discard Atraxa at their end step. If Dragonstorm: draw 2, discard Atraxa.
T3: Goryo's + Ephemerate. Atraxa enters permanently with 3 ETBs over 2 turns. You know their hand is safe (Thoughtseize T1). This is the consistent line — it happens in ~40% of games.

Decision Trees

Atraxa vs Griselbrand — When to Target Which
Default: always Atraxa. 4 keywords (flying, vigilance, deathtouch, lifelink) + draws 4-8 cards per ETB without costing life. Choose Griselbrand ONLY when: (1) You need to dig for a SPECIFIC answer immediately (draw 14 = find anything). (2) You're at 20+ life and can afford the life cost. (3) You don't have Ephemerate — Griselbrand's value is front-loaded (draw 14 on entry), while Atraxa's value scales with blinks. Without Ephemerate, one Griselbrand activation > one Atraxa reveal.
Goryo's Main Phase vs End Step — When Each Is Correct
Their end step (default): Creature enters with haste, exile at YOUR end step = full turn of attacks. Best when: you don't have Ephemerate (maximize the one turn of value), or you want to attack + draw with Griselbrand before exile. Your main phase (when you have Ephemerate): Goryo's → ETB fires → immediately Ephemerate → creature stays permanently. Best when: you have Ephemerate backup and want the creature to stay. Rule of thumb: Ephemerate in hand = main phase. No Ephemerate = their end step.
When to Sacrifice Frog to Flare of Denial
Flare of Denial costs {1}{U}{U} OR sacrifice a nontoken blue creature. Psychic Frog is blue. Quantum Riddler is blue. Sacrifice Frog when: your Goryo's is on the stack and they're trying to counter it — the combo resolving is worth more than any creature. Don't sacrifice when: you're in fair mode and Frog is your only clock. The decision is always: "Is resolving this spell worth more than having this creature?" Against combo: yes. Against midrange: usually no.
Dress Down Timing — Mid-Combat Flash for Maximum Blowout
Dress Down has flash. Cast it DURING COMBAT after they've committed to attacks and pumps. Prowess creatures lose prowess mid-resolution — their 5/6 Swiftspear becomes 1/2. Murktide loses flying (no flying ability). Solitude loses lifelink. ETB creatures entering this turn get no triggers. Dress Down sacrifices at end step — your creatures regain abilities after combat. The draw on entry means this is always card-neutral at worst.

Shadow mirrors your plan at half-scale. They run Thoughtseize, Fatal Push, and a fast clock — but their threats (4/4 Shadow, Ledger Shredder) are dwarfed by Atraxa's 7/7 flying deathtouch lifelink. Solitude exiles Death's Shadow cleanly — the "controller gains life equal to power" clause benefits you when exiling their 8/8. Fatal Push kills everything they run. Psychic Frog blocks their early pressure profitably and grows. Their Thoughtseize hurts, but you bring more disruption per game and your endgame is more powerful.

OutIn
-1 Ulamog+1 Celestial Purge
-1 Griselbrand+1 Fatal Push
-2 Spell Pierce+2 Thoughtseize

Ulamog and Griselbrand are too slow/life-intensive. Celestial Purge exiles Death's Shadow (black) and Kroxa (red). More Push and Thoughtseize to match their disruption. You want the fastest, most interactive version of your deck here.

% Point Edges

+3 pts: Solitude exiling Death's Shadow when it's 6+/6+. They lose a 6/6+ body AND you gain 6+ life — a 12+ point swing in one mana. This reverses any pressure they've applied and directly stabilizes the board.

+2 pts: Psychic Frog growing faster than their Shadow. Discard excess lands to Frog. Each discard grows Frog by 1/1. A 4/5 Frog with flying outclasses a 4/4 Shadow in combat. They want to go under you — don't let the race happen.

Mono-Green TronComboFavorableYou combo T2-3 before they assemble Tron T3

Mono-Green Tron has no meaningful interaction with your combo in game 1. No counters, no GY hate — just Relic of Progenitus (exiles one card per activation, sacs to exile all GYs). Monitor Relic closely: if they're ticking it every turn, discard Atraxa and cast Goryo's in the same main phase window before Relic gets another activation. Their clock (Karn Liberated, Ulamog) is T3 at earliest. You reliably win T2-3 before they stabilize. Post-board: they bring Grafdigger's Cage (prevents Goryo's from resolving). Wear // Tear answers it immediately — make destroying Cage your first priority before setting up the combo.

OutIn
-4 Fatal Push+2 Wear // Tear
-2 Solitude+2 Thoughtseize

Fatal Push and Solitude are dead — no creatures until T3+. Wear // Tear destroys Grafdigger's Cage and Relic of Progenitus. Thoughtseize takes Karn (which can exile a permanent immediately) or an assembled Tron piece.

% Point Edges

+4 pts: T1 Thoughtseize taking a Tron piece. Without all three Tron lands they're a midrange deck with overcosted spells. Taking any one delays their big spells by 2+ turns. Your T3 Goryo's is fast; their T5-6 catch-up is not.

+2 pts: Dump Atraxa + Goryo's in one main phase window when Relic is active. Relic exiles from GY on its tap trigger. Discard Atraxa and immediately cast Goryo's before Relic can tap again — one-turn window collapses its effectiveness entirely.

Domain ZooControlUnfavorableManage their clock with Solitude; Wear // Tear Leyline before combo turn

Domain Zoo is your second-hardest matchup. They present a T2 clock (Territorial Kavu, Scion of Draco, Nacatl) backed by Leyline of the Void G0 in some lists — if Leyline resolves on T0, your combo is offline until Wear // Tear arrives. Their threats grow fast (Kavu with domain = 5/5+), and Stubborn Denial becomes a hard counter once they have a 4/4+ creature. Your gameplan: Solitude evoke + Ephemerate buys a turn against their creature-heavy draws. Race to combo before they close. Psychic Frog blocks Nacatl profitably early. Main issue: their T1-2 curve is faster than your combo assembles, and their SB disruption (Leyline, Endurance) attacks both your GY and your rebound plan.

OutIn
-1 Griselbrand+2 Wear // Tear
-1 Ulamog+2 Nihil Spellbomb
-2 Spell Pierce+1 Celestial Purge
-2 Thoughtseize+1 Fatal Push

Griselbrand costs 7 life — impossible against a deck dealing 2-3 damage per turn per creature. Spell Pierce doesn't stop their threats. Wear // Tear is your Leyline answer. Nihil Spellbomb preemptively stops recursion angles. Celestial Purge exiles Scion of Draco (multicolored, red+white permanent).

% Point Edges

+4 pts: Solitude evoke + Ephemerate on their Territorial Kavu. Kavu grows to 5/5+ with full domain. Exiling it for free (Solitude evoke) + Ephemerate rebound means you exile 2 creatures and gain 10+ life. This swing often decides the game on the spot.

+3 pts: Wear // Tear on Leyline before your combo turn. If you see Leyline G0, find Wear // Tear on T1-2 before you discard Atraxa. A Leyline that persists until T3 means your entire combo window evaporates. Priority 1 post-board: destroy Leyline before setting up.

Free Interaction

GY Hate

Hand Disruption
Thoughtseize ×2 (extra copies)
Additional copies beyond the 3 main. Board in against combo, control, and any deck where information + disruption wins games. 4 total Thoughtseize post-board means you see their hand nearly every game. Against Jeskai Blink: take their Counterspell. Against Storm: take Past in Flames. Against mirror: take their Goryo's.
Enchantment/Artifact Hate
Wear // Tear ×2
Wear {1}{R}: Destroy artifact. Tear {W}: Destroy enchantment. Fuse: both. Primary target: Leyline of the Void, Rest in Peace (both shut off your entire combo). Also: Chalice of the Void at 2 (blocks Goryo's), Ensnaring Bridge, Blood Moon. Fuse mode destroys BOTH an artifact and enchantment for {1}{R}{W}. Critical SB card.
Precision Removal
Celestial Purge ×1
{1}{W} Instant: Exile target black or red permanent. Kills Orcish Bowmasters, Fury, any red/black threat — and EXILES them (no GY recursion). Against Dimir decks: exiles their Frog, Bowmasters, or planeswalkers. Clean answer that goes through indestructible.
Free Counter
Flare of Denial ×1
{1}{U}{U} or sacrifice a nontoken blue creature: Counter target spell. Sacrifice Psychic Frog or Quantum Riddler to counter for free. Against combo: protect your Goryo's by sacrificing a creature. The free cost is critical — you often tap out for Goryo's and need a counter with 0 mana available.
Ability Shutdown
Dress Down ×1
{1}{U} Enchantment. Flash. ETB: draw a card. "Creatures lose all abilities." Sacrifice at end step. Flash in mid-combat: prowess triggers vanish, Murktide counters don't matter for power, Solitude can't exile. The draw on entry means it's never card disadvantage. Against Affinity: Frogmite dies (0/0 without affinity).
Blue Mirror Counter
Mystical Dispute ×1
{2}{U} or {U} targeting a blue spell. Counter unless they pay {3}. Against Jeskai Blink, Dimir Control, mirror: a 1-mana hard counter for their Counterspell, Ephemerate, or Goryo's. The {U} cost when targeting blue makes it the most efficient counter in blue mirrors.

Chapter 09

Heuristics

  1. 01
    Ephemerate is the most important card in the deck — not Goryo's. Goryo's without Ephemerate gives you one turn of value. Ephemerate without Goryo's still combos with Solitude (free 3x removal) and Quantum Riddler (3 cards). Never use Ephemerate frivolously.
  2. 02
    Discard Atraxa at their end step, never your own turn. Psychic Frog's discard is instant speed. Dumping Atraxa at their end step minimizes the window for Surgical Extraction. They get one moment of priority before your untap → Goryo's.
  3. 03
    Goryo's at their end step is often better than your main phase. Creature enters with haste. "Exile at beginning of next end step" = your end step. Full attack step. With Griselbrand: attack for 7 lifelink → draw 14 → find Ephemerate in those cards. Without Ephemerate: you still got a full turn of attacks + ETBs.
  4. 04
    Solitude evoke + Ephemerate is your plan against aggro, not the combo. Against Boros Energy or Prowess: evoke Solitude (free), Ephemerate in response → exile 2 creatures immediately, third on rebound. You just removed their board for 1 card from hand. The combo (Goryo's) is backup in these matchups.
  5. 05
    Atraxa over Griselbrand in 90% of situations. Atraxa draws 4-8 cards per ETB without costing life, has 4 keywords, and survives combat against anything. Griselbrand requires 7 life per draw — against aggro you can't afford it. Griselbrand is better ONLY when you need to dig for a specific answer immediately (draw 14 = find anything).
  6. 06
    Goryo's targets LEGENDARY creatures only — know your legal targets. Legal: Atraxa, Griselbrand, Ulamog. Illegal: Psychic Frog, Solitude, Quantum Riddler, any non-legendary creature. Don't try to Goryo's a Solitude from GY — it won't work.
  7. 07
    Ulamog's cast trigger does NOT fire off Goryo's. "When you cast Ulamog, exile top half of opponent's library" — this triggers on CAST, not ETB. Goryo's puts Ulamog directly onto the battlefield without casting. You get the body + ward + annihilator, but NOT the library exile. Ulamog is your least common Goryo's target — use it when you need annihilator or ward protection.
  8. 08
    Ephemerate rebound only works from HAND. If you cast Ephemerate from graveyard (Snapcaster) or any zone other than hand, it does NOT rebound. Always prioritize casting Ephemerate from hand. The rebound = a free third blink, which is often the difference between winning and losing.
  9. 09
    Against GY hate: transform into Esper Control. Post-board when they have Leyline of the Void or Rest in Peace: cut Goryo's + fatties, bring in more interaction. Frog + Solitude + Ephemerate + Riddler = a real control deck. They over-SB'd for your combo and you aren't even playing it.
  10. 10
    Thoughtseize T1 tells you if the combo is safe. See their hand. Take Counterspell, Surgical, or their best threat. If they have no interaction → T2 Goryo's is safe. If they have Surgical → hold Atraxa until you can discard + Goryo's in the same turn window.

Tuning

Griselbrand Count: 1 vs 2
Life as a Resource
Most lists run 1 Griselbrand. It's the haymaker when you need to draw your entire deck, but costs 7 life per activation. Against aggro-heavy metas: cut to 0. Against combo/control metas: consider 2 (more targets for Goryo's). Griselbrand is your "go nuclear" option — one copy is usually enough because Atraxa is better in most situations.
Emperor of Bones
67% of Lists Run It
Emperor provides repeatable reanimation: exile a creature from GY → adapt → reanimate with haste (sacrifice at EOT). Not a Goryo's target (non-legendary). It's a 2-drop that gives you a second axis of reanimation. Run when you expect long games where multiple reanimation effects matter. Cut when you need more interaction or combo speed.
Quantum Riddler
58% of Lists Run It
Riddler at Warp {1}{U} = 1/1 that draws a card on ETB. Ephemerate on Riddler = 3 cards. Full cast {3}{U}{U} = 4/6 flying that draws a card + doubles future draws when hand is empty. Run when you want more fair game backup. Cut when the meta rewards pure combo speed.
Ulamog vs More Atraxa
Annihilator vs Card Advantage
Ulamog's cast trigger doesn't fire off Goryo's — so you lose the half-library exile. What you get: ward (sacrifice 2), counters from exile, annihilator. Run when: you face decks with indestructible/hexproof threats (annihilator forces sacrifice). Cut when: you need consistent ETB value (Atraxa is better).

Prep Sheet

20-Minute Crash Course

If you're sitting down in 20 minutes, run through this in order:

  • Mulligan rule: Keep any hand with Psychic Frog + a black source. Don't keep hands with only big creatures.
  • Discard rule: Always discard Atraxa at THEIR end step, never your own. Minimize the Surgical Extraction window.
  • Goryo's rule: With Ephemerate in hand: cast Goryo's in your main phase. Without it: cast at their end step.
  • Target rule: Goryo's targets LEGENDARY only. Atraxa, Griselbrand, Ulamog. Never Solitude, never Frog.
  • GY hate rule: When they have Rest in Peace: become Esper Control immediately. Cut Goryo's, keep the fair plan.
  • Sideboard rule: Wear // Tear is your primary Leyline answer. Find it before setting up the combo.

Matchday Routine

  • Register with Wear // Tear confirmed in SB. Leyline of the Void is everywhere.
  • Know which mode you're in before T1: Combo (Goryo's) or Control (Frog + Solitude). Don't split.
  • Between rounds: did you discard Atraxa at their end step? Always their end step.
  • When they have GY hate, become Esper Control immediately. Don't fight through hate, go around it.
  • Protect the combo. Thoughtseize T1 tells you if T2 is safe. Use the information.

The Combo: Get legendary creature in GY (Frog discard at their end step) → Goryo's Vengeance ({1}{B} instant) → Ephemerate ({W}) before end step → creature stays permanently → Rebound next upkeep = 3rd ETB. Total cost: 3 mana.

Primary target: Atraxa (reveal top 10, take one of each type, 7/7 flying vigilance deathtouch lifelink). Secondary: Griselbrand (7/7 flying lifelink, pay 7 draw 7). Backup plan: Solitude evoke + Ephemerate = exile 3 creatures for free. Psychic Frog = growing clock + card advantage.

Critical rules: Goryo's targets LEGENDARY only. Ephemerate rebound only from HAND. Ulamog cast trigger doesn't fire off Goryo's. Atraxa reveals go to bottom, not GY. Solitude exile = opponent gains life.
