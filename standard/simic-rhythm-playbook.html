Chapter 01

DECK
IDENTITY

"Every card in this deck is either a threat or ramp. There is no interaction, no counters, no removal — just an unstoppable sequence of power cards."

Simic Rhythm is the pure expression of the Nature's Rhythm ramp shell. Where Bant Rhythm adds white for Seam Rip and tempo tools, Simic doubles down on the power plan: Ouroboroid at four copies instead of two, Quantum Riddler at four copies instead of three, and top-end threats like Marang River Regent and Abhorrent Oculus that Bant doesn't have room for.

The deck's identity is acceleration into unanswerable threats. Llanowar Elves and Badgermole Cub let you cast Nature's Rhythm on turn two. Nature's Rhythm ramps another land and makes a spider. Gene Pollinator doubles the spiders. By turn four you have a board that requires four or five removal spells to answer, and you're already casting Ouroboroid for your second set of 4/4s.

The key distinction from Bant: you have no interaction pre-board. Opponents who know this can play more freely — but your threats are larger and your consistency is higher. Wistfulness filters draws while filling the graveyard. Marang River Regent generates 4/4 Dragon tokens whenever a non-token creature you control dies. Abhorrent Oculus is an 8/8 for five mana that fills the graveyard and has Ward 2. The power ceiling is simply higher than any other midrange deck in Standard.

Simic vs Bant — What You Gain and Lose

Simic Gains
Raw Power + Consistency
4 Ouroboroid (vs 2). 4 Quantum Riddler (vs 3). Marang River Regent as a death-replacement engine. Abhorrent Oculus as an 8/8 Ward 2 that replaces itself. Better mana base (no tri-color). More consistent opening hands — every card either ramps or threatens.
Simic Loses
Tempo and Interaction
Seam Rip (main deck counterspell). Bounce Off (tempo). Beza, the Bounding Spring (life gain). Meltstrider's Resolve (protection). You have zero main deck interaction. If they land a key permanent on turn 3, you cannot answer it — you can only race past it.

When to Play Simic vs Bant

Play Simic When
Field Is Aggro-Heavy
Against aggro, your large bodies (Ouroboroid 4/4, Abhorrent Oculus 8/8) wall everything. Without needing interaction, your streamlined plan operates at full speed. Marang River Regent replaces your creatures when they die, giving you inevitability vs removal-based aggro.
Play Simic When
You Expect Midrange Mirrors
Simic out-threatens every other midrange deck. Ouroboroid × 4 means 8 bodies from one card type. Quantum Riddler × 4 generates card advantage faster. If you're expecting Dimir Midrange and Bant Rhythm mirrors, Simic's sheer power level wins those.
Play Bant When
Control Is Prevalent
Against Jeskai Control, Seam Rip protecting your key threats is the difference between winning and losing. If sweepers are common, Bant's interaction buys the turns you need. Simic cannot interact with a Sunfall or Day of Judgment.

Key Card Notes

Why 4 Ouroboroid
The Primary Win Condition
Ouroboroid creates two 4/4 Serpent tokens when it enters or cycles. At four copies you reliably see it by turn 4. Unlike Bant's two copies, you can chain multiple Ouroboroids — two in one turn from the ramp acceleration means 4 immediate 4/4s for 8 mana. The cycle mode (discard + draw) keeps the hand live when you draw multiples.
The Death Replacement
Marang River Regent
Whenever a non-token creature you control dies, Marang River Regent creates a 4/4 Dragon token with flying. This makes your entire threat base resistant to single removal — every Llanowar Elves that dies becomes a 4/4 flier. Against removal-heavy decks, Regent turns their entire removal suite into advantage for you.
The Raw Finisher
Abhorrent Oculus
8/8 for five mana. Ward 2 (must pay 2 or the targeting spell fails). When it enters, you mill 4 cards. The milled cards feed your graveyard. Most importantly: it's an 8/8 that survives most targeted removal (Ward 2 + 8 toughness). Against decks without exile effects, Oculus resolving is usually game over.
The Filter Spell
Wistfulness
The Simic elemental incarnation: one mode destroys an artifact or enchantment (Naturalize), the other filters draws (look at top 3, take 1, discard the rest). In Simic Rhythm, the filter mode smooths your curve — discard excess lands or redundant threats. Note: the discard feeds Marang River Regent's death trigger if you discard creatures.

GAME
PLAN

"T1 dork. T2 Nature's Rhythm. T3 Gene Pollinator. T4 Ouroboroid into 4/4s. T5 Craterhoof."
Turns 1–3: Ramp
Establish the Engine
T1: Llanowar Elves or Badgermole Cub. T2: Nature's Rhythm (with dork mana) — ramp a basic, make a spider. T3: Gene Pollinator (all subsequent tokens are doubled). This is the non-negotiable opening. Any hand deviating from this sequence needs to have a very good reason.
Turns 4–5: Deploy
Threats Into Empty Air
T4: Ouroboroid for two 4/4s (+ Pollinator doubles spiders from Rhythm if Rhythm was T3 instead). Alternatively: Spider Manifestation EOT for 4 spiders with Pollinator, then Quantum Riddler. T5: Craterhoof with 6+ creatures for 30+ trample damage, or second Ouroboroid for 4 more 4/4s, or Abhorrent Oculus for the 8/8 follow-up.
On the Play
Speed Is Your Weapon
Any hand with T1 dork + T2 Nature's Rhythm is a keep. You'll be two turns ahead on board development. On the play you should have Craterhoof lethal available on turn 5 consistently. Mulligan aggressively for the T1 accelerant.
On the Draw
The Extra Card Covers
On the draw you can keep slightly slower hands — the extra card offsets being a turn behind. A hand with Pollinator + Ouroboroid + 3 lands is keepable on the draw even without a T1 dork. You'll deploy everything one turn later and that's fine. Don't over-mulligan trying to find the perfect draw-7.

Mulligan Framework

Snap keeps: T1 dork + Nature's Rhythm + any threat + 2-3 lands. T1 dork + Pollinator + Ouroboroid + 3 lands.

Keep on 7: Two lands + dork + Rhythm. Three lands + Pollinator + Ouroboroid. Four lands + two threats (Riddler + Ouroboroid).

Mulligan: 0-1 lands. 5+ lands. No green mana source. All top-end and no acceleration (Ouroboroid × 2 + Abhorrent Oculus + Marang Regent + 3 lands = must mulligan).

Chapter 05

LINES &
TACTICS

"Know what Pollinator does and doesn't trigger. This is the most misunderstood card in the deck."
Line 01 — Gene Pollinator Triggers Only from Non-Token Creature ETBs
Gene Pollinator creates an additional token whenever a non-token creature you control "creates one or more tokens." This means: Nature's Rhythm (a sorcery spell, not a creature) does NOT trigger Pollinator. Spider Manifestation (an instant, not a creature) does NOT trigger Pollinator. Only creature ETBs that create tokens trigger it. In this deck that means: if you have a creature that creates tokens on ETB, Pollinator doubles it. Ouroboroid creates tokens directly — it does NOT trigger Pollinator. This is a critical distinction from Bant Rhythm's conventional wisdom.
Line 02 — Spider Manifestation at Instant Speed, Their End Step
Spider Manifestation is an instant. Always cast it at the end of their turn (their end step), not during your main phase. Four 1/2 spiders with reach entering during your main phase means you attack with them next turn but they blocked nothing this turn. Entering during their EOT means they block on their next attack (which is already happening) AND you attack with them on your very next turn. The timing window is: hold mana open, let them pass, cast at EOT, untap and attack.
Line 03 — Ouroboroid Cycle Mode vs Cast Mode
Ouroboroid has two modes: cast it as a 4-mana creature that creates two 4/4 Serpent tokens on ETB, OR cycle it (discard + draw for {2}{G}). The cycle mode is correct when: you have 3+ Ouroboroids in hand, you're flooded and need a spell, or you need to fill the GY for Quantum Riddler's draw trigger. The cast mode is correct when: you need board presence now, you have Craterhoof ready to follow up, or you need to absorb a removal spell. Never cycle your last Ouroboroid — the cast mode is your primary win condition.
Line 04 — Marang River Regent Requires Non-TOKEN Creatures to Die
Marang River Regent triggers when a "non-token creature you control" dies. This means: Llanowar Elves dying → 4/4 Dragon. Badgermole Cub dying → 4/4 Dragon. Gene Pollinator dying → 4/4 Dragon. Quantum Riddler dying → 4/4 Dragon. Spider tokens dying? NO. Ouroboroid Serpent tokens dying? NO. Only your actual creatures (non-tokens) trigger Regent. This means Regent is best in matchups where they remove your small creatures — every killed Elf or Cub becomes a 4/4 flier.

ROLE
ASSIGNMENT

"You are always the bigger midrange deck. Their plan is good. Yours is better."
Primary Role
Ramp Into Power
Get dork → Rhythm → Pollinator → Ouroboroid down in order. You're not trying to interact. You're not trying to be clever. You're putting the biggest threats on the table at the fastest possible speed and daring them to have an answer for each one sequentially.

vs All fair decks

Secondary Role
Grind with Regent
When they have removal: use it. Every non-token creature they kill triggers Marang River Regent for a 4/4 Dragon. In matchups with 6+ removal spells (Dimir Midrange, Jeskai Control), you want them to spend every removal spell — each one generates a 4/4 flier. Play Regent early so death-triggers start immediately.

vs Removal-heavy decks

Tertiary Role
Overwhelm with Hoof
Save Craterhoof for when you have 6+ creatures. Count power: 6 creatures × (power+6) + trample = usually 40+ damage in one attack. Don't cast Hoof until the math is lethal — it's a sorcery and they untap after a non-lethal Hoof. With Regent generating fliers and Ouroboroid making 4/4s, reaching lethal Hoof math is fast.

vs Any board state with 6+ creatures

Chapter 09

HEURISTICS

"Know Pollinator's trigger condition by reflex. Most Simic Rhythm mistakes come from misunderstanding what it does."
  1. 01Gene Pollinator does NOT trigger from Nature's Rhythm or Spider Manifestation. These are spells, not creature ETBs. Pollinator only triggers when a non-token creature you control "creates tokens." In this deck, no main deck creature creates tokens on ETB except via specific effects. Pollinator's primary value is doubling your spider tokens from Manifestation — but only if a creature is generating them, not the spell itself.
  2. 02Nature's Rhythm ramps a BASIC land, entering TAPPED. You cannot use the ramped land the turn you cast Rhythm. Your five-mana plays require a dork on turn 1. Without the dork: T1 land, T2 land + Rhythm (3 lands total, tapped), T3 deploy a 3-drop. With dork: T1 dork, T2 land + Rhythm (3 untapped mana), T3 5+ effective mana.
  3. 03Cast Spider Manifestation at the end of their turn, not yours. Four 1/2 reach spiders entering at their EOT block their next attack AND attack on your very next turn. Main-phase casting loses you a full attack step. Instant speed is the power — use it.
  4. 04Marang River Regent triggers on NON-TOKEN creatures dying. Spider tokens dying → no trigger. Ouroboroid Serpent tokens dying → no trigger. Llanowar Elves dying → trigger. Badgermole Cub dying → trigger. Gene Pollinator dying → trigger. Quantum Riddler dying → trigger. The trigger converts your small creature removal into 4/4 Dragons.
  5. 05Ouroboroid cycle mode is not the default. Creating two 4/4 Serpent tokens is the primary mode. Cycle only when you have 3+ Ouroboroids in hand or need to fill GY for Quantum Riddler. Never cycle your last copy when behind on board.
  6. 06Wistfulness modes: Naturalize kills Monument to Endurance immediately. Don't filter with Wistfulness vs Izzet Lessons — destroy Monument the moment you see it. Their entire long-game plan requires Monument's GY recursion. Without it they're just a draw deck.
  7. 07Abhorrent Oculus has Ward 2, not full protection. Ward 2 means any spell targeting it costs 2 more. Removal that doesn't target (sweepers, Pyroclasm) still affects it. The Ward is effective against single-target removal but a Day of Judgment still kills it. Don't run Oculus into open mana assuming it's fully protected.
  8. 08Craterhoof math: count creatures before casting. With 6 creatures: each gets +6/+6 trample. Four Ouroboroid tokens (4/4 + 6/6 = 10/10) × 4 = 40 trample damage before other creatures. Don't cast Hoof unless the math is lethal — they untap and you have nothing if it doesn't kill.
  9. 09Hushwood Verge enters untapped if you control a Forest OR Plains. You have only Forest (no Plains). Play a basic Forest or land that counts as Forest (Breeding Pool, Botanical Sanctum) before Hushwood Verge for it to enter untapped. Incorrect sequencing wastes a turn of mana.
  10. 10You have zero main deck interaction — embrace it. Don't slow down trying to play around their threats. Your plan resolves faster than theirs if you sequence correctly. Every turn you spend not ramping is a turn they spend developing. Go full speed.

Lock — Never Cut
8 Dorks + 4 Rhythm
4 Llanowar Elves + 4 Badgermole Cub. Full playset of both. The T2 Nature's Rhythm requirement is too important to compromise. Dropping below 8 accelerants makes the deck function like a normal midrange deck at 3-mana-on-turn-3 pace — you lose the speed advantage entirely.
Lock — Never Cut
4 Ouroboroid
This is the key distinction from Bant Rhythm. Four copies means you see it consistently by T4 and can chain multiples. If you cut Ouroboroid to 2 you're playing Bant Rhythm without white, which is just a worse Bant. The full set is the reason to play Simic.
Flex 0-2
Wistfulness
Excellent in control matchups (Naturalize mode on Monument) and for card filtering. In aggro-heavy metas, cut to 0 — you don't need GY hate or filtering, you need speed. In control-heavy metas, run the full 2. In mixed metas, 1 is the compromise.
Flex 2-4
Spider Manifestation
Four copies is the norm for its role as a flash board-builder. Some builds trim to 2 in the maindeck and rely more heavily on Ouroboroid tokens. Cutting Manifestation makes you more vulnerable to aggro (no instant-speed reach blockers) but more powerful in midrange/control matchups.
Flex 1-3
Mockingbird
Copies the highest-power creature on attack — typically Abhorrent Oculus (8/8) or Quantum Riddler. At 3 copies, you see it mid-game. Some builds run 2, preferring a second Marang River Regent or an additional Abhorrent Oculus. The sweet spot depends on how much you need the card advantage engine in your expected matchups.
Consider Adding
Archdruid's Charm
Flexible green modal spell seen in Turin Top64 Bant builds. Can ramp (search land), remove artifacts/enchantments, or put a creature into play directly. Worth testing 1-2 copies main in control-heavy metas where the removal mode (killing their sweeper-enabling enchantments) justifies the inclusion.

Know Cold
Craterhoof Math
6 creatures: each gets +6/+6 trample. 8 creatures: +8/+8. A standard board of 4 Serpents (4/4) + Pollinator (3/3) + Elves (1/1) = 6 creatures → each becomes 10/10+ with trample. Count it out before every potential Hoof turn. Never cast it without lethal math confirmed.
Know Cold
Pollinator Trigger Conditions
Pollinator triggers when a non-token creature you control creates tokens. In Simic Rhythm, Nature's Rhythm and Spider Manifestation are spells not creatures — they do NOT trigger Pollinator. Only actual creature ETBs that create tokens trigger it. Know this before game 1.
Verify
SB Configuration
Check expected field. Izzet Lessons above 10%: Soul-Guide Lantern mandatory. Jeskai Control above 5%: Keen-Eyed Curator and Vivien Reid in. Mono-Green above 10%: Unable to Scream, Get Lost, Reclamation Sage for the combo. Confirm your 15 matches the room.
Round 1 Tell
T1 Land Identification
Breeding Pool or Spirebluff Canal = Izzet variant (Prowess/Lessons/Spellementals) — go fast, spiders at EOT T3. Fabled Passage or Forest = Mono-Green — board in Unable to Scream for game 2. Island or Watery Grave = Dimir — play Regent early, let them spend removal.

20-Minute Crash Course

If you're sitting down in 20 minutes, run through this in order:

  • Mulligan rule: Keep any 7 with Badgermole Cub + Earthbender Ascension OR Llanowar Elves + a 3-drop. Never keep a hand without a T2 play.
  • Badgermole Cub rule: Badgermole Cub puts a +1/+1 counter on itself each time any creature you control attacks. Swing with your full board â€” each Cub attack generates one counter.
  • Earthbender Ascension rule: Earthbender Ascension triggers when you play a land after the first each turn (via Icetill Explorer or similar). Each land drop generates a +1/+1 counter. Double land drops = double counters.
  • Mossborn Hydra rule: Mossborn Hydra can't be countered if you control 7+ lands. At 7 lands it's an uncounterable 7/7 trample. Against counter-heavy opponents, wait until 7 lands to deploy.
  • Spider Manifestation rule: Spider Manifestation (flash instant) creates a 1/4 Spider with reach. Flash it at their EOT to deploy a reach blocker without committing on your turn.
  • Board rule: Meltstrider's Resolve fights their key creature AND puts a +1/+1 counter on your creature. The fight damage is based on your creature's total power â€” pump your creature BEFORE using Resolve.

Testing Framework

Block 01
Badgermole Cub Counter Tracking
Practice tracking Badgermole Cub's counters. Each creature that attacks generates one counter per Cub. With 3 creatures attacking: Cub gets 3 counters per combat. Know the math at each board state.
Block 02
Earthbender Ascension Trigger
Practice the Ascension trigger sequence. Play first land: nothing. Play second land: Ascension triggers. Each subsequent land: another trigger. Know the land count before deploying Ascension.
Block 03
Mossborn Hydra Timing
Practice deploying Mossborn Hydra at exactly 7 lands. At 6 lands: Hydra is counterable. At 7: uncounterable. Count your lands throughout the game and know when the uncounterable window opens.
Block 04
Spider Manifestation Defense
Practice flashing Spider Manifestation at their attack step. A 1/4 reach blocker at instant speed stops their fliers and provides ground defense simultaneously.
