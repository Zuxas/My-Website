Chapter 01

DECK
IDENTITY

"This is not Izzet Spellementals. This is the Incarnation-based Elemental tribal deck. Every creature is an Elemental. Cavern names Elemental. Sunderflock costs zero."

Izzet Elementals is the Incarnation tribal variant of the Elemental archetype, distinct from Izzet Spellementals. Where Spellementals uses cantrips and Hearth Elemental to reduce Sunderflock's cost, Elementals uses the Incarnation cycle — Ashling, Rekindled, Flamebraider, Vibrance, Wistfulness — as a suite of hybrid-mana Elementals that each contribute to reducing Sunderflock's cost by {1} per Elemental you control.

Roaming Throne is the force multiplier. Named Elemental, it doubles every triggered ability of Elementals you control. Ashling's ETB (discard X, draw X) fires twice. Flamebraider's attack trigger (create tokens) fires twice. With Throne active and four Elementals in play, Sunderflock is free AND deals double damage when it enters (with Throne doubling its ETB).

Cavern of Souls names Elemental, making every creature in the deck uncounterable. Deceit (Distress mode: discard, or bounce mode: return small permanent) provides disruption. Winternight Stories generates card advantage over multiple turns. The deck is resilient, recursive, and reaches a critical mass of Elementals that produces a free Sunderflock board wipe on demand.

Izzet Elementals vs Izzet Spellementals

Izzet Elementals (This Deck)
Incarnation Tribal Engine
Uses Ashling, Flamebraider, Vibrance, Wistfulness as the Elemental package. Each is a hybrid-mana Elemental that enters with an effect. Roaming Throne doubles all their triggers. Sunderflock reduced by 4 with four Elementals = free. Cavern of Souls. More resilient vs removal — the Incarnations replace themselves and generate ongoing value.
Izzet Spellementals
Spell-Density Engine
Uses Hearth Elemental + Eddymurk Crab + cantrips. GY-based cost reduction. More explosive burst potential (free 5/5 Crab with 5 GY spells). Less tribal synergy but faster in specific draws. Izzet Elementals is the more consistent and resilient variant; Spellementals is more combo-capable but less stable.

The Four Incarnations

Fire Incarnation
Ashling, Rekindled // Ashling, Rimebound
Hybrid {R/U}{R/U}{R/U} Elemental Spirit. Front face: ETB discard any number of cards then draw that many. Back face (Ashling, Rimebound): when it dies, exile it and return to hand. With Roaming Throne: discard/draw triggers twice = cycle 2x more cards per ETB. Front-face Ashling is the card advantage engine. Use Bounce Off or Deceit to return it to hand for another ETB cycle.
Red Incarnation
Flamebraider
Hybrid {R/U}{R/U} 2/2 Elemental. Whenever it attacks: create a 1/1 red Elemental token. With Roaming Throne: creates two tokens per attack. These tokens ARE Elementals — they count toward reducing Sunderflock's cost. A T2 Flamebraider that attacks twice creates 2-4 Elemental tokens, rapidly assembling the Sunderflock cost reduction threshold.
Blue Incarnation
Vibrance
Hybrid Elemental. ETB or whenever you draw a card: do something relevant. With Roaming Throne: the draw trigger fires twice. In the Elementals deck, Vibrance contributes both as an Elemental (reducing Sunderflock's cost) and as a card advantage engine that scales with your draw engine.
Simic Incarnation
Wistfulness
Hybrid Elemental. Mode 1: Naturalize (destroy artifact or enchantment). Mode 2: Look at top 3 cards, take 1, discard the rest. With Wistfulness in Elementals: the filter mode helps find Roaming Throne or Sunderflock consistently. The Naturalize mode destroys Monument to Endurance vs Izzet Lessons. Play Wistfulness immediately — Abandon Attachments can destroy it.

Key Card Notes

The Doubler
Roaming Throne — Name Elemental
Roaming Throne named Elemental: each Elemental you control's triggered abilities trigger an additional time. Ashling's discard/draw ETB fires twice (cycle 2x cards). Flamebraider's attack trigger fires twice (two tokens instead of one). Vibrance's draw trigger fires twice. Sunderflock's ETB (damage to each creature) fires twice with Throne = double the damage. This is the deck's key force multiplier — protect Roaming Throne at all costs.
The Win Condition
Sunderflock — Costs 0 With 4 Elementals
Sunderflock costs {X}{U} where X reduces by 1 per Elemental you control. With 4 Elementals in play: X=0, Sunderflock costs {U}. ETB: deals X damage to each creature (where X = number of Elementals you control). With Throne active: deals 2X damage to each creature. Four Elementals + Throne = 8 damage to each creature — kills almost everything in Standard. Cast Sunderflock at your opponent's end step, then untap and attack.
The Disruption
Deceit — Two Modes
Deceit has two modes (like Thoughtseize + bounce): Distress mode (sorcery speed): target player reveals hand, you choose a nonland card, they discard it. Bounce mode: return target nonland permanent with MV ≤ 2 to its owner's hand. Distress strips their key piece before they cast it (Monument to Endurance, Roaming Throne, Sapling Nursery). Bounce mode removes their T1-T2 dorks or resets your own Ashling for another ETB.
The Card Engine
Winternight Stories
Three-chapter saga: Chapter 1 draws a card. Chapter 2 mills 3 then returns up to 1 from your GY to hand. Chapter 3 each opponent discards a card. Three turns of value — card draw, recursion, and hand disruption. Running 3 copies ensures you see it consistently. Chapter 2 can return Ashling from GY to hand, enabling another ETB cycle without casting it from hand. Critical in grindy matchups.
Chapter 03

GAME
PLAN

"T1-T3: Build Elementals. T3: Roaming Throne. T4: Sunderflock for free, deal 8 damage to each creature. T5: win."
Standard Line
Elementals → Throne → Sunderflock
T1: Any Elemental (Ashling for {R/U}, Flamebraider for {R/U}{R/U}, etc.). T2: Second Elemental. T3: Roaming Throne (named Elemental). T4: Third Elemental (Throne doubles its ETB) + Sunderflock for free (3 Elementals = free at {0}{U}). Sunderflock ETB deals 3 damage to each creature, Throne doubles it = 6 damage to each. Kills most Standard boards instantly.
Ideal Line
Four Elementals + Throne = 8 Damage Each
With 4 Elementals and Roaming Throne: Sunderflock is free, deals 8 damage to each creature (4 Elementals × 2 from Throne). Every creature in Standard with 8 or less toughness dies. This board wipe is better than Day of Judgment because it also damages large creatures and happens at sorcery speed as part of your own threat deployment. The mana investment: T1+T2+T3 building Elementals, T3 Throne, T4 Sunderflock free.
Mulligan
Need Elementals + Throne or Sunderflock
Snap keep: 2+ Elementals + Roaming Throne + 3 lands. Keep on 7: 2 Elementals + Sunderflock + lands. Mulligan: all spells and no Elementals, or all Elementals and no Sunderflock/Throne, or 5+ lands. You need the critical mass to generate the free Sunderflock. A hand of Cavern + 4 Elementals + Throne is nearly perfect.
Cavern Naming
Always Name Elemental
Cavern of Souls named Elemental makes ALL your creatures uncounterable — Ashling, Flamebraider, Vibrance, Wistfulness, Beza, and crucially Sunderflock (which is an Elemental). This means your entire game plan — every creature AND your board wipe — cannot be countered. Against Izzet Lessons, Azorius Flash, and Jeskai Control this is the single most important decision every game.

CORE
ENGINES

"Four Elementals. One Roaming Throne. One free Sunderflock. That's the clock."
Engine 1 — Critical
Elemental Count → Sunderflock Cost
Sunderflock costs {1} less for each Elemental you control. 1 Elemental: {2}{U}. 2 Elementals: {1}{U}. 3 Elementals: {U}. 4 Elementals: free! All 20 creature slots are Elementals. This is the deck's combo engine — assembling Elementals is the game plan. Count your Elementals before every main phase.
Engine 2 — Multiplier
Roaming Throne Doubles
Throne named Elemental: each Elemental ETB trigger fires twice. Ashling ETB: discard/draw twice. Flamebraider attack: two tokens. Vibrance trigger: twice. Sunderflock ETB: deals damage TWICE (4 Elementals × 2 triggers = 8 total damage per trigger instance). The Throne-Sunderflock interaction is the deck's game-ending board wipe.
Engine 3 — Recovery
Ashling Discard/Draw Loop
Ashling's ETB: discard any number, draw that many. Returns to hand when the back face dies. Bounce Off / Deceit bounce mode: return Ashling to hand for another ETB cycle. Winternight Stories Chapter 2: return Ashling from GY. The result: you can cycle Ashling's ETB multiple times per game, finding Throne and Sunderflock through sheer card selection.
Chapter 05

LINES &
TACTICS

"Count Elementals before every main phase. The Sunderflock cost changes every turn."
Line 01 — Sunderflock is an Elemental — Cavern Names Elemental Makes It Uncounterable
Sunderflock is an Elemental creature spell. When cast off Cavern of Souls mana (named Elemental), it cannot be countered. This is critical: their Spell Snare ({U}: counter target spell with MV 2), No More Lies, and It'll Quench Ya all fail against a Cavern-cast Sunderflock. With 4 Elementals in play, Sunderflock costs {U} — cast it off Cavern for free and uncounterable. Their entire permission suite does nothing against your board wipe.
Line 02 — Roaming Throne Doubles the Sunderflock ETB — Damage Hits TWICE
When Sunderflock enters, its ETB triggers: "deals X damage to each creature and planeswalker." With Roaming Throne (named Elemental), this trigger fires an additional time — so Sunderflock deals X damage TWICE to each creature. With 4 Elementals: X=4, fires twice = 8 total damage to each creature. Almost every relevant creature in Standard has ≤8 toughness. This is not "deal X damage then deal X damage again" — it's two separate instances of the trigger. Indestructible creatures survive (Sarkhan's Dragons under indestructible); exile effects don't trigger from the damage alone.
Line 03 — Ashling, Rimebound (Back Face) Returns to Hand on Death — This Is Not a May
Ashling, Rimebound (the back face after Ashling, Rekindled flips) says "when this creature dies, exile it, then return it to its owner's hand." This is a mandatory trigger — not "may." If the Rimebound face is in play and dies, you MUST return it to hand. This means Ashling is nearly unkillable as a value engine — every time it dies, it comes back. Don't try to leave Ashling back as a blocker when you need it to die and cycle back; it will return to hand after blocking, not after the combat damage step.
Line 04 — Deceit Distress Mode Is Sorcery Speed
Deceit's Distress mode (discard their card) is a sorcery — you cannot cast it in response to their spell. Cast Deceit on T2 to strip their key piece before they can cast it. The best targets to strip: Monument to Endurance (their graveyard engine), Roaming Throne (in the mirror), Sapling Nursery (Mono-Green), or No More Lies (control). The bounce mode (return MV ≤ 2 permanent to hand) IS instant speed — use it reactively. Deceit's bounce mode resets your Ashling for {1}{U} when you want another ETB cycle.

HEURISTICS

"Count your Elementals before every main phase. The Sunderflock cost changes every turn."
  1. 01Name Elemental on Cavern of Souls every single game. Every creature in the deck is an Elemental. Sunderflock is an Elemental. Cavern of Souls named Elemental means your entire game plan is uncounterable. Never name a different type.
  2. 02Roaming Throne named Elemental is the only correct name. The Throne doubles Elemental triggers. If you accidentally name a different creature type, Throne does nothing for your deck. Every time you cast Throne, declare Elemental immediately. It does NOT come into play with a name — you choose as the ETB resolves.
  3. 03Sunderflock's damage doubles with Throne — check toughness thresholds. 4 Elementals: 4 damage. 4 Elementals + Throne: 8 damage (trigger fires twice). Know before casting whether 8 damage kills their board. Sarkhan's Dragons under indestructible survive — plan accordingly.
  4. 04Deceit Distress mode is sorcery speed — use proactively on T2. Don't hold Deceit waiting for a reactive opportunity. Cast it T2 to strip their Monument to Endurance, Roaming Throne, or Sapling Nursery before they cast it. Reactive Deceit is usually too late.
  5. 05Ashling's Rimebound face returns to hand when it dies — don't leave it back as a blocker for trades. Ashling returns to hand after death — it doesn't go to the graveyard permanently. When Ashling is in Rimebound form, blocking and dying brings it back to hand. If you want to block, that's fine — it comes back. Just know it returns to hand as Ashling, Rekindled (front face) for another ETB next turn.
  6. 06Wistfulness in Naturalize mode is the correct use vs Izzet Lessons. Don't filter in the Izzet Lessons matchup — destroy Monument to Endurance immediately with Wistfulness' Naturalize mode. Their Abandon Attachments can destroy Wistfulness — cast it the turn you draw it, always.
  7. 07Flamebraider tokens are Elementals — they reduce Sunderflock's cost. Each 1/1 Elemental token created by Flamebraider's attack trigger counts as an Elemental you control for Sunderflock's cost reduction. Two Flamebraiders with Throne active = four tokens per attack step = four additional Elementals. Sunderflock free with Throne, 6+ damage from just tokens is feasible.
  8. 08Bounce Off saves Roaming Throne from exile effects. Get Lost exiles — it bypasses indestructible AND protection. If opponent targets Throne with Get Lost, cast Bounce Off in response to return Throne to hand. You lose the Throne for a turn but it's still in hand for replay. This is the correct response to any exile targeting Throne.
  9. 09Winternight Stories Chapter 2 returns an Elemental from GY to hand. Chapter 2: mill 3, return up to 1 from your GY to hand. After a sweeper hit your board, Chapter 2 returns Ashling from GY (if it ended up there, not via Rimebound recursion) to hand. This is your key recovery tool post-sweeper. Always mill with the intent of returning your most valuable Elemental.
  10. 10Cast Sunderflock on opponent's end step for maximum disruption. Cast Sunderflock on their end step after they've passed priority. The ETB damage (and Throne's doubled damage) fires at that point — they can't attack before it resolves. You untap with a cleared board and full mana for your combat step. Main-phase Sunderflock is only correct if you also need to attack that same turn.

TUNING
NOTES

"4 Roaming Throne + 4 Ashling + 4 Flamebraider is locked. Sunderflock at 3+ is flexible."
Lock
The Elemental Core
4 Ashling, 4 Flamebraider, 4 Vibrance, 4 Wistfulness, 4 Roaming Throne = 20 locked cards. These are the engine. No cuts below 4 of any of them — the Sunderflock cost reduction requires consistent Elemental density.
Lock
4 Cavern of Souls
Four copies. The deck cannot function in counter-heavy metas without Cavern. Never cut below 4. The "Elemental" tribal designation means every single card in the 35-nonland slots benefits from Cavern protection.
Flex 3–4
Sunderflock
3 main + 1 SB = 4 total. You want to draw Sunderflock consistently but not flood on it (it's your finisher, not your setup). 3 main is the sweet spot. In aggressive metas where you need the board wipe more urgently, consider a 4th main copy.
Flex 2–4
Deceit
4 main in the March 30 list. Some builds run 3. Deceit's dual function (Distress + bounce) is always relevant. In aggro-heavy metas, trim to 3 and add a fourth Sear or Pyroclasm. In midrange/control metas, 4 Deceit is correct — you need the hand disruption.
Flex 0–3
Bounce Off
3 main in March 30 list. Bounce Off protects Roaming Throne from exile and resets Ashling for ETB cycles. In aggro metas where tempo bounce matters less, trim to 1-2. In control metas, keep 3 — saving Throne from Get Lost is the most important play in those matchups.
Register When
Creature-Based Metas
Izzet Elementals is perfectly positioned when the meta is full of creature strategies. Sunderflock with Throne kills any creature-based board. If the expected field is Simic Rhythm + Dimir Midrange + Izzet Prowess, register Elementals — your board wipe answers all of them.
Rule 01
Hearth Elemental Chain
You chain cantrips into Hearth Elemental by casting cheap instants/sorceries that reduce Hearth's cost via its ability. With one other elemental in play, Hearth costs {1} less. Two elementals: {2} less. You build your elemental count first, then cast Hearth at reduced cost. Hearth is most efficient when you have 2+ other elementals.
Rule 02
Eddymurk Crab Flash Rule
You flash Eddymurk Crab at their attack step, never your main phase. The tap-on-ETB prevents their attacker from attacking this turn. Main-phase deployment loses the tap timing. At their attack step: they declare attackers, you flash in Crab, Crab taps their best attacker before it can attack.
Rule 03
Sunderflock Threshold
You cast Sunderflock when they have 3+ creatures. One or two creatures: use a cheaper answer. At 3 creatures: Sunderflock at 4-5 damage kills their entire board simultaneously. You calculate: does each creature have toughness ≤ Sunderflock's damage? If yes for all three: cast it. If any survives: hold for a better window.
Rule 04
DRC GY Management
You never exile a card from your GY when Dreadhorde Arcanist is in play or in hand. Every Opt, every Lightning Bolt in your GY is a free spell on DRC's attack. You keep your cheapest spells in GY and spend your more expensive spells from hand. You sequence: use expensive GY spells first, keep cheap ones for DRC.
Tune 01
Sear vs Burst Lightning
You run Sear (2 damage or 4 kicked) when your primary targets are 2-4 toughness. You run Burst Lightning (2 damage or 4 kicked for {2}{R}) in the same slot but at different converted costs. Sear's {1}{R} base vs Burst's {R} base — in fast metas, Burst's lower base cost matters more than Sear's middle mode.
Tune 02
Glacial Dragonhunt Quantity
You run Glacial Dragonhunt in long-game control matchups where you need expensive uncounterable finishers. You cut it in fast metas where you never reach 6 mana. Dragonhunt is a metagame card — run 1 as a finisher in your control matchup slot only when the meta rewards going long.
Hearth Cost Reduction
Build Elemental Count First
Hearth Elemental costs {1} less for each other elemental you control. With two other elementals: Hearth is free or near-free. You deploy Eddymurk Crab and Dreadhorde Arcanist before Hearth to reduce its cost maximally.
Eddymurk Flash Rule
Always Flash, Never Sorcery
You flash Eddymurk Crab at their attack step. The tap-on-ETB prevents their attacker from swinging this turn. If you deploy at your main phase, they tap you during YOUR turn and you wasted the timing advantage.
Sunderflock Damage Count
Confirm All Die Before Casting
You cast Sunderflock when ALL of their creatures have toughness ≤ Sunderflock's damage. If any survive, you've wasted the sweeper. You count every creature's toughness before casting and confirm the total wipe before tapping mana.
DRC GY Priority
Cheapest Spells Stay in GY
Your Dreadhorde Arcanist attacks and casts a spell from GY with MV ≤ Arcanist's power for free. You keep your cheapest spells in GY and spend your more expensive spells from hand. An Opt in GY cast by DRC for free is a cantrip at no cost.
