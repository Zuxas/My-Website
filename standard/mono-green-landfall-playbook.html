Chapter 01

Deck
Overview

"You are a ramp deck that can go both taller and wider than most of the format. You have a solid aggro plan, a built-in combo kill, and a grind mode. The opponent never knows which angle to defend." — January, RC Vancouver 2nd Place

Mono-Green Landfall is the Tier A deck in Standard, sitting at 61.3% win rate across 23,000 BO3 games (Plat–Mythic). It is the most popular deck you will face at Cincinnati and DC, and also one of the strongest to pilot — three distinct win conditions that switch dynamically based on what the opponent presents. Against aggressive opponents you play Sapling Nursery and go wide with Treefolk. Against interactive opponents you combo kill through their interaction with Icetill Explorer + Mightform Harmonizer. Against slow decks you stack counters on Sazh's Chocobo and Earthbender Ascension and beat them with a 20/20 trampler.

The deck's power comes from earthbending — turning a land into a haste creature with +1/+1 counters. Earthbending onto a fetch land triggers landfall twice. Badgermole Cub's earthbend on entry turns fetch land plays into mana-positive sequences. Earthbender Ascension accumulates quest counters and eventually grants +1/+1 + trample to any creature on every land drop. The entire engine snowballs: one fetch land can trigger four separate landfall effects simultaneously when your board is developed.

Three Win Conditions

Plan A — Beatdown
Counter Stack + Trample
Sazh's Chocobo enters as a 0/1 but grows a counter every time a land enters — turn 1 Chocobo into turn 2 Ascension means every subsequent land gives +1/+1. Stack trample from Ascension and swing. Effective against any deck that doesn't block efficiently. T1 Chocobo is a clock they must respect immediately.
Plan B — Combo Kill
Explorer + Harmonizer
Icetill Explorer + Mightform Harmonizer + Escape Tunnel (or trample enabler) creates one-shot potential from any board state. Play a land → Harmonizer doubles target creature's power → repeat with each additional land drop → unblockable through Escape Tunnel. A 2-power creature at 4 doublings = 32 damage. Faster with existing counters.
Plan C — Grind
Sapling Nursery Flood
Sapling Nursery creates a 3/4 Treefolk with reach every time a land enters. With Icetill in play, every fetch puts a land into play and mills a card — potentially recovering more fetch lands to continue the loop. Against removal-heavy decks, the Nursery generates threats faster than they can spend resources. The indestructible activation is a sweeper protection button.

Meta Position

As of April 2026 (Plat–Mythic BO3): Mono-Green Landfall is Tier A at 61.3% with 23,000 games and 72 active decks. It is the single most-played archetype in the RC meta by a wide margin. The deck received a significant power upgrade from TMNT: Leatherhead, Swamp Stalker (hexproof + trample + destroy artifact/enchantment on damage) is a combo kill enabler that is immune to most targeted interaction. Her hexproof counter means she survives the removal Dimir typically uses to stop the combo, and trample means she punches through any board state.

The deck's primary weakness is non-interactive combo (Bant Airbending, Nature's Rhythm) and speed — very aggressive red or white decks can kill before Nursery stabilizes. Sideboard construction revolves around these two axes: Torpor Orb for ETB-based strategies, and Meltstrider's Resolve / Mossborn Hydra for the mirror and combo matchups.

Why Study This Deck

You will face Mono-Green Landfall in rounds 1, 3, 5, and 7 at Cincinnati. It is 15.8% of the field. Your Dimir Midrange playbook rates this matchup at 38% — your worst common matchup. Understanding how Mono-Green operates, when their combo is live, and what their sideboard plan is against you makes you significantly better prepared to navigate the matchup correctly. Know your opponent's deck as well as your own.

Card Notes

The Clock
Sazh's Chocobo
{G} 0/1 Bird. Landfall — whenever a land you control enters, put a +1/+1 counter on this creature. Enters as a 0/1 but grows on every land drop thereafter. With Earthbender Ascension in play, every land after Ascension's trigger also gives Chocobo a counter AND gives it trample when Ascension fires. A T1 Chocobo that sees 6 land drops by turn 4 is a 6/6 trampler. Never side out — you're too light on cheap pressure if you do.
The Ramp Engine
Badgermole Cub
{1}{G} 2/2. ETB: earthbend 1 (target land becomes 0/0 creature with haste, gets a +1/+1 counter, returns tapped when it dies). Whenever you tap a creature for mana, add {G}. The ETB earthbend is land-based ramp that triggers landfall — earthbend onto a fetch for two landfall triggers and a land. Cub also doubles mana from Llanowar Elves: tap Elves for {G}, Cub triggers adding {G}. Key vulnerability: opponents can stun or bounce your earthbent land in response to the earthbend trigger, killing it before it can contribute.
The Combo Kill
Mightform Harmonizer
{2}{G} 4/4 Insect Druid. Landfall — whenever a land you control enters, double the power of target creature you control until end of turn. Warp {2}{G}: exile from hand, may cast from exile later. The combo: Icetill Explorer plays extra lands, Harmonizer doubles power on each land drop, Escape Tunnel makes the target unblockable. A 2-power creature with 4 doublings = 32 damage. Warp is critical — warping Harmonizer into exile preserves its ability to double while protecting it from removal. Never cast Harmonizer on an empty board unless you have immediate trample/evasion.
The Land Engine
Icetill Explorer
{3}{G} 2/4. You may play an additional land each turn. You may play lands from your graveyard. Landfall — whenever a land you control enters, mill a card. The graveyard land recursion means cracking Fabled Passage from the GY triggers landfall again. "Milling is upside" — every mill increases the chance of hitting a fetch land in the yard to replay. A naked Icetill with no extra lands or GY fetches is just a 2/4 for 4. Sequence: play Icetill when you have a fetch in hand or GY to immediately start the engine.
The Ramp + Engine
Earthbender Ascension
{1}{G} Enchantment. ETB: earthbend 2 + fetch basic tapped. Landfall — whenever a land enters, add a quest counter; at 4+ counters, put +1/+1 counter on a creature AND grant trample until EOT. The ETB earthbend on a fetch land = two landfall triggers (earthbend + fetch resolves). T1 Elf → T2 Ascension might be the strongest curve in the deck. Every subsequent land drop gifts a counter AND can trigger Ascension's payload. Against control, earthbending your land is sometimes correct even at no profit just to protect it from land destruction or bounce.
The Grind Engine
Sapling Nursery
{X}{G} Enchantment (affinity for Forests). Landfall — whenever a land enters, create a 3/4 green Treefolk token with reach. {1}{G}, exile: Treefolk and Forests you control gain indestructible until EOT. Against any deck that interacts on the board, Nursery generates threats faster than they can remove them. The indestructible activation is a panic button — activate it in response to a sweeper to save your Treefolk board. Key: Nursery reduces cost for each Forest you control. With 5 Forests it costs {G}. The token's reach matters against flying threats.
TMNT Addition
Leatherhead, Swamp Stalker
{3}{G} 5/4 Legendary Crocodile Mutant Rogue. Trample. Enters with a hexproof counter. Whenever Leatherhead deals combat damage to a player, you may remove a counter from her — when you do, destroy target artifact or enchantment that player controls. The hexproof counter protects her from the targeted removal Dimir uses to stop the combo. Trample means she punches through any board state. On damage trigger destroys their key permanents — Enduring Curiosity, Sapling Nursery, equipment. Run 2 copies in the TMNT build replacing 1 Mossborn Hydra and 1 Harmonizer.
The Tutor
Archdruid's Charm
{G}{G}{G} Instant. Three modes: (1) Tutor creature or land — put land onto battlefield tapped or put creature into hand. (2) Put +1/+1 counter on creature, it fights target creature you don't control. (3) Exile target artifact or enchantment. The tutor mode finds Harmonizer for the combo kill or Leatherhead/Greenshell as a situational tutor target. Fight mode kills their blocker and grows a creature simultaneously. Exile mode handles permanents your other spells can't answer. The {G}{G}{G} cost means it requires 3 Forests — plan your earthbends around keeping enough Forests for this activation.
The Top Deck
Esper Origins
{3}{G}{G} Sorcery // Saga Enchantment. Sorcery mode: Surveil 2, gain 2 life. Flashback {3}{G}. If cast from GY: exile it, transform to Saga with finality counter. Saga (Summon: Esper Maduin) — I: reveal top, if permanent put in hand; II: add {G}{G}; III: creatures get +2/+2 + trample. Rarely happy to see it in opening hand. Strong as a mid-game top deck — the surveil works with your GY synergies, 5 mana for 4/4 + 4 life + draw with selection is premium value when you have extra mana. Transforming into the Saga in the late game is a major swing.
The Mirror Breaker
Mossborn Hydra
{3}{G} 0/0 Elemental Hydra. Enters with a +1/+1 counter. Trample. Landfall — whenever a land enters, double the number of +1/+1 counters on this creature. The mirror and combo matchup breaker. One land drop on a Hydra with 4 counters = 8 counters = 16-power trampler with the next drop. Extreme high-variance: incredible when it connects, irrelevant against removal. Side out against anything with efficient removal. Key: being an Elemental means Sunderflock (Izzet Spellementals) cannot bounce it.
The Fetch Lands
Fabled Passage / Escape Tunnel
Fabled Passage: sacrifice → fetch basic tapped, untap if 4+ lands. Escape Tunnel: sacrifice → fetch basic tapped OR give ≤2 power creature unblockable. Four of each is mandatory. Passage untapping at 4+ lands enables important mana sequences. Escape Tunnel's unblockable mode is what enables the combo kill — a 2/2 that's unblockable + Harmonizer doublings = game over. Every Passage and Tunnel in the GY is a free land play with Icetill Explorer active.
The Grind Threat
Fecund Greenshell
{4}{G} 4/6 Elemental Turtle. Reach. As long as you control 10+ lands, all creatures you control get +2/+2. ETB: if toughness > power among your creatures, look at top — put land into play tapped or into hand. The floor is low (5 mana 4/6 reach), but the ceiling is enormous — at 10 lands all your Treefolk tokens are 5/6 reach. The ETB trigger puts lands into play off the top, enabling further landfall chains. Run as a 1-of tutor target via Archdruid's Charm in the late game when you have 8+ lands already.
Chapter 03

Gameplan

"T1 Elf into T2 Ascension is the best curve in the deck. Every land drop after that is a free counter and a free trample trigger."

Turn-by-Turn Framework

Turns 1–2: Setup
Ramp + Engine
Best: T1 Llanowar Elves → T2 Earthbender Ascension (earthbends 2 + fetches a basic). Now every land gives Ascension a quest counter. Also strong: T1 Sazh's Chocobo → T2 Ascension or Badgermole Cub. T1 Chocobo means it's already been growing before Ascension fires. T2 Cub earthbending a fetch = 2 landfall triggers on T2.
Turns 3–4: Power Online
Deploy Threats
Turn 3: Mightform Harmonizer (warp it immediately if removal is likely), or Sapling Nursery (first Treefolk token lands the same turn if you play a land). Turn 4: Icetill Explorer — immediately play your land for turn, mill, and if there's a fetch in the GY you can replay it for a second land drop triggering Nursery + Harmonizer simultaneously.
Turns 5+: Execute
Kill Window
Assess which plan is live: board full of Treefolk → swing for lethal with trample from Ascension. Icetill + Harmonizer + evasion piece (Escape Tunnel) → chain land plays, double target creature power, Escape Tunnel for unblockable, deal 20+ damage. Mossborn Hydra with 3+ counters → one more land = 8+ power trampler.

The Combo Kill — Step by Step

Understanding the combo kill is essential for both playing the deck and playing against it. This is the sequence that ends games in one attack regardless of board state:

Requirements
What You Need
1. Icetill Explorer on the battlefield (extra land plays + land from GY). 2. Mightform Harmonizer on the battlefield (doubles power on landfall). 3. A creature with at least 2 power (Chocobo with counters, Badgermole Cub, or Harmonizer itself). 4. Multiple land plays available — either fetch lands in hand or GY, and/or Ba Sing Se activation. 5. Escape Tunnel or trample enabler for the creature.
The Sequence
Execute the Kill
Activate Escape Tunnel targeting your 2-power creature (unblockable). Play any land → Harmonizer triggers, double that creature's power (2 → 4). Play another land (fetch or GY land via Icetill) → double again (4 → 8). Again → 16. Again → 32. Attack with the 32-power unblockable creature for lethal. Alternatively: use trample from Ascension on a creature that already has 10+ power from counters — one more doubling = 20-40 power trampler.

Reading the Game State

When to Switch Plans
Adaptive Game Plan
Switch to Nursery plan: When you have 4+ lands and a Nursery on board and the opponent doesn't have a sweeper available. Treefolk tokens with reach block their fliers and attack profitably. Switch to combo plan: When they have a board full of blockers. One unblockable creature bypasses their entire defense. Stay on beatdown plan: Against control with no board — Chocobo + Ascension + attacks is faster than waiting to assemble the full combo.
When Earthbending is Wrong
Earthbend Caution
Earthbending is strong but creates vulnerability: an earthbent land can be stunned (Floodpits Drowner), bounced (Into the Flood Maw), or killed before returning. In those cases you lose mana temporarily. Against Dimir: be careful earthbending when they have {1}{U} open — they can stun your earthbent land in response, leaving it as a dead permanent. Against control with open mana: earthbending is telegraphed. Sometimes holding the earthbend for combat or end step is better.
Chapter 05b

Disruption
Lines

"Every land drop is a decision. Earthbend onto the fetch, not the basic. Sequence Escape Tunnel before Harmonizer fires. Know your doubling math before you go to combat."

The Earthbend-onto-Fetch Rule

Badgermole Cub's ETB earthbends 1. Earthbender Ascension ETBs earthbend 2. The target is a land you control. The single most common Mono-Green mistake: earthbending onto a basic Forest instead of a fetch land. A basic Forest earthbent gives you one landfall trigger. A Fabled Passage earthbent gives you two — the Passage entering, then you sacrifice it to fetch a basic (second trigger). The difference is one extra Treefolk token, one extra Harmonizer doubling, one extra Ascension quest counter, one extra Chocobo counter. Over a game that compounds significantly.

Earthbend Onto
Fetch Land (Fabled Passage / Escape Tunnel)
Sequence: earthbend targets Fabled Passage → Passage becomes 0/0 creature with +1/+1 counter + haste (trigger 1: all your landfall sources fire). When it dies / next upkeep it returns as Fabled Passage → you can then sacrifice it to fetch a basic (trigger 2: all sources fire again). Two triggers from one earthbend. Escape Tunnel works identically as the earthbend target — earthbend it, it becomes a creature, when it returns as Escape Tunnel you have its full functionality available (sacrifice for unblockable, sacrifice to fetch basic).
Earthbend Onto
Promising Vein (Situational Double)
Promising Vein: {T} add {C}, {1}{T} sacrifice to fetch basic tapped. Earthbend onto Vein → it becomes a creature (trigger 1). When it returns as Vein → sacrifice it {1}{T} to fetch a basic (trigger 2). Same double-trigger as a fetch. Use this when you've already used your Fabled Passages and Escape Tunnels — Promising Vein is your backup double-trigger earthbend target in the mid-to-late game.

The Combo Kill Sequence — Full Mapping

The Icetill Explorer + Mightform Harmonizer + Escape Tunnel combo is your fastest kill. Mapping every decision point:

Step 1 — Establish the Requirements
What You Need in Play
Mandatory: Icetill Explorer (extra land plays, lands from GY). Mandatory: Mightform Harmonizer (doubles a creature's power on each landfall). Mandatory: A creature with power ≤ 2 at the moment Escape Tunnel activates. Mandatory: Escape Tunnel in play (or multiple other land plays available for the doubling chain even without unblockable — use trample from Ascension instead).

The power-2 check is critical: Escape Tunnel gives unblockable to a creature with power ≤ 2 at the time of activation. If your target creature already has 3+ power from counters, Tunnel cannot target it. You must activate Tunnel BEFORE any Harmonizer doublings happen.
Step 2 — Execute in Order
The Mandatory Sequence
  1. Escape Tunnel activation first — sacrifice Tunnel targeting your 2-power creature (unblockable). Do this before any land drops that would trigger Harmonizer.
  2. Play land 1 → Harmonizer trigger: double target creature (2 → 4). Now it has 4 power.
  3. Play land 2 (fetch or GY land via Icetill) → Harmonizer trigger: double (4 → 8).
  4. Play land 3 → double (8 → 16).
  5. Play land 4 → double (16 → 32). Attack with 32-power unblockable for lethal.
The Doubling Math
Power Required vs Land Drops Available
Starting power determines how many doublings you need:

Start at 2 power (bare minimum): 2→4→8→16→32 = 4 doublings = 4 land drops needed.
Start at 4 power (Harmonizer itself at 4/4): 4→8→16→32 = 3 doublings = 3 land drops.
Start at 8 power (Chocobo with 8 counters): 8→16→32 = 2 doublings = 2 land drops.

Count your available land plays before committing to the combo line — Icetill gives 1 extra, each fetch in GY gives 1 more. If you don't have enough land drops for lethal, wait a turn.
Leatherhead as Combo Piece
Hexproof + Trample Instead of Escape Tunnel
Leatherhead, Swamp Stalker (5/4, trample, enters with hexproof counter) is the Harmonizer combo piece that doesn't need Escape Tunnel. Leatherhead already has trample — no need for unblockable. She has a hexproof counter — targeted removal can't stop the combo attempt mid-sequence. Start the Harmonizer chain with Leatherhead: 5→10→20→40 with only 3 doublings. The opponent cannot target her to break the combo (they must use non-targeting effects or Archdruid's Charm fight mode on her hexproof counter first).

Sapling Nursery — Indestructible Activation Timing

Sapling Nursery has an activated ability: {1}{G}, exile Nursery → Treefolk and Forests you control gain indestructible until end of turn. This is your sweeper protection button. The critical timing rule: activate it in response to the sweeper being cast — not after it resolves.

Correct Sequence
Respond to the Sweeper on the Stack
They cast Day of Judgment (destroy all creatures). Before it resolves, activate Nursery: exile Nursery, all Treefolk and Forests gain indestructible until EOT. Day of Judgment resolves — tries to destroy all creatures. Your Treefolk have indestructible — they are not destroyed. Day of Judgment is wasted.

The two-Nursery bonus: If you have two Nurseries in play, activate one to protect your board, then replay the other next turn to continue generating tokens. You sacrifice one Nursery for protection but the second keeps the engine running.
What Gets Protected
Treefolk AND Forests
The ability grants indestructible to two categories: Treefolk (your tokens) AND Forests (your lands). This means: your earthbent Forest that's currently a creature also gains indestructible — it won't die to mass removal even in its creature form. But note: Forests as lands still get indestructible, which is mostly irrelevant against creature sweepers but matters against Destroy target land effects. The Treefolk protection is the primary use case.

Archdruid's Charm — Three Modes, When to Use Each

Archdruid's Charm ({G}{G}{G} instant) has three modes. The {G}{G}{G} cost means you need three Forests accessible — plan your earthbends around keeping basic Forests available (Ba Sing Se and Promising Vein are not Forests):

Mode 1 — Tutor
Search Your Library
Find any creature or land. Put land into play tapped or creature into hand. Use this to: tutor Mightform Harmonizer for the combo kill when you have the other pieces assembled, tutor Leatherhead as a hexproof trample combo piece, tutor Fecund Greenshell as the 10-land anthem trigger, tutor Icetill Explorer as the engine piece. The tutoring mode effectively makes your deck have more copies of your key pieces.
Mode 2 — Fight
+1/+1 Counter + Fight
Put a +1/+1 counter on your creature, it fights (deals power damage to, takes power damage from) target creature an opponent controls. Your creature gets the counter first, then the fight damage uses the new power. Use to: kill their Ojer Axonil (your creature gets the counter, fights Ojer — if your creature has 5+ power, Ojer dies), kill their Shocking Sharpshooter (1/3 — any creature with 3+ power kills it in the fight). Don't use it when your creature would die in the fight unless that removes their key piece.
Mode 3 — Exile
Exile Target Artifact or Enchantment
Your only main-deck answer to Stormchaser's Talent, Warleader's Call, Earthbender Ascension (in the mirror), and any other enchantment. Exile is permanent — no returning. Use fight mode on their creatures, exile mode on their enchantments. Don't use the exile mode on a creature when fight mode would handle it — the fight mode also gives your creature a permanent +1/+1 counter as a bonus.

Icetill Explorer — Playing Lands From the Graveyard

Icetill Explorer gives you "you may play lands from your graveyard" — this is a static ability, not a triggered one. As long as Icetill is in play, any land in your GY is playable as your land play for the turn (or extra land play from Icetill's other ability). The mill trigger (landfall → mill 1) puts cards into your GY — treat every mill as a potential free land play:

The Loop
Fabled Passage Recycling
Icetill mills a Fabled Passage into GY. Next turn: play Passage from GY (landfall trigger 1), sacrifice Passage to fetch a basic (landfall trigger 2), basic enters (landfall trigger 3 — wait, fetched basics don't trigger separately, just the basic entering as one event). Actually: Passage's sacrifice + fetch = one landfall event (the basic entering). So replaying Passage from GY = 2 landfall events total (Passage entering from GY, then the basic it fetches entering). Each Passage in GY is worth 2 Treefolk tokens, 2 Harmonizer doublings, 2 Ascension quest counters.
Sequencing Icetill
Deploy When You Have a Fetch in Hand or GY
A "naked" Icetill with no fetches in hand or GY is just a 2/4 for 4 with no immediate value. The correct deployment: T4 Icetill when you can immediately play a land and mill — even milling one card gives you a chance of hitting a fetch. Or deploy when you already have a Fabled Passage in GY (from a previous turn's normal play) — deploy Icetill, play your land for the turn, and immediately replay the GY Passage. The engine starts the same turn Icetill enters.

Torpor Orb — Sequencing Your Own ETBs First

When you bring in Torpor Orb from the sideboard (vs Dimir ETB package), deploying it requires care — Orb also blanks your own Badgermole Cub's ETB earthbend:

Correct Sequence
Your ETBs First, Then Orb
Turn 2: cast Badgermole Cub. ETB triggers — earthbend 1. Choose a fetch land to earthbend. Two landfall triggers (earthbend fires, then you sacrifice the earthbent fetch later). All your ETB value is extracted.
Turn 3: cast Torpor Orb. Now all creature ETBs (including future Badgermole Cubs) do nothing. But Dimir's Tidebinder, Drowner, Bat are also blanked.

Never deploy Orb before your own key ETBs unless you're certain the Dimir ETBs are more dangerous than your own.
What Orb Doesn't Blank
Static Abilities and Activated Abilities Are Fine
Torpor Orb stops triggered abilities that trigger when creatures enter. It does NOT stop: Icetill Explorer's static abilities (play extra land, play from GY — those are continuous), Badgermole Cub's "tap creature for mana, add {G}" (that's triggered by tapping for mana, not by entering), Earthbender Ascension's landfall triggered ability (triggered by land entering, not creature entering). Orb is narrower than it appears — most of your engine remains intact.

Oracle-Verified Edge Cases

Lotus Cobra — Mana on EVERY Land Entry, Not Just Your Land Drop
"Landfall — Whenever a land you control enters, add one mana of any color." This triggers on: your normal land drop, fetch land cracking (land searched enters = second trigger), Innkeeper's Talent putting a land in, any effect that puts lands onto the battlefield. Cobra + fetch = 2 mana in one action. Cobra + multiple landfall triggers = explosive mana turns.
Innkeeper's Talent — Class Level Matters for Counter Interaction
Level 1: "At beginning of combat, put a +1/+1 counter on target creature." This targets — triggers valiant on the creature if it has valiant. Level 2 ({G}): "Permanents you control with counters on them have trample and ward {1}." Ward {1} means opponent pays {1} extra to target your creatures with counters. Level 3 ({3}{G}): "Whenever one or more +1/+1 counters are put on a creature you control, put that many +1/+1 counters on each other creature you control." This doubles all counter placement across your board.
Territorial Scythecat — Counters Are Permanent, Not Until EOT
"Landfall — put a +1/+1 counter." Unlike prowess (until EOT), Scythecat's growth is permanent. A Scythecat that triggers 4 times over 4 turns is a 7/7 trample. With Innkeeper's Talent Level 3: each landfall trigger puts a counter on Scythecat AND every other creature you control. A single fetch crack with Level 3 Talent + 3 creatures = 6 total counters distributed.
Kami of Transience — Triggers on Enchantment CAST, Not Entry
"Whenever you cast an enchantment spell, put a +1/+1 counter." Cast, not ETB — means tokens don't trigger it (tokens aren't cast). Kami also has: "At end step, if an enchantment was put into GY from battlefield this turn, return Kami from GY to hand." This makes Kami self-recurring — kill it, sacrifice an enchantment, get Kami back. Persistent threat that's hard to permanently answer.
Fetch Land + Cobra + Scythecat = 2 Triggers + 2 Mana
Play fetch land: Scythecat gets +1/+1, Cobra adds mana. Crack fetch: new land enters → Scythecat gets ANOTHER +1/+1, Cobra adds ANOTHER mana. One fetch = 2 landfall triggers = +2/+2 on Scythecat + 2 free mana from Cobra. This is 4 total triggers if you have 2 Scythecats. The deck's explosive potential comes from maximizing landfall triggers per turn.
Chapter 04

Core Engines

A
The Landfall Cascade Engine
Trigger
Land Enters
Every land that enters simultaneously triggers: Sazh's Chocobo (+1/+1 counter), Mossborn Hydra (doubles counters), Mightform Harmonizer (doubles a creature's power), Earthbender Ascension (quest counter → at 4+: +1/+1 + trample), Sapling Nursery (3/4 Treefolk token), Icetill Explorer (mill a card). One land drop can simultaneously generate 6 separate effects when the full engine is assembled.
Amplify
Double Triggers
Fabled Passage: sacrifice counts as a land entering, then the fetched basic enters — two landfall triggers. Badgermole Cub earthbend onto a fetch: the fetch enters (trigger 1), then you sacrifice the fetch for a basic (trigger 2), AND the earthbent land has a counter on it. Ba Sing Se's activated earthbend on a fetch land = another double trigger. A single Fabled Passage activation with full engine = 12 simultaneous effects.
Sustain
GY Loops
Icetill Explorer lets you play lands from your graveyard. Mill a Fabled Passage → replay it from GY → two more triggers → possibly mill another Passage. With 8+ lands in play, Fecund Greenshell's ETB looks at the top and puts a land into play directly — adding fuel to the engine without spending a land drop. The loop self-sustains once Icetill is active with fetches in the GY.
B
The Mana Acceleration Engine
Base

Heuristics

"The deck is deceptively difficult to play. The earthbending mechanic has weird interactions and the combo math requires practice." — January, RC Vancouver 2026
  1. 01
    T1 Llanowar Elves → T2 Earthbender Ascension is the best curve in the deck. T1 Elf enables T2 Ascension which normally costs {1}{G}. Ascension's ETB earthbends 2 lands and fetches a basic tapped — three landfall triggers before any land plays. Every subsequent land drop gives Ascension a quest counter and eventually grants +1/+1 + trample. This curve outpaces almost any opponent who doesn't interact on T2.
  2. 02
    Earthbend onto a fetch land for two landfall triggers, not onto a basic Forest. The earthbend targets a land you control — target a Fabled Passage or Escape Tunnel. The fetch becomes a 0/0 creature with a counter. When it dies, it returns as a tapped fetch land in your control. Then sacrifice it to fetch a basic — that's trigger 1 (earthbend entering) + trigger 2 (fetch finding a basic). Earthbending a basic Forest only generates one trigger.
  3. 03
    Warp Mightform Harmonizer proactively against Dimir and any removal-heavy deck. Warping Harmonizer costs {2}{G} and exiles it from your hand. The Harmonizer can be cast from exile on any later turn — it is not gone. Warping it protects it from Deep-Cavern Bat (hand disruption), Duress, and any discard effect. Against Dimir who runs Bat in 73.4% of decks, warping Harmonizer on T3 before T4 Bat is a critical sequencing play that guarantees your combo piece is available.
  4. 04
    Sapling Nursery's indestructible activation is a response — hold it for the sweeper. {1}{G}: exile Nursery → Treefolk and Forests gain indestructible until EOT. This activates at instant speed and can be used in response to a Wrath effect, Day of Black Sun, or any mass removal. Timing: hold the activation until they announce the sweep, then respond to save your board. You sacrifice Nursery but keep all Treefolk tokens. If you have two Nurseries, activate one to save the Treefolk then replay the second Nursery next turn to resume token generation.
  5. 05
    Mossborn Hydra doubling math: N counters + 1 land = 2N counters = 2N power trample. A Hydra with 4 counters = 4/4. One land trigger = 8 counters = 8/8 trampler. Two consecutive land plays: 4 → 8 → 16. With three land plays available (fetch + Fabled Passage + Icetill extra land): 4 → 8 → 16 → 32. Track your available land drops before committing to attacks — you want to know if the Hydra can reach lethal before declaring attackers.
  6. 06
    Archdruid's Charm's fight mode requires your creature to survive — target their smallest threat. Fight mode: put a +1/+1 counter on your creature, it deals damage equal to its power to their creature, their creature deals back. If their creature has more power than your creature's toughness, your creature dies. Always use Charm's fight mode on a creature your attacker can survive trading with — or use it to kill their exact-toughness creature knowing your creature will survive the return damage. The +1/+1 counter means your creature might survive one more point of damage than you'd expect.
  7. 07
    Torpor Orb also blanks your own Badgermole Cub ETB — sequence accordingly. Before Torpor Orb enters, cast and resolve Badgermole Cub (and earthbend). Then deploy Orb. This lets you get the mana generation from Cub without blanking your own ETBs. If you draw Orb after Cub is already in play and you haven't earthbent yet — you've locked yourself out. The correct play: cast Cub, earthbend, then play Orb the following turn.
  8. 08
    Never over-invest in Sapling Nursery in the mirror — the Treefolk tokens lose to Mossborn Hydra. A wall of 3/4 reach Treefolk tokens is irrelevant when the opponent has a 50-power trampler. Recognize mirror matchups quickly (they also have 4 Forests, Ascension, Nursery) and pivot entirely to the Hydra plan G1. Side out all 4 Nurseries G2/G3 and replace with Hydras + disruption. The cards you spend on Nursery are wasted resources in the mirror.
  9. 09
    Milling is upside — Icetill Explorer's landfall mill puts fetches in your GY for replaying. Every mill with Icetill has a chance to hit a fetch land (you run 10 fetch lands = 10/34 remaining library). A milled Fabled Passage becomes playable from the GY for free — triggering two more landfall effects. Track what's in your GY. When Icetill is active, you want to be playing lands that trigger it every turn — not holding them. Mill aggressively and trust the GY recursion.
  10. 10
    Leatherhead's hexproof counter protection: use the damage trigger once, strategically. Leatherhead enters with one hexproof counter — protecting her from one targeted effect. Whenever she deals combat damage to a player, you may remove a counter to destroy an artifact or enchantment they control. The key decision: when to spend the hexproof counter vs when to keep it. Against Dimir: keep the hexproof counter until they try to target her. Against control: spend it on their key permanent (Teferi, sweeper enchantment). Against the mirror: spend it on their Sapling Nursery.
  11. 11
    Escape Tunnel's unblockable mode targets power ≤ 2 only — check before activating. Escape Tunnel: {T}, sacrifice → target creature with power 2 or less can't be blocked. If your target creature has a counter making it power 3+, Escape Tunnel cannot make it unblockable. The combo kill requires the target creature to have power ≤ 2 at the time of Escape Tunnel activation. After the first Harmonizer doubling (2 → 4 power), Escape Tunnel no longer works. Sequence: activate Escape Tunnel FIRST with the 2-power creature, then start doubling with land plays.
  12. 12
    Fecund Greenshell is a tutor target — don't deploy it naked without 8+ lands. Greenshell's ETB puts a land from the top into play tapped — triggering Nursery, Ascension, and Harmonizer. The 10-land +2/+2 bonus is good but not the primary reason to run it. Deploy Greenshell when you're already at 7-8 lands and the ETB will trigger multiple effects simultaneously. Use Archdruid's Charm to tutor it as a 5-mana "play a land + trigger engine once" effect. Don't hardcast it when the floor is just a 4/6 reach — there are better 5-drops in other contexts.

Both are flex 1-2 slots

Ba Sing Se Count: 2 vs 3
3 is the RC number
Ba Sing Se enters tapped unless you control a basic land — T1 Ba Sing Se is essentially a tapped Forest. But {2}{G}{T}: earthbend 2 makes it a mana-neutral land activation in the mid-game that generates double landfall triggers. The problem is it's not a Forest, which matters for Sapling Nursery cost reduction and Archdruid's Charm's {G}{G}{G}. Don't run 4 — the non-Forest penalty stacks. 3 is the correct balance.
Promising Vein: 2 copies
The mana-positive T2 land
Promising Vein enters as {C} mana but its activated ability {1}{T} sacrifices to fetch any basic tapped — enabling the T2 earthbend-onto-fetch play with Cub or setting up later land sequences. Not a Forest, so it doesn't help Nursery affinity or Charm. Run exactly 2 — they fill a specific niche but aren't generically good enough for 3+. Side note: {C} mana doesn't trigger Badgermole Cub's "tap creature for mana" — only colored mana sources do.
Torpor Orb: 3 copies in SB
Non-negotiable in current meta

If you cut to 2 or less, you'll have games where Dimir has the better G2 simply because you didn't find Orb. At RC Cincinnati expect to face Dimir 3-4 times. Three Orbs is mandatory preparation.

20-Minute Crash Course

If you're sitting down in 20 minutes, run through this in order:

  • Mulligan rule: Keep any 7 with Icetill Explorer + a land OR Badgermole Cub + Earthbender Ascension. Never keep a hand without at least one landfall enabler.
  • Earthbender Ascension rule: Puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature each time you play a land after the first. One Ascension + two extra land drops = your creature has 2 extra counters. Never tap out the turn you play Ascension.
  • Icetill Explorer rule: Lets you play additional lands. With Explorer in play, T3 is a double land drop turn. Protect Explorer with your entire interaction suite — it's your best card.
  • Lumra rule: Lumra enters and puts X lands from GY into play tapped (X = Lumra's power). Fill your GY with lands via Spelunking and cantrips before casting Lumra.
  • Mossborn Hydra rule: Can't be countered if you control 7+ lands. At 7 lands it enters uncounterable as a 7/7 trample. Deploy it when you hit 7 lands — they can't interact.
  • Board rule: Meltstrider's Resolve fights their key creature AND puts a +1/+1 counter on yours. Always choose their largest threat to fight with your largest creature.

Testing Framework

Block 01
Ascension Trigger Sequencing
Practice the Earthbender Ascension trigger order. Play land → Ascension triggers → choose which creature gets the counter. Know which creature to pump for max value.
Block 02
Icetill Explorer Protection
Practice protecting Icetill Explorer. Their removal on Explorer = your removal on their threat. Counter their removal before protecting Explorer.
Block 03
Lumra Setup
Practice Lumra setup: mill your GY with Spelunking. Count GY lands before casting. Lumra P/T = lands in play; GY lands ETB = more counters.
Block 04
Mossborn Hydra Timing
Deploy Hydra at exactly 7 lands. At 7: uncounterable 7/7 trample. At 6: counterable. The one-land difference is critical vs counter-heavy opponents.

Matchday Routine

  1. 01
    Icetill Explorer protection: your best card. Spend removal specifically to protect Explorer.
  2. 02
    Earthbender Ascension triggers: count your land drops. One Ascension after 3 lands = 3 counters distributed.
  3. 03
    Mossborn Hydra timing: exactly 7 lands. Know your land count throughout so you deploy at the uncounterable window.
  4. 04
    Between rounds: did you correctly sequence land drops to maximize Ascension counters? Inefficient sequencing is the most common mistake.
  5. 05
    Lumra GY preparation: count your GY lands before casting Lumra. Each land entering play makes Lumra bigger.
