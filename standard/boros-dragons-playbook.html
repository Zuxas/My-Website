Chapter 01

DECK
IDENTITY

"Every creature flies. Every threat requires an immediate answer. You play bigger than aggro and faster than control."

Boros Dragons — known competitively as "Momo Drum" — is a flying tribal midrange deck. The core engine is Momo, Friendly Flier: a 1/1 flying lifelink lemur that costs {W} and reduces the cost of each Dragon you cast by {1}. With Momo in play, Clarion Conqueror costs one mana, Magmatic Hellkite costs two, Nova Hellkite costs two. An entire curve of Dragons comes down a full turn early.

The Dragon package is brutal: Sarkhan, Dragon Ascendant makes every Dragon you control indestructible until EOT when it attacks. Clarion Conqueror taps target permanent when it enters — locking their mana dork, blocker, or key piece. Magmatic Hellkite "locks" their mana for a turn. Nova Hellkite deals 2 damage on entry. Dragonhawk, Fate's Tempest deals damage equal to your creatures' power and exiles to generate card advantage.

The result is a deck that opens on T1 Momo → T2 discounted Dragon → T3 more Dragons, presenting 8-12 flying power by turn 3 that opponents struggle to interact with. Cavern of Souls names Dragon or Bird, making your key threats uncounterable. Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon tutors for Dragons and provides them mana. Everything is synergistic and fast.

The Momo Ramp Engine

Why Momo Is the Deck
One Mana Ahead Every Turn
Momo reduces each Dragon's cost by {1}. With Momo on turn 1: T2 Clarion Conqueror for {W} (instead of {1}{W}) + tap their creature. T2 Nova Hellkite for {1}{R} (instead of {2}{R}) + 2 ETB damage. T2 Magmatic Hellkite for {2}{R} (instead of {3}{R}). The acceleration is linear — Momo at 1, Clarion at 0-1, Hellkite at 1-2, repeat. Against Llanowar Elves + Badgermole Cub decks, your T2 Clarion tapping their T1 dork before they use it is a devastating tempo play.
Cavern of Souls
Makes Your Threats Uncounterable
Cavern of Souls naming Dragon makes every Dragon you cast off its mana uncounterable. Momo is a Bird — Cavern naming Bird makes Momo uncounterable too. Against Izzet Lessons (Spell Snare, It'll Quench Ya), Jeskai Control (No More Lies), and Azorius Flash (Seam Rip) — your Dragons simply cannot be countered. This is the deck's primary answer to permission-heavy metas.
Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon
Tutor + Mana Engine
Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon is a saga that both tutors for Dragons and provides colored mana. Three copies in the main deck ensures consistent Dragon access even when drawing poorly. It counts as mana fixing, tutor, and threat multiplier in one slot. Against control decks that answer every individual threat, Maelstrom guarantees you always have another Dragon to deploy.

Key Card Notes

The Engine
Momo, Friendly Flier
1/1 flying lifelink for {W}. Each Dragon you cast costs {1} less. Momo doesn't need to be protected (it's already done its job once you've cast 1-2 discounted Dragons), but it attacks for 1 lifelink every turn which accumulates against aggro. Cavern naming Bird makes Momo uncounterable. With Momo on T1, every Dragon in the deck effectively costs 1 less all game.
The Tempo Play
Clarion Conqueror
Dragon creature ({1}{W}{W} normally, {W}{W} with Momo). ETB: tap target permanent. The tap is crucial — it locks their Llanowar Elves before use, taps their Pollinator before it can double tokens, or taps their land to set them back a turn. Clarion comes with a body that attacks. Flying + ETB tap for {0-1}{W}{W} with Momo is one of the best T2 plays in the format.
The Indestructible
Sarkhan, Dragon Ascendant
Dragon creature. When Sarkhan attacks, each Dragon you control gains indestructible until EOT. Your entire Dragon board becomes immune to removal during combat. Wrath effects that destroy still kill (indestructible ≠ protection from), but any "deal damage" or "destroy" targeting is blanked. Attack aggressively with Sarkhan each turn to protect your investment.
The Finish
Dragonhawk, Fate's Tempest
When Dragonhawk enters, it deals damage to each opponent equal to the highest power among creatures you control, and you exile that many cards from the top of your library, casting one for free. With a 4/4 Dragon on board: 4 damage + exile 4 + cast 1 free. With Sarkhan (power 3 typically): 3 damage + exile 3. This provides both reach damage and card advantage simultaneously.
Chapter 03

GAME
PLAN

"T1 Momo. T2 Clarion taps their dork. T3 two more Dragons. They're dead before their engine goes online."
The Gold Standard
T1 Momo → T2 Discounted Dragon
T1: Momo ({W}), 1/1 flying lifelink in play. T2: Clarion Conqueror for {W}{W} (not {1}{W}{W}) — ETB taps their Llanowar Elves or Badgermole Cub before they tap for mana. Now they're at 2 mana on T3 instead of 4. Your T3: two more discounted Dragons. Their T3: scramble to find an answer to 6-8 flying power.
Without Momo
Full Curve Beats Too
Without Momo, the curve starts T2 Clarion (full cost {1}{W}{W}) or T2 Sarkhan (check cost). Still effective — your Dragons are individually powerful enough without the discount. The deck doesn't require Momo to function; it's a speed amplifier, not a crutch. If Momo dies immediately, continue deploying Dragons at full cost and attack from the air.
Mulligan
Need White Mana T1
Momo requires {W} on T1 — keep hands with Plains, Inspiring Vantage, Sacred Foundry, or Multiversal Passage. Mulligan any hand with no white source. Hands with Momo + white source + any 2-3 Dragons are snaps. Hands with 5+ Dragons and no Momo or Maelstrom are mediocre — they're castable but miss the power discount. At 6 keep: Momo + 2 lands + any Dragon.
End Game
Attack Every Turn
Sarkhan makes everything indestructible during combat when he attacks. Always attack with Sarkhan first (to trigger the indestructible clause), then declare remaining attackers. This means your Dragons survive damage-based removal and fight effects during combat. In racing scenarios: Sarkhan + 3 Dragons = entire board is indestructible every turn you attack.
Chapter 04

CORE
ENGINES

"Sarkhan attacks first. Always. The indestructible clause is the reason the deck wins combat."
Engine 1
Momo Cost Reduction
Momo reduces every Dragon's cost by {1}. Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon provides additional Dragon mana. Cavern of Souls taps for colored mana on Dragon spells. Result: by T3-T4, you're deploying Dragons that should cost 4-5 mana for 2-3 mana. The curve compression means you always have more threats than they have answers.
Engine 2
Sarkhan Indestructible
When Sarkhan attacks, ALL your Dragons gain indestructible until EOT. This protects your entire board from removal during combat — fight effects, damage triggers, blocking damage. The key: Sarkhan must attack. Always attack with him unless the life total math doesn't allow it. One Sarkhan attack makes your board completely immune to combat-based removal.
Engine 3
Dragonhawk Reach
Dragonhawk deals damage equal to your highest-power creature and exiles that many cards. With a 4-power creature: 4 damage to face + exile 4 + free cast. In closing games, Dragonhawk connecting for 4+ damage in the air while also playing your next card for free is an overwhelming value engine. Always aim to have a 4+ power creature in play when Dragonhawk enters.
Chapter 05

LINES &
TACTICS

"Always attack with Sarkhan before declaring other attackers. This is the #1 sequencing rule in the deck."
Line 01 — Sarkhan Must Attack to Give Indestructible
Sarkhan, Dragon Ascendant's trigger reads: "Whenever Sarkhan attacks, each Dragon you control gains indestructible until end of turn." This means: (1) Sarkhan himself must be declared as an attacker for the trigger to fire. (2) ALL your Dragons (including Sarkhan himself) gain indestructible. (3) It's until END of turn — so the Dragons are indestructible during combat AND during the post-combat main phase. Always declare Sarkhan as an attacker. Never leave him back as a blocker if you want combat indestructibility.
Line 02 — Clarion Conqueror's ETB Tap Targets ANY Permanent
Clarion Conqueror says "When ~ enters, tap target permanent." Any permanent — creature, artifact, enchantment, land, planeswalker. Priority targets in current meta: (1) Llanowar Elves / Badgermole Cub before they tap for mana. (2) Gene Pollinator before it can double tokens. (3) Quantum Riddler before it attacks and draws cards. (4) Sapling Nursery before it generates tokens. (5) Their key land if they're color-strapped. The tap is at the START of their turn — tap it when they're most constrained on mana or actions.
Line 03 — Momo's Cost Reduction Applies When Casting, Not on the Battlefield
Momo, Friendly Flier reduces Dragon spells you cast. The reduction is applied at cast time. If Momo is removed before you declare the Dragon (in response to your casting), the Dragon's cost is NOT reduced — it was already on the stack with the cost locked in. Conversely: if you cast a Dragon targeting Clarion Conqueror in response to Momo dying, the cost was already locked when you paid mana. Momo only needs to be alive when you START casting, not when it resolves.
Line 04 — Dragonhawk Exiles, Then Casts — All in One ETB
Dragonhawk, Fate's Tempest ETB: deal damage equal to the power of the highest-power creature you control to each opponent, then exile that many cards from the top of your library. You may cast one of those cards for free (any mana cost). The exile and cast are part of the same trigger — it all happens at once. The free cast can be any mana cost, including 5+ mana Dragons. With a 5-power creature in play: 5 damage + exile 5 + play a Nova Hellkite free. This is the deck's most explosive line in the late game.

Chapter 09

HEURISTICS

"Attack with Sarkhan first every combat. This is the single most important sequencing rule in the deck."
  1. 01Attack with Sarkhan before declaring other attackers. His trigger fires when he attacks: all your Dragons gain indestructible until EOT. If you attack with other Dragons first and they get hit by fight effects or damage, Sarkhan's trigger hasn't fired yet. Always Sarkhan first, everything else second.
  2. 02Name Dragon on Cavern of Souls — not Bird, not Human. Your Dragons are your power plays. Momo is a Bird but it's a 1/1 — spending Cavern mana on Momo is less critical than protecting your 4/4+ Dragons. The exception: if they're countering Momo specifically, name Bird. Default: Dragon.
  3. 03Clarion Conqueror's tap targets any permanent — evaluate optimally before casting. Ask: what is their most important untapped permanent right now? Their Gene Pollinator is about to double tokens? Tap it. Their Quantum Riddler is about to attack and draw cards? Tap it. Their Hallowed Fountain is their only blue source? Tap it. This ETB is a free tempo play — maximize it.
  4. 04Torpor Orb also shuts off YOUR ETBs. When Torpor Orb is in play, YOUR Clarion Conqueror, Nova Hellkite, and Voice of Victory ETBs also don't trigger. Only board Torpor Orb when opponent's ETBs matter more than yours — primarily vs Simic/Bant Rhythm where Gene Pollinator is the core engine.
  5. 05Momo only needs to be in play when you START casting the Dragon. The cost reduction is locked in when you pay mana. If opponent removes Momo in response to your Dragon, the Dragon still casts at the discounted cost. This makes Momo very hard to punish — they can't "cost denial" by removing Momo after you start casting.
  6. 06Dragonhawk's exile triggers before the free cast — use the highest-power creature to maximize. The damage AND the number of cards exiled is based on the highest-power creature at the moment Dragonhawk's ETB resolves. With Sarkhan (3 power), Magmatic Hellkite (4 power) — exile 4 and cast 1 free. Try to have a 4+ power creature in play when Dragonhawk enters.
  7. 07Maelstrom of the Spirit Dragon is an enchantment — Abandon Attachments can destroy it. Izzet Lessons pilots run Abandon Attachments which destroys any enchantment. Don't treat Maelstrom as guaranteed — cast its chapters as quickly as possible and get the Dragon tutor early.
  8. 08Sarkhan's indestructibility is until END of turn — covers post-combat removal too. After combat, during main phase 2, your Dragons are still indestructible until EOT. This means Get Lost cast in response to a post-combat Shock still fails to exile (if Get Lost uses destroy... check: Get Lost exiles, not destroys. Indestructible ≠ immune to exile). Indestructible stops DESTRUCTION, not exile. Get Lost still works even with Sarkhan's ability active. Know this before going to combat.
  9. 09Requisition Raid draws a card if the destroyed permanent had MV 4+. Monument to Endurance is MV 4 — always draws. Sapling Nursery is MV 4 — draws. Use Requisition Raid on high-value targets to not only remove the threat but also replace the spell in your hand.
  10. 10Blood Crypt provides B mana — this is mostly irrelevant but occasionally matters. The deck is R/W but Blood Crypt is in the mana base for some flex. Don't accidentally tap Blood Crypt for red then find yourself without black if a SB card requires it. Check your SB cards for any black mana requirements before finalizing the 75.

Rule 01
Haste Sequence
You attack with Dragonhawk the turn it enters. You never wait to attack — their flash creatures (Floodpits Drowner, Spider Manifestation) come down AFTER Dragonhawk's haste attack. You declare the attack before they can deploy flash blockers. Haste means your first attack is always free.
Rule 02
Worldsoul's Rage Calculation
Before casting Worldsoul's Rage: count your lands. 6 lands = 6 damage. 8 lands = 8 damage. 10 lands = 10 damage. You never cast Rage without knowing the exact damage output. You target their creature when the damage kills it AND leaves enough for face damage. You target face when they're at or below your land count.
Rule 03
Temur Battlecrier Sequencing
You deploy Temur Battlecrier AFTER your Dragon is in play. Battlecrier on an empty board gives +0/+0 to nothing — it's dead. Battlecrier with Dragonhawk in play: Dragonhawk attacks as 5/3 haste flier. You always check: "do I have a Dragon?" before deploying Battlecrier.
Rule 04
Devastating Onslaught Kill Math
Before casting Devastating Onslaught (+X/+X trample where X = land count): identify your kill target. At 5 lands, Onslaught on Dragonhawk (3/3): it becomes 8/8 trample. That's lethal from 20 with one hit. You know the lethal math at each land count before casting.
Tune 01
Worldsoul's Rage Land Count Threshold
You track your land count specifically for Worldsoul's Rage efficiency. At 5 lands: Rage kills 5-toughness creatures. At 7: kills 7-toughness. You cut lands only when your curve is low enough to not need the high-count Rage. You run 24+ lands when Rage is your primary removal and win condition.
Tune 02
Requisition Raid Flex Slot
You adjust your Requisition Raid count based on how often your board gets swept. In wipe-heavy metas: +1 Raid (three bodies from one card rebuilds faster). In creature-heavy metas: -1 Raid (you need more removal). You treat Raid as your resilience card — more against sweepers, less against aggro.
Haste Priority
Attack Before Blockers
You declare attackers with Dragonhawk before your opponents deploy flash blockers. Their Floodpits Drowner comes down at flash speed — but only AFTER you've already declared attackers. Your haste attack happens before any EOT flashing is possible.
Temur Battlecrier Order
Dragon First, Crier Second
You sequence: Dragon enters → then cast Temur Battlecrier. Battlecrier on an empty board grants +2/+0 to nothing. With any Dragon in play: Battlecrier makes every Dragon attack for +2/+0. You never cast Battlecrier speculatively.
Worldsoul's Rage Mode
Face or Creature?
Before casting Worldsoul's Rage, you decide: face or creature? Face: use when their life total is ≤ your land count. Creature: use when their creature's toughness is ≤ your land count AND the creature is blocking your lethal attack.
Pyroclasm Window
Board State Threshold
You cast Pyroclasm ({1}{R}: 2 damage each creature) when they have 3+ creatures with 2 or less toughness. Fewer creatures: hold Pyroclasm. Your dragons (typically 3+ toughness) survive Pyroclasm — you sweep their board while your threats remain.

Know Cold
Sarkhan Attack Order
ALWAYS attack with Sarkhan first in any combat where you want indestructibility. His trigger fires when he attacks — all your other Dragons get the benefit only if he attacks. Forgetting to attack with Sarkhan means your Dragons die to their burn spells and fight effects. Practice this until it's automatic.
Know Cold
Indestructible ≠ Exile Protection
Indestructible means your Dragons can't be destroyed. Get Lost exiles — it bypasses indestructible. Avatar's Wrath that says "exile all creatures" bypasses indestructible. Only "destroy" effects are stopped. Know which of their removal effects destroy vs exile before going to combat each game.
Verify
Cavern Naming Decision
Name Dragon on Cavern. Only name Bird if you specifically need Momo to be uncounterable (rare). Default: Dragon = your 4 Sarkhan, 4 Clarion, 4 Magmatic, 4 Nova all become uncounterable. This is worth more than protecting one 1/1 Momo.
