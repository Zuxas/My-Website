Chapter 01

DECK
IDENTITY

"You win by drawing three cards for one mana, making Treasure tokens to cast more spells, and pinging them for three every turn — all from one artifact."

Izzet Lessons won the 2025 Magic: The Gathering World Championship in Seth Manfield's hands and has remained one of the top two decks in Standard ever since. The deck is built around a single artifact — Monument to Endurance — and an engine that makes it work as early as turn 3.

The engine: every Lesson subtype card in your graveyard (Firebending Lesson, Abandon Attachments, Combustion Technique, Iroh's Demonstration, Boomerang Basics) powers two discount engines. Gran-Gran reduces the cost of each spell you cast by one mana when you have 3+ Lessons in the GY. Artist's Talent does the same. With both active and 3 Lessons in the GY, every spell costs two less. Monument to Endurance drops to one mana. Accumulate Wisdom drops to free and draws three cards.

Once Monument is in play, every spell you cast triggers a discard → you choose: draw a card, make a Treasure token, or deal 3 damage to any target. Three spells in a turn = 9 damage from nowhere. Monument triggers on both turns — their turn and yours — so a resolved Monument is effectively dealing 6+ damage per full turn cycle while drawing you cards and making mana.

The deck has only one creature (Gran-Gran, 4 copies). Everything else is instants, sorceries, and enchantments. There are no creatures to block your spells with — no Thoughtseize, no Removal-friendly targets. Opponents who come in with creature removal have dead cards.

The Three Axes

Axis 1 — Resource Engine
Accumulate Wisdom: Draw 3 for Free
With 3+ Lessons in GY and Gran-Gran or Artist's Talent active, Accumulate Wisdom ({1}{U} normally) costs {U} or even {0}. Drawing 3 cards for effectively free is Ancestral Recall territory. You hit this condition by turn 3 routinely — your entire spell package is Lessons. Once you can draw 3 per Wisdom cast, you never run out of resources.
Axis 2 — Win Condition
Monument to Endurance: 3 Damage Per Discard
Monument says: discard a card, choose — draw, make Treasure, or opponent loses 3 life. Three discards in a turn = 9 damage. Monument triggers on both players' turns. With Artist's Talent leveled, you discard whenever you cast a spell. A chain of 3 spells on your turn = 9 damage. They untap, you hold up It'll Quench Ya, they cast something = 3 more damage on their turn. Monument kills from full life in 2-3 turns once active.
Axis 3 — Protection
It'll Quench Ya + Stormchaser's Talent
It'll Quench Ya ({U}, counter unless they pay {3}) protects Monument and Artist's Talent at minimal cost. Stormchaser's Talent provides an alternate win condition — Level 3 creates 1/1 Otters per spell, and with the Lessons engine active you're casting 4-6 spells per turn. Stormchaser's also generates Otters to block early aggression, buying time for the Monument engine to come online.

Register Conditions

Register When
Midrange and Creature Decks Dominate
Izzet Lessons is at its best when opponents are leaning on creatures and targeted removal — both of which do nothing against your 30+ noncreature spells. If the field is Dimir Midrange, Boros Aggro, Mono-Green, and Bant Rhythm, you're favored in most matchups and drawing 3 cards per Accumulate Wisdom while they have dead removal in hand.
Consider Other Options When
Artifact/Enchantment Hate Is Widespread
Annul, Ancient Vendetta, Abrade, and Graveyard exile (Rest in Peace) all hurt you. If the metagame shifts to heavy hate for artifacts and enchantments specifically, the engine is more fragile. Also consider alternatives when the mirror (Izzet Lessons) is above 15% of the field — the mirror is a technical grind that depends heavily on Monument hitting the table first.

Chapter 03

GAME
FLOW

"T1 Gran-Gran. T2 Abandon Attachments (bin 2 Lessons). T3 Artist's Talent + Accumulate Wisdom (free, draw 3). T4 Monument."

Turn-by-Turn Blueprint

Turn 1
Gran-Gran
Always T1 Gran-Gran if it's in hand. It's a 1/3 that blocks early aggression, its attack trigger discards to fuel Monument later, and it's the cost reduction engine. Every hand without Gran-Gran T1 is a slower hand. Gran-Gran on T1 means full cost reduction is available by T3.
Turn 2
Abandon Attachments
Cast Abandon Attachments ({2}{U}, reduced to {1}{U} with Gran-Gran). Look at top 4, bin 2 Lessons. Now you have 3 Lessons in GY (Attachments + 2 binned). Full cost reduction is LIVE on T3. Alternatively: cast Artist's Talent Level 1 on T2 to set up Monument triggers earlier.
Turn 3
Artist's Talent + Free Wisdom
Cast Artist's Talent Level 1 ({1}{U}, free with Gran-Gran). Every spell now triggers Monument when it resolves. Then cast Accumulate Wisdom for free — draw 3 cards. You're now up 3 cards from Wisdom + maintaining hand size. If you drew Monument, deploy it. If not, hold up It'll Quench Ya or cast Firebending Lesson to keep binning Lessons.
Turn 4+
Monument + Chain
Cast Monument to Endurance ({3}, reduced to {1}). It resolves. Now every spell you cast triggers Artist's Talent (discard) which triggers Monument (choose: draw/Treasure/3 damage). Cast Firebending Lesson (free) → discard → Monument → 3 damage. Cast Combustion Technique (free) → discard → Monument → Treasure. Cast Accumulate Wisdom (free, draw 3) → discard → Monument → 3 more damage. You're doing 6-9 damage per turn while refueling.

Play vs Draw

On the Play
T1 Gran-Gran is Non-Negotiable
On the play, Gran-Gran on T1 means you're one full turn ahead on the engine. T2 Abandon Attachments fills the GY. T3 you have full discount live and can cast Accumulate Wisdom for free. Any hand with T1 Gran-Gran and 3 lands is a keep. Prioritize the T1 Gran-Gran line over any other consideration.
On the Draw
Extra Card Buys Flexibility
On the draw, you have an extra card — this makes hands without T1 Gran-Gran more keepable. A hand with Artist's Talent T2 + Abandon Attachments T3 still hits the engine by T4. The extra card often means you draw Gran-Gran by T2 anyway. Looser keeps on the draw, but still mulligan hands with 0 or 1 lands.

When Disrupted

If Gran-Gran Dies
Rebuild Through Artist's Talent
Gran-Gran dying is painful but not game-ending. Artist's Talent Level 2 provides cost reduction independently. If you have Artist's Talent Level 2 active, you still get {1} discount per spell. A second Gran-Gran from hand restores the full engine. Don't overextend trying to protect Gran-Gran — let them spend removal on it and rebuild. Their removal is sorcery-speed in most cases.
If Monument is Destroyed
Use Boomerang Basics to Replay
If Monument is Annulled or Abrade'd, bounce it with Boomerang Basics before they destroy it — return it to hand as a new object. Recast next turn. If it's destroyed before you can Boomerang it, deploy the second copy. Four copies means you typically have one in hand or draw it quickly. Use It'll Quench Ya to protect Monument resolution.

Mulligan Framework

Snap keeps: Any 7 with Gran-Gran + 3 lands + any Lesson spell. Any 7 with Artist's Talent + Abandon Attachments + 3 lands.

Keep on 7: 3 lands + Gran-Gran + any 3 spells. 3 lands + Abandon Attachments + any enchantment. Hands with Monument but no engine setup are keeps if you have 3 lands and a Lesson.

Mulligan: 0-1 lands. 5+ lands. Any hand without at least one Lesson spell (Firebending, Combustion, Iroh's, Abandon, Boomerang Basics). Any hand with 4+ copies of the same card.

To 6: Keep any 6 with 2-3 lands + Gran-Gran OR any 6 with 3 lands + Artist's Talent + 2 Lessons. At 5, you need Gran-Gran + 2 lands + 2 spells minimum.

Engine Priority Order

Engine P1
Lesson Accumulation (GY Setup)
Priority one is getting 3+ differently named Lessons into your GY. Abandon Attachments bins 2 at once. Firebending Lesson, Combustion Technique, Iroh's Demonstration, Boomerang Basics all count. This is the prerequisite for everything else. Actively cast cheap Lessons early even if they don't do much — you're fueling Gran-Gran and Accumulate Wisdom.
Engine P2
Cost Reduction Active
Gran-Gran (up to {3} reduction) + Artist's Talent Level 2 ({1} reduction) = up to {4} total cost reduction. In practice with 3 Lessons in GY: {2} from Gran-Gran + {1} from Talent = {3}. Monument costs {0}. Accumulate Wisdom costs {0}. Get both discount engines active before deploying Monument for maximum efficiency.
Engine P3
Monument Loop
Monument active + Artist's Talent Level 1 + spells in hand = the kill engine. Each spell triggers Talent (discard) which gives you a Monument activation. Three spells = 3 activations = choose from: 9 damage, 3 Treasures (3 mana), 3 cards, or any combination. The correct mode depends on your board state — Treasure when you need mana, damage when they're at 9 or below, draw when you need gas.

Chapter 05

LINES &
TACTICS

"Bounce Monument with Boomerang Basics to reset it as a new object. It re-enters the battlefield and you get the ETB trigger — but Monument has no ETB. The point is to make it a new permanent that hasn't been tracked for removal."

Three Lines You Must Know Cold

Line 01 — Critical
Monument Triggers ONCE Per Activation — You Choose the Mode
Monument to Endurance's activated ability is: {T}: discard a card, then choose one — draw a card, create a Treasure, or opponent loses 3 life. This is ONE activation per tap. Artist's Talent Level 1 says "whenever you cast a spell, discard a card" — that discard does NOT automatically trigger Monument. Monument requires you to TAP it as an activated ability. Artist's Talent gives you the discard trigger, but you must then separately activate Monument using that discarded card or another. Plan: cast spell → Talent triggers (discard) → separately tap Monument → choose mode.
Line 02 — Critical
Gran-Gran Discount Counts DIFFERENTLY Named Lessons
Gran-Gran reduces spell costs by {1} for each DIFFERENTLY named Lesson card in your GY. Having 4 Firebending Lessons in GY = {1} discount (they're all the same name). Having Firebending Lesson + Combustion Technique + Iroh's Demonstration = {3} discount (3 differently named). Your 5 Lesson card types (Firebending, Combustion, Iroh's, Abandon Attachments, Boomerang Basics) are all differently named. With all 5 types in GY = maximum {3} discount (capped at 3). Sequence Abandon Attachments T2 to bin 2 differently named Lessons immediately.
Line 03 — Critical
It'll Quench Ya Counters Spells Unless {3} — Not Just {1}
It'll Quench Ya! costs {U} and counters target spell unless its controller pays {3}. This is a "soft counter" — opponents CAN pay {3} to get through it. Against Monument to Endurance on T3 (they cast for {3}): hold It'll Quench Ya but recognize they CAN pay {3} to force it through. The best use is against cheap removal like Annul ({U}) or Abrade ({1}{R}) where paying {3} extra is prohibitive. Don't rely on It'll Quench Ya against expensive spells where they have mana available.

Timing Windows

When to Use Monument's Damage Mode vs Draw vs Treasure
Damage mode (opponent loses 3 life): use when they're at 12 or below and you can chain 3-4 activations in a turn to kill. Never use damage mode when they're at full life — you need at least 7 turns of 3 damage each to kill from 20. Treasure mode: use when you need to cast more spells this turn (generate mana to chain). Draw mode: use when you're low on cards and not in immediate kill range. The optimal pattern mid-game: Treasure → cast another spell → Treasure → cast another spell → Damage when in kill range.
Boomerang Basics — Return Monument to Reset vs Return Their Permanent
Boomerang Basics returns ANY nonland permanent. Two main uses: (1) return your own Monument if they target it with removal — it goes to hand as a new object, can be redeployed next turn. (2) Return their key permanent — Sapling Nursery, Stormchaser's Talent in the mirror, Gene Pollinator. The self-return mode is correct when Monument is about to die. The tempo mode is correct when their permanent would end the game before you can stabilize.
Artist's Talent Level Timing
Level 1 ({1}{U}) is always correct to deploy early — the discard engine is live immediately. Level 2 ({3}{U}) — pay when you have excess mana and want cheaper spells. Don't level up if you need to hold mana for It'll Quench Ya. Level 3 ({5}{U}) — extremely expensive; only correct in very long games where Otter tokens close out the game. Never level 3 when they have counterspell mana open.
Chapter 06

ROLE
ASSIGNMENT

"You are always the inevitability player. They run out of answers before you run out of resources."
Primary Role
Resource Grind
Set up your engine, draw cards, stay ahead on resources. Don't rush Monument — deploy it when you have It'll Quench Ya backup or they're tapped out. You win by having more cards than they have answers. Gran-Gran + Accumulate Wisdom generates 3+ card advantage per turn.
vs Control, Midrange
Secondary Role
Reactive Control
Hold Firebending Lesson, Combustion Technique, and Iroh's Demonstration as interaction rather than deploying them for Lesson count. Answer their key threats before they can pressure you. Gran-Gran's 1/3 body blocks early aggression. Iroh's Demonstration wipes token boards.
vs Aggro, Token Decks
Endgame Role
Monument Kill Machine
Once Monument is active and they're at 12 or below, switch all Monument modes to Damage. Three activations per turn at 3 damage each = 9 per turn. They die in 2 more turns. Cast spells specifically to generate Monument activations rather than for their effect.
vs All, Late Game

Role Mistakes to Avoid

  1. 01Deploying Monument into open blue mana. They have It'll Quench Ya or Annul. Wait until they're tapped or at sorcery speed. Monument into a counter is a {3} wasted resource and sets your engine back two turns.
  2. 02Using Monument's damage mode when they're at full life. 3 damage at 20 life does almost nothing. Use Treasure or Draw mode when they're above 12. Switch to Damage only when you can see a kill within 2-3 turns.
  3. 03Casting all Lessons early just for GY count. Firebending Lesson and Combustion Technique are real removal spells. Sometimes holding them as interaction is better than binning them for a {1} discount on turn 2. Read what your opponent is doing before firing off all your removal.
  4. 04Leveling Artist's Talent into open countermagic. Leveling is a sorcery-speed action. Level 1 into sorcery-speed blue mana = your Talent gets Annulled for {U}. Level up EOT or when they're tapped out.

Role-Switching Signals

Switch to Kill Mode When
They're at 12 or Below With Monument Active
Three Monument activations at 3 damage each = 9 damage. From 12 life, two full turns of three activations each = dead. Switch ALL Monument modes to Damage once they're at 12. Don't take Treasure or Draw — every activation must deal damage until they're dead.
Stay Reactive When
Aggro Has Early Board Presence
When Boros Aggro or Izzet Prowess has a 4-6 power board by T3, don't try to set up your engine. Use your Lesson spells as removal (Firebending Lesson kills 2-toughness creatures for free), use Iroh's Demonstration to sweep their board, and only deploy Monument when the board is stabilized.

HEURISTICS

"Internalize these before round 1."
  1. 01T1 Gran-Gran always, on the play. Gran-Gran on T1 means full cost reduction by T3. Every other T1 play delays your engine by a full turn cycle.
  2. 02Gran-Gran reduces cost by {1} per DIFFERENTLY named Lesson. 4 copies of Firebending Lesson in GY = {1} reduction, not {4}. Sequence Abandon Attachments T2 to bin 2 different Lessons immediately.
  3. 03Accumulate Wisdom draws 3 cards for free when the engine is live. With Gran-Gran + Artist's Talent (Level 2) + 3 Lessons in GY, Wisdom costs {0}. This is Ancestral Recall. Use it freely — you have 4 copies.
  4. 04Monument needs to be tapped as an activated ability — Artist's Talent gives you the discard. Artist's Talent Level 1 says "when you cast a spell, discard a card." That discard fuels Monument's activation (you tap Monument and spend a discard to activate it). The sequence is: cast spell → Talent triggers (discard) → tap Monument → choose mode.
  5. 05Use Monument's Treasure mode when you need mana, Damage when they're at 12 or below. Never use Damage mode at full life — it takes 7 activations to kill from 20. Use Treasure to chain more spells, use Damage only in the final 2-turn window.
  6. 06Hold It'll Quench Ya for Monument resolution — not for their removal spells. Letting them Annul your Artist's Talent is painful but survivable. Letting them counter Monument wastes your {3} mana AND sets back your entire engine. Protect Monument at all costs.
  7. 07Boomerang Basics returning Monument makes it a new object. If they target Monument with removal, respond with Boomerang Basics — return Monument to hand. The removal spell fizzles (no target). Redeploy Monument next turn. This requires having {1}{U} available.
  8. 08Iroh's Demonstration deals damage to EACH creature equal to Lessons in GY. With 5 Lessons in GY, Iroh's deals 5 to every creature. This is a full board wipe against Bant Rhythm and token strategies. Save it for maximum impact — don't fire it when they have 1 creature when you need it as a sweeper on turn 5.
  9. 09Soul-Guide Lantern exiles their GY in the mirror — all Lesson discounts disappear. Their Gran-Gran loses all discount, their Accumulate Wisdom draws 1 instead of 3. This is game-defining for {1} mana in the mirror. Deploy it T1 or T2 before they have 3 Lessons in GY.
  10. 10Don't level Artist's Talent into open blue mana. Leveling is a sorcery-speed action. Level on their end step when they tap out, not during your main phase against a player holding {U}.
  11. 11Agna Qel'a turns extra lands into discard fuel. When you have lands to spare in the late game, Agna Qel'a converts them to {U}{U} that can also trigger Monument activations via the discard. Never discard Agna Qel'a to Monument — keep it as a mana source and land converter.
  12. 12Quantum Riddler in the SB needs 2+ instants/sorceries in GY to draw. You hit this condition by T3 trivially. Once active, Riddler attacks = surveil 2 + draw 1 every turn. Against control where games go long, Riddler generates more card advantage per turn than any other threat you have.

Register Conditions

Register When
Field is Creatures and Midrange
Izzet Lessons is built to beat creature decks — your 30+ noncreature spells make their removal dead. Be Cautious When

In a field heavy with Izzet Lessons mirrors and Jeskai Control, both of which can answer Monument efficiently, your SB needs to be heavily skewed toward the mirror and counter-heavy configurations. This is also when Quantum Riddler becomes more important than Pyroclasm in the 15.
