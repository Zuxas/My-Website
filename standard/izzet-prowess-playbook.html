Chapter 01

Deck
Overview

"Cast spells. Pump creatures. Kill before they stabilize. Every cantrip is a free attack boost — cast them all before you declare attackers."

You are playing the second most powerful deck in Standard at 59.2% win rate across 18,000 BO3 games. Your plan is simple and your execution needs to be tight: deploy a threat on T1, build your engine on T2, and start killing on T3 before opponents find their footing. You generate more damage per mana than almost anything in the format and you punish any opponent who gives you a free turn.

Your two primary threats operate differently and you need both. Slickshot Show-Off flies — it dodges every ground blocker in the format, gets +2/+0 per noncreature spell (a 7/2 flier after three cantrips), and its Plot ability lets you preload it into exile and drop it with haste at the exact moment the opponent taps out. Elusive Otter is your evasive threat on the ground — prowess makes it grow, and creatures with lower power can't block it, which in practice means it becomes unblockable once it reaches 2+ power since most 1-drops have power 1. Between the two, you have a flying threat and an evasive ground threat that together demand two different forms of interaction.

Stormchaser's Talent is the engine that turns your cantrips into a board. Level 1 creates an Otter immediately. Level 3 creates an Otter every time you cast an instant or sorcery. With Level 3 active and three cantrips in hand, you're creating three prowess Otters in one turn. Your 16 cantrips (Opt, Sleight of Hand, Stock Up, Boomerang Basics × 4 each) are what powers all of this — they're not just card selection, they're the fuel that makes every threat you control into a threat worth killing immediately.

Your Three Kill Speeds

Fastest — Turn 3
Talent Level 3 + Cantrip Chain
T1 Otter. T2 Stormchaser's Talent (Level 1 creates a second Otter immediately). T3 Level up Talent to 3, cast 3 cantrips — each creates a third Otter + pumps all prowess creatures +1/+1. You now have 4 creatures that are all 4/4+. This sequence requires holding Talent leveling for T3 and having 3 cantrips available. Wins before removal can line up.
Standard — Turns 4–5
Slickshot Plot + Burn Finish
T1 Otter or Slickshot (plot). T2 Talent. T3 cast plotted Slickshot with haste, chain 2-3 cantrips. Slickshot becomes a 5-7/2 flier on T3. Attack for 5-7 flying. T4 they're at 11-13, Burst Lightning + Sear closes it. The Plot line is specifically designed for the moment they tap out to cast their T3 play — drop Slickshot hasted right over their defenses.
Grind — Turns 6–8
Eddymurk Crab + Ral
When the game goes long, you flip to a different gear. With 5+ spells in your GY, Eddymurk Crab costs {U} — a free 5/5 that taps their two biggest blockers on entry. Ral, Crackling Wit from the SB gains loyalty from every cantrip you cast — with 3 cantrips he goes from 4 to 7 loyalty in one turn, generates Otters, and approaches his -10 storm emblem fast. The grind plan is very real.

Card Notes

The Primary Threat
Slickshot Show-Off
{R} 1/2 Bird Wizard. Flying, haste. Whenever you cast a noncreature spell, this creature gets +2/+0 until EOT. Plot {1}{R}: exile from hand, cast free on a later turn (sorcery speed). The plot ability is the key threat — it converts a hand card into surprise T3 damage. A Slickshot with 3 cantrips in a turn is a 7/2 flier dealing 7 flying damage. Irrelevant unless killed immediately, and it has haste so it attacks the turn it resolves.
The Evasive Clock
Elusive Otter
{U} 1/1 Otter. Prowess. Creatures with power less than this creature's power can't block it. Adventure — Grove's Bounty {X}{G}: distribute X +1/+1 counters among any number of target creatures you control. The prowess pumping makes Otter effectively unblockable once it reaches 2+ power — most creatures in the format have power 1. Multiversal Passage and Willowrush Verge provide {G} access for the adventure mode to permanently buff creatures with counters rather than the temporary prowess pump.
The Engine
Stormchaser's Talent
{U} Enchantment — Class. Level 1 (base): creates 1/1 Otter token with prowess. {3}{U}: Level 2 — returns an instant/sorcery from GY to hand. {5}{U}: Level 3 — creates 1/1 Otter per instant/sorcery you cast. Level 1 on T1 (with an Otter token) into T2 cantrips means every spell pumps two prowess creatures. Level 3 mid-game creates a token factory that's nearly impossible to race. Never tap out to level it when they have countermagic; do it EOT with mana up.
The Late-Game Closer
Eddymurk Crab
{4}{U} 5/5 Flash Elemental Crab. Costs {1} less per instant/sorcery in GY. Enters tapped if not your turn. ETB: tap up to two target creatures. With 4+ spells in GY: free 5/5. With 6+ spells in GY: free 5/5 that taps their two best blockers on entry. Used defensively (flash block on opponent's turn) or offensively (flash in on their end step after clearing blockers). The ETB tapping combos with plotted Slickshot — tap their blockers, then Slickshot comes down with haste and flies for lethal.
The Cantrip Core
Opt / Sleight / Stock Up
Opt: {U} instant scry 1 draw 1. Sleight of Hand: {U} sorcery look at top 2 pick 1. Stock Up: {1}{U} sorcery look at top 5 put 2 in hand rest on bottom. All 12 copies are here primarily as prowess triggers, secondarily as card selection. The key difference: Opt at instant speed can be held for their turn to pump prowess during their attack step, or to counter the damage with Slickshot mid-combat. Cast Opt before attacking to get +1/+1 on all prowess creatures in combat, then let them declare blocks.
The Burn Package
Burst Lightning / Sear
Burst Lightning: {R} instant — 2 damage, or 4 damage with kicker {4}. Sear: {1}{R} instant — 4 damage to creature or planeswalker. Primary role: clear blockers and pump prowess simultaneously. Kill their creature + give Slickshot +2/+0 in one spell. Sear at 4 damage handles most relevant threats. Kicker Burst Lightning on their end step clears a blocker before your attack step. These are reach spells too — when they're at low life, Burst Lightning to face closes games that stall.
The Lesson Staple
Boomerang Basics
{1}{U} Sorcery — Lesson. Return target nonland permanent to owner's hand. If you controlled it, draw a card. When used on your own permanent: bounce your Otter back to hand after it attacked, draw a card, and re-cast it to trigger Talent's level 3 token again. When used on opponents': return their key blocker, enchantment, or threat. The Lesson subtype means it can be fetched from outside the game by Learn effects. Four copies ensures it's always available as both interaction and card advantage.
The Wild Card
Wild Ride / Secret Identity
Wild Ride: {R} sorcery — target creature gets +3/+0 and haste until EOT. Harmonize {4}{R} from GY. Gives Slickshot or Otter a surprise power boost mid-combat or from nowhere. Secret Identity: {U} instant — Conceal mode makes creature 1/1 with hexproof (protect from removal), Reveal mode makes it 3/4 with flying and vigilance (combat threat). Both one-ofs serve as flexible combat tricks that also trigger prowess and fill the GY for Eddymurk Crab cost reduction.
Chapter 03

Gameplan

"Cast your noncreature spells BEFORE attacking. Every spell is a free +1/+1 or +2/+0 in combat."

Turn-by-Turn Framework

Turns 1–2: Establish
Creature + Engine
T1: Elusive Otter or Slickshot Show-Off (plot if you want surprise T3 haste). T2: Stormchaser's Talent (creates immediate 1/1 Otter). Now 2 prowess creatures in play before T3. Or: T1 Otter, T2 3x cantrips — Otter becomes a 4/4 that can't be blocked by most things in the format and swings for 4. The T2 cantrip blitz is one of their fastest openers.
Turns 3–4: Execute
Spell Chain
Main phase: cast all cantrips in hand to pump creatures before combat. Opt, Sleight, Boomerang Basics all fire before you declare attackers. Each spell adds +2/+0 to Slickshot, +1/+1 to Otters. Attack. Then post-combat, cast removal to clear remaining blockers for next turn. By T4 they should be at 6-8 life with a Slickshot threatening lethal through any blocker.
Turns 5+: Close
Burn to Face
At 8 life or less, all burn spells become face damage. Burst Lightning = 2 to face (or 4 kicked). Sear to face. A hand of 3 burn spells and 1 cantrip is usually 7+ damage from nowhere. Wild Ride targets a creature with trample — +3/+0 then attacks for lethal. Eddymurk Crab at this point taps their blockers so your damaged Slickshot can finish.
Your Spell Sequencing
When to Cast What
Your decision before every attack: which spells are you casting before declares, during combat, and after? Before attacks: cantrips to pump Swiftspear. During combat: Burst Lightning in response to a blocker (Swiftspear pumps to 4/4 with power higher than their blocker). After combat: removal or another cantrip to grow DRC for next turn. You never dump your hand randomly — you sequence spells for maximum combat math.
Your Clock Math
The Kill Turn
Your T4 kill from a normal curve: T1 Swiftspear (2 damage). T2 cantrip → cantrip → Swiftspear attacks as 3/3 (5 total). T3 cast 3 spells → Swiftspear 5/5 attacks (10 total). T4 cast 2 spells + Burst Lightning kicked (4) → Swiftspear attacks for 4 (14) + 4 burn = 18. You know this progression in your sleep. Any deviation from the goldfish tells you when to evaluate closing vs extending the game.
Your DRC Line
Dreadhorde Arcanist Value
Your T2-T3 DRC line: T2 DRC enters (1/3). T3: DRC attacks → cast a MV ≤ 1 spell from GY for free (the Opt you cantripped T1). You build your GY specifically for DRC attacks — T1 cantrip goes to GY, T2 DRC enters, T3 DRC attacks and recasts T1 cantrip for free. You never exile GY cards unnecessarily when DRC is in play or in hand. Every cantrip in your GY is a free spell on DRC's attack.
Your Counter Timing
Holding Spell Pierce
Your Spell Pierce sits in hand until their most important noncreature spell. You don't Pierce their cantrips — you Pierce their board wipe (Anger of the Gods), their Narset (shuts off your draws), or their key payoff (Glorious Sunrise). Identifying the exact spell you're holding Pierce for is a pre-game decision, not an in-game reaction. Know your Pierce target before game 1 starts.

The Critical Sequencing Rule

Always cast noncreature spells before combat. This is the most important rule in the deck and the one players forget most. Prowess triggers happen "until end of turn" — but you want the pumped power for THAT attack. If you cast Opt after attacking, your Otter is a 1/1 when it attacks instead of a 3/3. The sequence: main phase → cast all cheap spells → declare attackers with pumped power. Then in post-combat main, cast anything else.

Exception: Hold one Opt/Sleight for their end step to also pump during their attack if you're on defense. Or hold instant-speed spells for their turn to force through damage from Slickshot if they tap low.

The GY Acceleration Engine
Fill
Cantrip Chain

Role Assignment

"We are more aggressive than we are controlling. Prioritize applying pressure before they stabilize."

Aggressor
You are almost always the beatdown player. Apply pressure from T1 and force them to react. Your creatures grow faster than they can remove them when you chain spells. Never give them a free turn to set up their value engine.
vs All opponents except faster aggro
Secondary Role
Tempo Disruptor
Get Out counter + bounce, Spell Pierce in SB, Into the Flood Maw — disrupt their key spell while maintaining pressure. Don't go fully control; maintain your clock while disrupting theirs. Remove one key piece that enables their plan.
vs Control, Combo

Burn Race

Dimir MidrangeRaceFavorable (56%)Stormchaser's Talent is unkillable for them main deck. Land it and they're on a losing game plan.

Your best midrange matchup. Izzet Prowess MirrorTempoEvenTake less damage than you deal. Eddymurk Crab is your mirror breaker. Bounce your own Talent tokens to cut their clock.

The mirror is pure life total management. Both players kill at approximately the same speed. The player who takes more damage loses. This sounds simple but the decision density is high: every time you consider attacking, calculate if you'll be at a lower life total than them if they attack back equivalently. The player who blinks and goes wide first usually dies first.

The Crab advantage: Eddymurk Crab is the most important card in the mirror. A 5/5 that doesn't die to Burst Lightning (4 damage) or Sear (4 damage) and taps their two biggest creatures on entry changes the board state completely. Flash it in on their end step after they've committed to casting cantrips — they've added spells to their GY (making your Crab cheaper) and now their board is tapped. Your T5 attack with Crab + Slickshot is nearly unblockable.

The Boomerang Basics bounce: You can bounce your own Stormchaser's Talent token with Boomerang Basics — draw a card, remove one of their clock targets. This is correct when they have a large board and you need to reduce their attack math. The Otter re-enters from exile when you re-cast it, triggering Talent Level 3 again for a fresh 1/1. Net result: you drew a card and generated a new token that hasn't taken damage.

Turn-by-turn plan: T1 Otter or Slickshot (don't plot T1 — deploy the threat immediately). T2 Talent. T3 cast cantrips before attacking — track their life total carefully. Never attack if doing so puts you at a life total where their next swing kills you. Hold Burst Lightning for their Slickshot before it attacks (2 damage is enough), not for face damage. T4-5 Eddymurk Crab — this is when you win the mirror.

Jeskai ControlTempoFavorableStormchaser's Talent wins this matchup. Deploy it EOT when they tap out. Ral charges faster than they can sweep.

You are favored. Control has answers to individual threats but cannot efficiently handle Stormchaser's Talent generating tokens continuously. Inevitable Defeat can exile Talent (it's a nonland permanent) but it costs 4 mana, is sorcery speed... wait, Inevitable Defeat is an instant. Still, at {2}{B}{W} they need specific mana open. Your goal: put them in a position where they must answer Talent but can't afford to leave up {2}{B}{W} every turn while you're generating damage.

By the time they can handle Ral, he's at 7-8 loyalty and their hand has been drained trying to answer your other threats. Control can't efficiently handle a planeswalker that charges itself and generates tokens simultaneously while you're casting free card draw spells.

Timing rule: Deploy Stormchaser's Talent EOT when they're tapped, not during your main phase when they have {U}{W} up for No More Lies. Never walk Talent into open blue-white mana. The Pre-Attack Cantrip Rule

This is the single most important execution point in the deck and the most common mistake. Every noncreature spell you cast pumps prowess creatures until end of turn. That pump only matters during combat — which means you must cast all your cantrips in your main phase BEFORE declaring attackers. A 1/1 Otter that attacks as a 4/4 after three cantrips deals 4 damage instead of 1. If you cast those same cantrips after combat, the Otter dealt 1.

Correct Sequence
Main → Cantrips → Attack
Main phase: Cast Opt (Otter +1/+1 until EOT), cast Sleight of Hand (Otter +1/+1), cast Burst Lightning on their blocker (Otter +1/+1 + removes blocker). Now Otter is 4/4. Declare Otter as attacker — it can't be blocked by anything with 3 or less power. Deal 4 damage. Post-combat: cast anything left.
Correct Exception
Hold Opt for Their End Step
Opt at instant speed on THEIR end step pumps your creatures for blocks on their attack — or more importantly, it's sometimes correct to pass your turn with mana open, let them declare attackers, and then cast Opt during their attack step to pump your blocker to survive a trade. The flexibility of instant-speed cantrips is a real tool — use it deliberately, not just as reflexive draw.

The Plot Timing Window

Slickshot Show-Off's Plot ability ({1}{R}: exile from hand, cast for free on a later turn, sorcery speed) is your primary way around countermagic and hand disruption. The decision of WHEN to plot is matchup-dependent:

vs Dimir
Plot T2, Drop T3 on Bat
Deep-Cavern Bat strips a card from your hand when it enters. Plot Slickshot on T2 before they can Bat it — exiled cards can't be stripped. When they tap for Kaito T3 and Bat enters, your hand is safe and Slickshot is ready. The moment they tap for any play, drop Slickshot with haste before they can respond with sorcery-speed removal.
vs Control
Plot, Then Wait for Their Tap-Out
Control holds {U}{W} for No More Lies every turn. Plot Slickshot T2. Then wait. When they spend their mana on Consult the Star Charts, Lightning Helix, or Inevitable Defeat — that's your window. Drop Slickshot with haste before they untap. It enters, attacks, and they're answering a 1/2 flier instead of developing their game plan.
vs Aggro
Deploy Immediately, Don't Plot
Against Boros Aggro and Mono-White, the game is decided by turns 4-5. You don't have time to wait for the perfect window — plot costs a full turn of not applying pressure. Against aggro, cast Slickshot normally ({R}) and attack immediately. The one exception: plot if you're on the draw and they're at T3 already threatening lethal — then preserve the burst option for T4.

Eddymurk Crab Flash Timing

Eddymurk Crab is a 5/5 that enters tapped on your opponent's turn, but costs {1} less per instant/sorcery in your GY. With 4+ spells in GY it's {U}. With 6+ spells it's free. The ETB taps up to two targets. The timing windows matter:

Best Window: Their End Step
Tap Their Board, Attack Free
Flash Crab on their end step after they've passed priority and can't respond with another flash creature. ETB taps their two biggest creatures. You untap with Crab (which untaps on your turn since it only enters tapped on their turn) + your full board. Attack for free — their board is tapped. This sequence on T5 when Crab is free or near-free is often your kill in the matchups that go long.
Defensive Window: Their Attack Step
After Attackers Declared, Tap Non-Attackers
After they declare attackers, flash Crab. ETB taps their non-attackers — they can't add more blockers on this attack, can't tap mana from non-land sources, and their board is compromised for the counterattack. In the Dimir matchup this taps Floodpits Drowner before it can tap your attackers. In Boros this taps Ojer Axonil and Sharpshooter simultaneously, cutting off their noncombat damage.

Boomerang Basics — Both Modes

Boomerang Basics (Lesson sorcery: return target nonland permanent to owner's hand; if you controlled it, draw a card) has an offensive mode and a self-bounce mode that are both essential:

Offensive Mode
Bounce Their Key Permanent
Bounce Sapling Nursery (enchantment — they spent 4+ mana, it goes back to hand, no tokens this turn). Bounce Earthbender Ascension (resets quest counters to 0). Bounce Stormchaser's Talent in the mirror (removes a token generator AND draws you a card). Bounce Enduring Curiosity (stops their draw engine). The timing: bounce EOT when they're tapped and can't recast immediately.
Self-Bounce Mode
Return Your Own Otter, Draw a Card
Return your own Elusive Otter that has +1/+1 counters — draw a card, exile Otter, re-cast it from exile (which triggers Talent Level 3 for a new 1/1 Otter), then adventure Grove's Bounty for permanent counters. This sequence: costs 1 card (Boomerang Basics), generates 1 new Otter token (Talent Level 3), produces permanent counters on your creatures, and draws you a card. Net: one card in, two permanents out, hand size maintained.

Get Out — Full Application Map

Get Out ({1}{U} instant) has two modes: counter target creature or enchantment spell, OR return 1-2 target creatures/enchantments you own to your hand. Both modes are strong in different situations:

Counter Mode
Stop the Key Spell
Counter targets: Stormchaser's Talent in the mirror (they don't get Level 1 Otter), Sapling Nursery vs Mono-Green, Caretaker's Talent vs Mono-White, Enduring Curiosity vs Dimir, Ojer Axonil vs Boros. The {1}{U} cost is efficient for a hard counter on creatures and enchantments specifically — most of the deck-defining spells in Standard are enchantments or key creatures.
Return Mode
Save Your Permanents or Bounce Theirs
Return YOUR permanents: save a Stormchaser's Talent from being exiled by Seam Rip (bounce it before Seam Rip exiles it), save an Otter from being killed (bounce in response to removal). Return THEIR permanents: bounce their Earthbender Ascension (resets quest counters), bounce their key creature during combat (they lose a blocker). Remember: return mode says "1-2 target creatures/enchantments you own" — only your permanents for the return-to-hand mode.

Oracle-Verified Edge Cases

Slickshot Show-Off Gets +2/+0 Per Noncreature, Not +1/+1
Slickshot's trigger is NOT prowess — it gives +2/+0, not +1/+1. It stacks: 3 spells = +6/+0 until EOT. A 3/1 flying haste that becomes a 9/1 after 3 spells. It dies to any 1-toughness removal regardless of pump. The +2/+0 matters for lethal math: Slickshot threatens more damage per spell than standard prowess creatures.
Heartfire Hero — Valiant Triggers Only ONCE Per Turn
"Whenever this creature becomes the target of a spell or ability you control for the FIRST time each turn." Second and third targeting spells don't trigger valiant. The +1/+1 counter is permanent (unlike prowess). The death trigger deals damage equal to POWER (including counters). A Hero with 3 counters that dies deals 4 damage.
Leyline of Resonance — Copies Trigger Prowess Again
"Whenever you cast an instant or sorcery that targets only a single creature you control, copy that spell." The COPY triggers prowess/Slickshot again (it's a new spell on the stack). Cast Burst Lightning targeting Swiftspear → Leyline copies → Swiftspear gets prowess from original AND copy = +2/+2 total. Careful: the copy also targets your creature — redirect to opponent's creature or face if possible.
Plot — Cast from Exile Triggers Prowess
Slickshot's Plot {1}{R}: exile from hand, cast later as sorcery (no mana cost). Casting from exile IS casting — triggers prowess, Slickshot's +2/+0, and all "when you cast" effects. Plot lets you bank a creature during a turn you can't use it, then deploy for free later with haste + prowess chain.
Screaming Nemesis — "Can't Gain Life" Is Permanent for the Game
"If a player is dealt damage this way, they can't gain life for the rest of the game." This lasts the ENTIRE GAME — not until end of turn. Even if Nemesis leaves play, the "can't gain life" persists. One Nemesis redirect shuts off all lifegain for the opponent permanently.

Heuristics

  1. 01
    Cast all noncreature spells before declaring attackers. Prowess and Slickshot's bonus apply "until end of turn" — but they must be pumped before combat to matter. An Otter that attacks as a 1/1 instead of a 4/4 because you forgot to cast your cantrips first is one of the most common and costly mistakes in this deck.
  2. 02
    Plot Slickshot when they have counterspell mana open, cast it when they're tapped. Plot {1}{R} on T2 exiles Slickshot safely. On T3 when they tap for Kaito or Tidebinder, cast Slickshot free with haste — it attacks immediately. The plot ability specifically enables playing around countermagic by decoupling "pay the cost" from "deploy the threat."
  3. 03
    Stormchaser's Talent is a Class enchantment — leveling up is a sorcery-speed action. Don't try to level up Talent into open countermagic. Level it on your own turn or EOT in response to their tap-out. Level 1 to 2 and 2 to 3 are both sorcery-speed activations. Many players forget this and try to level mid-combat.
  4. 04
    Elusive Otter becomes unblockable once it has more power than any potential blocker. At 2 power: can't be blocked by 1-power creatures (most 1-drops). At 3 power: can't be blocked by 2-power creatures. At 5+ power: effectively unblockable against most boards. Prioritize casting spells before the Otter attacks rather than after — each spell pumps it permanently.
  5. 05
    Eddymurk Crab ETB taps up to two creatures — use it offensively to clear blockers. Flash Crab in on their end step when they tap to cast a spell. Tap their two best blockers. Then your Slickshot (already in play with haste from a previous turn) connects for lethal. The ETB trigger resolves before you go to your untap step — their creatures stay tapped through your attack.
  6. 06
    Boomerang Basics returning your own permanent draws a card — use it on your own Otter. Return your Otter with 4 counters to hand → draw a card → replay Otter from exile (triggers Talent Level 3 for another 1/1 Otter) → adventure Grove's Bounty for X counters. This sequence generates a card, a body, and permanent counters off one card. Not just for bouncing opponents' permanents.
  7. 07
    Ral, Crackling Wit gains loyalty from every noncreature spell — deploy him when you have a full hand of cantrips. A turn-4 Ral into 3 cantrips gives him 3 loyalty from the passive, +1 for one more Otter, and now he's at 5 loyalty. Control can't spend a counterspell on every cantrip that charges him. Deploy Ral when you have 4+ cantrips in hand to immediately make him difficult to remove efficiently.
  8. 08
    In the mirror, take less damage than you deal — not more damage faster. Both players kill around turn 4-5. The player at 7 life when the final attack happens loses. Track both life totals every combat and calculate: if they attack for X and you attack for Y, who dies first? Blocking profitably is better than attacking aggressively when you're the one who falls to 0 first.
  9. 09
    Wild Ride's Harmonize cost {4}{R} from GY — remember it can be used as a second copy. Wild Ride exiles itself when harmonized. But if you cast it from hand and it goes to GY normally, you can pay {4}{R} tapping a creature to cast it again. In late-game grind scenarios where you need one more pump to close, the Harmonize mode on Wild Ride is an often-forgotten source of extra damage.

Eddymurk Crab: 2 vs 3 copies
Meta dependent
2 is standard in the LucasG1ggs list. Run 3 in control-heavy metas where the longer game plays out more — Crab is your best closer against control. Run 2 or trim to 1 in aggro-heavy metas where you need more 1-drops and faster kills. Crab is a dead draw in aggro mirrors early.

20-Minute Crash Course

If you're sitting down in 20 minutes, run through this in order:

  • Mulligan rule: Keep any 7 with Monastery Swiftspear + a cantrip OR Slickshot Show-Off + 2 mana. Never keep a hand without at least one early threat.
  • Slickshot rule: Slickshot Show-Off gets +2/+0 for each instant/sorcery you cast on the turn it attacks. Plot it EOT, then cast 3+ spells next turn before attacking. It attacks as a 7/4+ evasion creature.
  • DRC rule: Dreadhorde Arcanist (1/3): when it attacks, cast a spell with MV ≤ DRC's power from GY for free. Keep cheap instants in GY. A 1/3 DRC casts Opt or Bolt from GY when it attacks.
  • Burst Lightning rule: Burst Lightning deals 2 damage normally or 4 if kicked ({2}{R}). Against control: kick it to deal 4 damage to their face or their 4-toughness blocker.
  • Into the Flood Maw rule: Bounces up to two target creatures. Board in vs creature-heavy decks to tempo them out. Never bounce cantrip creatures — bounce their win conditions.
  • Board rule: Spell Pierce ({U}) counters noncreature spells unless they pay {2}. Board in vs Dimir and Control. Maximum value turns 1-4 before they have extra mana. Board it out if the game goes long.
