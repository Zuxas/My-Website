Chapter 01

DECK
IDENTITY

"Your creatures are Elementals and your Elementals care about spells. Every spell you cast does double duty — it's an interaction piece AND a board pump."

Izzet Spellementals was designed by The Boulder team for Pro Tour Lorwyn Eclipsed 2026, where it posted an extremely high win rate against Badgermole Cub strategies. The deck sits at 5.26% of Standard and is the premier Elemental-tribal midrange strategy in the format.

The core engine: Ashling, Rekindled is a legendary Elemental that triggers on ETB — discard a card, if you do draw a card. With Roaming Throne naming Elemental, that trigger fires twice. You discard 2, draw 2, and net cycle your hand completely on a creature that's also a 3/3. When Ashling transforms into Ashling, Rimebound at the beginning of your first main phase (pay {U}), she becomes an even more powerful variant that continues generating value every turn.

Sunderflock is your sweeper — an Elemental that deals damage to each creature equal to something, and costs less per Elemental you control or cast. Flamebraider and Vibrance fuel the Elemental synergy. Wistfulness and Deceit provide interaction. Bounce Off buys tempo. The entire package is built to go wide with Elementals, generate card advantage through Ashling + Throne, and close with a Sunderflock sweep that clears the opponent's board while yours survives.

Why Roaming Throne Doubles Ashling

The Interaction
Roaming Throne: Double Elemental Triggers
Roaming Throne enters and you choose a creature type — choose Elemental. Now whenever an Elemental you control triggers an ability, that ability triggers an additional time. Ashling's "discard a card, draw a card" ETB triggers TWICE → net: discard 2, draw 2. All your Elemental ETBs double. Sunderflock's damage doubles. Flamebraider's triggers double. Roaming Throne effectively makes every Elemental ETB cost-efficient.
The Win Condition
Sunderflock as a Board Wipe
Sunderflock is an Elemental that deals damage to each creature an opponent controls equal to the number of Elementals you control or the number of Elemental spells you've cast this turn. With Roaming Throne doubling the trigger: the damage fires twice. Against go-wide decks, this wipes their entire board while your Elementals survive (they don't deal damage to creatures you control). With 4 Elementals on board, Sunderflock does 4+4 = 8 damage to each of their creatures.

Chapter 03

GAME
FLOW

"T2 Roaming Throne. T3 Ashling (double trigger: discard 2, draw 2). T4 Flamebraider + Wistfulness into board pressure. T5 Sunderflock wipes their board."
Turns 1–2
Set Up the Doubler
T1: Vibrance or Wistfulness to filter/develop. T2: Roaming Throne (choose Elemental). Throne on T2 means every Elemental you cast afterward has doubled triggers. If Throne isn't in hand, deploy Flamebraider or Wistfulness to build board presence. Cavern of Souls naming Elemental ensures Throne and Ashling can't be countered.
Turns 3–4
Ashling Engine Online
T3: Ashling, Rekindled with Throne active → discard 2, draw 2 (double trigger) on ETB. Your hand cycles through itself efficiently. T4: Pay {U} at beginning of first main phase to transform Ashling into Rimebound for additional value. Deploy Flamebraider or more spells to build Elemental count for Sunderflock.
Turns 4–5
Sunderflock Wipe
With 3+ Elementals on board, Sunderflock deals 3+ damage to each creature they control. With Roaming Throne: that trigger fires twice → 6+ damage to each creature. Their board is wiped. Your Elementals survive (Sunderflock targets only opponent's creatures). Attack for lethal on an empty board.
Alternate Lines
Deceit + Beza Grind
When Sunderflock isn't the right play, Deceit provides early interaction (Distress + bounce). Beza, the Bounding Spring generates creatures and life gain on ETB — with Roaming Throne, Beza's Elemental trigger fires twice. Winter Stories discards to generate advantage. This grindy line wins in attrition matchups where sweepers aren't needed.

Mulligan Framework

Keep: Any 7 with Roaming Throne + 2 Elementals + 3 lands. Any 7 with Ashling + 2 lands + any spell. Cavern of Souls hands are stronger keeps — they protect your key creatures from countermagic.

Mulligan: 0-1 lands. 5+ lands. Hands with only Sunderflock (needs other Elementals to matter). Hands with 4+ noncreature spells and no Elementals.

Chapter 04

CORE
ENGINES

"Roaming Throne makes every Elemental ETB happen twice. This is the multiplier that makes the entire deck work."
Engine 1
Ashling + Roaming Throne
Ashling ETB: discard, draw. Throne doubles it: discard 2, draw 2. Net: hand stays the same size while cycling your whole hand. You filter aggressively looking for Sunderflock, lands, or key pieces. Deploy Ashling multiple times (bounce with Bounce Off and redeploy) to cycle repeatedly.
Engine 2
Sunderflock Sweep
Sunderflock deals damage equal to Elementals on board. With Throne: double that. With 4 Elementals: 8 damage to each of their creatures. Their Prowess Otters (1/1-3/3), Badgermole Cubs (2/2), and spiders (1/2) all die. Your Elementals don't. You swing for lethal on an empty board the same turn or next turn.
Engine 3
Vibrance + Wistfulness Flood
Vibrance and Wistfulness are cheap instants/sorceries that draw cards and/or generate card advantage. They fill the graveyard, enable Eclipsed Realms (a land that does something with instants/sorceries in GY), and keep your hand full. Run all 8 copies to maintain card advantage even without Ashling online.
Chapter 05

LINES &
TACTICS

"Name Elemental with Cavern of Souls before casting Ashling or Roaming Throne. They cannot be countered on Cavern mana."
Line 01 — Cavern of Souls Protects Your Key Pieces
Cavern of Souls lets you cast creatures of the chosen type without them being countered. Name Elemental — now Ashling and Sunderflock and Flamebraider cannot be countered if cast off Cavern mana. This is critical vs Izzet Lessons (It'll Quench Ya) and Jeskai Control (No More Lies). Roaming Throne is an artifact, not a creature — it CAN be countered even off Cavern. Protect Throne with Bounce Off or Spell Snare (SB).
Line 02 — Sunderflock Timing
Sunderflock's damage to each creature fires at cast time (it's a triggered ability on enter, or the spell itself if it's a sorcery). With Roaming Throne active: the damage trigger fires twice. Count your Elementals before casting Sunderflock — you need enough to kill what they have. With 3 Elementals: 6 damage to each of their creatures (Throne doubles 3 → 6). Flamebraider and Ashling count. Roaming Throne does NOT count (it's not an Elemental). Cast Sunderflock after all your Elementals are deployed, not before.
Line 03 — Bounce Off Protects Ashling for Multiple ETB Triggers
Bounce Off ({1}{U}: return target creature to its owner's hand; if that creature is legendary, draw a card) can target your own Ashling. Return Ashling to hand → draw a card (Ashling is legendary) → re-cast Ashling → ETB fires again (discard 2, draw 2 with Throne). Total value from one Bounce Off cycle: return Ashling + draw 1 + discard 2 draw 2 = cycle 2 cards and draw 1. Use this when Ashling has taken damage and is about to die, or when you need to find Sunderflock urgently.
Chapter 06

ROLE
ASSIGNMENT

"You're always the midrange deck. You don't rush, you don't purely control — you set up and then end the game on one decisive turn."
Primary
Board Control Then Swing
Build your Elemental board, double triggers with Throne, wipe their board with Sunderflock, attack into empty opposition. This is the main win pattern. You're the control player in aggro matchups and the aggro player against control.
Secondary
Ashling Grind
When Sunderflock isn't right, grind with Ashling + Throne card advantage. Cycle your hand repeatedly, find your best cards, outvalue their engine. Beza and Winternight Stories contribute to the grind. You win through card quantity.
Tertiary
Tempo Through Deceit
Strip their key spell with Deceit (Distress mode) or bounce their key permanent (bounce mode for MV ≤ 2 permanents). Use Bounce Off for tempo. Deploy Sunderflock as a threat they have to answer. Sometimes you win through tempo rather than a clean combo.

Chapter 09

HEURISTICS

"Name Elemental on Cavern every single game. Not naming Elemental is the most common mistake in this deck."
  1. 01Always name Elemental on Cavern of Souls. Ashling, Hearth Elemental, Sunderflock, and Flamebraider are all Elementals. Cavern makes them uncounterable when cast off its mana. This is the deck's primary way around countermagic. Never name a different creature type.
  2. 02Roaming Throne is an artifact — Cavern does NOT protect it. Cast Throne only when they have 3 or fewer mana open (can't pay for No More Lies or No More Lies {1}{U}{W}). Bounce Off can protect Throne in response to a counter. Throne resolving is your highest-priority action.
  3. 03Count your Elementals before casting Sunderflock. Sunderflock deals damage equal to the number of Elementals you control to each creature. With Roaming Throne, it doubles: 3 Elementals = 6 damage each. A Flamebraider (2/2), Hearth Elemental (3/4), Ashling (3/3) in play = 9 damage to each creature with Throne. Math it out before casting.
  4. 04Hearth Elemental costs 1 less per instant/sorcery in your GY — like Eddymurk Crab. With 3 spells in GY: Hearth costs {1}{R}. With 5 spells: {R}. Prioritize filling your GY with cheap spells in the first 3 turns to reduce Hearth's cost. It arrives as a 3/4 with haste — that haste matters for racing.
  5. 05Bounce Off on your own Ashling draws a card (Ashling is legendary). Return Ashling → draw a card → recast → ETB triggers again (discard 2 draw 2 with Throne). Use this when Ashling is damaged, or when you need a specific card urgently from a fresh ETB trigger cycle.
  6. 06Ashling's discard-draw on ETB happens BEFORE you draw. Discard 2 cards first, then draw 2. You can't draw to replace what you just discarded. Choose carefully what to pitch — your GY cards matter less than your hand cards in most situations.
  7. 07Flamebraider creates 1/1 Elemental tokens when it attacks — those tokens count for Sunderflock. If Flamebraider attacks and creates 2 tokens, you now have 2 more Elementals. Cast Sunderflock AFTER Flamebraider's attack trigger resolves for maximum damage. This is the "Sunderflock after combat" sequencing.
  8. 08Wistfulness draws a card when you cast another Elemental. The draw trigger fires when you cast — not when the Elemental enters the battlefield. Bounce Off'ing your own Elemental back to hand and recasting it triggers Wistfulness again. Each Elemental you cast while Wistfulness is in play draws a card.
  9. 09Eddymurk Crab at flash speed on THEIR end step taps two creatures — attack with your whole board next turn. Don't cast Crab during your main phase unless lethal matters. The correct window is their end step after they pass priority. The tapped creatures stay tapped through your full attack.
  10. 10Deceit's Distress mode (discard mode) is sorcery speed. You can't cast it at instant speed to strip a card. Cast it on your T2 to strip their key piece before they cast it. Don't hold it hoping for a perfect instant-speed discard window — use it proactively on the spell you're most afraid of.
Chapter 10

TUNING
NOTES

"4 Cavern is non-negotiable. 4 Roaming Throne is non-negotiable. Everything else is flex."

Lock Cards — Never Cut

Lock
4 Cavern of Souls + 4 Roaming Throne
These are why you play Spellementals over other Elemental strategies. Cavern makes your threats uncounterable. Throne doubles every ETB trigger. The deck doesn't function without both at 4 copies. Any list that cuts either is a different deck.
Lock
4 Ashling, Rekindled + 4 Sunderflock
Ashling is the engine (ETB: discard 2, draw 2 with Throne). Sunderflock is the board wipe and finisher (doubles with Throne). These are your 8 power cards. Full playset of both is mandatory.

Flex Slots

2 copies — adjust up/down
Winternight Stories
Excellent in fair matchups, slow against aggro. Run 2-3 in control-heavy metas, 1-2 in aggro-heavy fields. The passive card advantage over multiple turns outgrows sweepers.
1-2 copies
Bounce Off
Protects Ashling (draw a card) and provides tempo. Run 2 in metas with high-value permanents to bounce (Sapling Nursery, Roaming Throne in mirror). 1 is fine in aggro-heavy fields.
0-1 copy
Glacial Dragonhunt
Ground creature sweeper with flying exemption. Cut in almost every non-aggro meta. The only reason to run it: a field of Mono-Green Landfall and Boros Aggro where you need another sweeper.

When to Register

Register When
Midrange + Control Are Popular
Cavern of Souls is at maximum value against Dimir Midrange and Jeskai Control — both rely heavily on counterspells. If the field is these two archetypes plus fair creature decks, Spellementals is well-positioned with free-casting Elementals and Throne doubling everything.
Avoid When
Izzet Prowess Is Dominant
Your speed disadvantage vs Prowess means a Prowess-heavy field is rough. If more than 15% of the expected field is Prowess, consider Mono-Green Landfall or Izzet Lessons which have better Prowess matchups.

Night Before Checklist

Know Cold
Sunderflock Damage Math
3 Elementals + Throne = 6 damage each. 4 Elementals + Throne = 8 damage each. 2 Elementals + Throne = 4 damage each (kills most 4-toughness creatures with a Burst Lightning follow-up). Know these by reflex before sitting down.
Know Cold
Cavern Only Protects Creature Spells
Roaming Throne is an artifact spell — casting it off Cavern mana does NOT make it uncounterable (Cavern only applies to creature spells of the named type). Cast Throne with open mana to protect it. Bounce Off in response to their counter saves it.
Verify
Hearth Elemental Cost Reduction
Hearth Elemental costs {1} less for each instant/sorcery in your GY. At 4 spells in GY it's {R} — essentially free. Count your GY spells before every main phase to know what Hearth currently costs. Missing a free 3/4 with haste is a significant play error.
Confirm
SB for Expected Meta
Check the current meta breakdown. Soul-Guide Lantern in against every GY deck. Annul and Disdainful Stroke in against control. Pyroclasm and Abrade in against aggro. Confirm your 15 before heading out.

Round 1 Tells

T1 land tells: Steam Vents or Spirebluff Canal = Izzet something (Prowess, Lessons, or mirror) — name Elemental on Cavern, hold interaction. Forest or Fabled Passage = likely Mono-Green — get Roaming Throne down fast. Island or Watery Grave = Dimir — play your Cavern game freely.
