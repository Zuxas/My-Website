About Zuxas

I'm Jermey Wallace, better known online as Zuxas. I'm a competitive Magic: The Gathering player, the team captain of Team Resolve, and someone who loves strategy, improvement, and building systems that help people perform at a higher level.

Background

I served in the U.S. Navy from 2012 to 2018 as a Fire Controlman, working with TAS, MK23, SPQ-9B, NATO Seasparrow, and RAM systems. That experience taught me discipline, teamwork, and how to stay sharp under pressure.

Today, I'm a husband, a father of two daughters, and a student working toward an associate degree in networking at Olympic College. I'm focused on improving my future for my family while continuing to compete at a high level.

Magic: The Gathering

I started playing Magic during Saviors of Kamigawa, and over the years it has become my main competitive outlet. I've earned 19 Top 8 finishes at Regional Championship Qualifiers (RCQs) and continue working toward deeper Regional Championship runs and eventual Pro Tour qualification.

I spend a lot of time on deck tuning, matchup preparation, sideboard planning, metagame analysis, and testing across Pioneer, Modern, and Standard.

Team Resolve

I'm the captain of Team Resolve, an up-and-coming Magic team built around player development, accountability, and long-term competitive growth.

Our core philosophy is:

  • Endure — Stay consistent through setbacks and variance
  • Adapt — Learn quickly and adjust to the metagame
  • Delve — Go deeper than surface-level play and preparation

We focus on structured testing, better preparation habits, cleaner communication, and helping each other improve.

What I Work On

  • Deck optimization and sideboard mapping
  • Meta analysis and matchup preparation
  • Testing systems for competitive MTG teams
  • Building resources to help players improve

Other Games
