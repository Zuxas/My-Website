Entry 001 · March 2026 · Modern

season start — evaluating the field

Coming off back-to-back qualifying seasons — 23 RCQ Top 8s on Dimir Midrange last Standard season and 17 RCQ Top 8s on Boros Energy last Modern season. Five total RC qualifications. The process works. Now we're doing it again in Modern and the question is simple: what do we play?

the deck decision problem

The naive answer is to run back Boros Energy. I know it well, Team Resolve knows it well, and it's still one of the best decks in the format. But "still good" and "correct call for this RC cycle" are different questions. Last season Boros Energy was good and underrespected. This season it's good and everyone knows exactly what it does and has prepared for it. The margins tighten when the field is ready for you.

So we're not running it back reflexively. We're doing what we always do: running the gauntlet, tracking win rates, and letting the data tell us what the field supports.

what's in the gauntlet

Primary Test
UW Blink
Most testing hours so far. Ephemerate is legitimately one of the best cards in Modern. The skill ceiling is high which means the reps compound — every hour of testing translates into a percentage point of edge at an RC.
Known Quantity
Boros Energy
Evaluating whether the hate density in the expected field has reached the point where the margins don't support it at the RC level. RCQs might still be correct. Full RC might not be.
Active Test
Grixis Reanimator (Glockulous)
The combo option. Fastest kill in Modern when it's on. The field is built to interact with board-based threats — not to stop a turn-two Atraxa. The question is whether it holds up in a prepared RC field over two days.
Evaluating Splash
Jeskai Blink
Does red improve the blink shell enough to justify the mana hit? Teferi is a game against combo. Lightning Bolt helps the aggro matchups. But clean openers matter and the red sources create some tension there.

what we know so far

Early gauntlet data is pointing toward the blink shell as the highest-upside choice for an RC field. It's harder to hate out than Boros Energy, the flexibility to play control against aggro and beatdown against control is exactly what you want in a diverse field, and the cards are individually powerful enough that even games where the synergy doesn't come online are winnable.

Glockulous is interesting. It's won two gauntlet sessions in ways that Boros Energy couldn't — specifically against Prowess, where creature removal is mostly useless against a turn-two Atraxa. If the expected RC field is light on dedicated graveyard hate, we might be looking at this as the correct call. That's a read we'll make closer to the event.

"The goal isn't to play the best deck in a vacuum. It's to play the best deck for the specific field at the specific event."

next steps

  • Continue gauntlet reps on UW Blink through end of March
  • Pull MTGMelee data on graveyard hate density in recent Modern RCQs
  • Make final deck selection decision before the next RCQ block
  • Build full sideboard guide for whichever shell we lock
