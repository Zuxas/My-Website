Chapter 01

DECK
IDENTITY

"Every creature is a Human. Every Human entering play pumps Thalia's Lieutenant and generates a token from Adeline. Brave the Elements makes them all unblockable or indestructible. The deck kills fast, protects itself, and rebuilds after sweepers."

Mono-White Humans is Pioneer's premier tribal aggro deck. The plan is straightforward: deploy one-drop Humans on T1, develop a wide board T2-T3, pump the entire team with Thalia's Lieutenant and Coppercoat Vanguard, and win through a single combat step protected by Brave the Elements. The deck's power comes from the explosive synergy between its Human tribal lords and its go-wide token generation.

Adeline, Resplendent Cathar is the deck's premier threat: a 1/1 Human for {1WW} that whenever you attack, puts a 1/1 Human token into play tapping for each opponent (growing each time). The tokens enter tapped and attacking — Adeline herself doesn't need to attack. A single Adeline generates 1-2 tokens per combat, and each token triggers Thalia's Lieutenant when it enters (putting a +1/+1 counter on it) and gets +1/+1 from Coppercoat Vanguard.

Recruitment Officer provides the deck's card advantage engine: a 2/1 Human for {W} with Outpost Recruit — {3W}: look at the top four cards, put a Human into your hand and the rest on the bottom. In the mid-game when you're running low on threats, Recruitment Officer ensures you always have the next Human to deploy. Brave the Elements is the deck's primary protection spell — naming white makes all your white creatures immune to spot removal and indestructible to damage-based sweepers for the turn, while also making them unblockable by non-white creatures.

Why Mono-White Humans

Speed
T3-T4 Lethal With Protection
A T1 Recruitment Officer, T2 Thalia's Lieutenant, T3 Adeline generates a 4/4 Adeline, a 3/3 Lieutenant, and a 2/2 token with one attack. Brave the Elements on T4 makes everything unblockable by non-white and immune to targeted removal. This clock kills T4 on average and is essentially impossible to answer without a sweeper that resolves before Brave.
Resilience
Brave the Elements Blanks All Targeted Removal
Brave the Elements costs {W} at instant speed: choose a color — all your white creatures gain protection from that color until EOT. Protection from red blanks Fatal Push and Lightning Axe. Protection from black blanks Thoughtseize targeting and most removal. Protection from blue blanks counters targeting creatures. One card nullifies an opponent's entire removal suite in a single turn.
Disruption
Thalia Taxes Their Answers
Thalia, Guardian of Thraben taxes every noncreature spell {1} extra. Against UR Cutter (20+ spells), Greasefang (10+ spells), and control (15+ spells), Thalia generates 10-20 extra mana worth of tempo over a game. Your creatures cost nothing extra (all are creatures). Opponents pay more for every answer while you deploy threats for the normal cost.

Key Card Notes

The Pump
Thalia's Lieutenant + Coppercoat Vanguard
Thalia's Lieutenant: {1W} Human — when it enters, put a +1/+1 counter on each other Human you control. Whenever another Human enters under your control, put a +1/+1 counter on Lieutenant. Coppercoat Vanguard: {1W} Human — other Humans you control get +1/+1 and have ward {1} (opponent must pay {1} extra to target them). These two together: every Human that enters pumps Lieutenant, and every Human on board has ward and gets +1 from Vanguard.
The Generator
Adeline, Resplendent Cathar
1/1 for {1WW} (grows via counters from Thalia's Lieutenant and Vanguard). Whenever you attack: for each opponent, create a 1/1 Human token that's tapped and attacking. Each token: triggers Thalia's Lieutenant (+1/+1 counter), gets +1/+1 from Vanguard, and has ward from Vanguard. One Adeline with Lieutenant and Vanguard in play can generate 3-5 creatures in two attack steps. Adeline herself is the #1 removal target — opponents always kill her first.
The Shield
Brave the Elements
{W} instant. Choose a color — all white creatures you control gain protection from that color until EOT. Protection means: can't be targeted by that color, can't be blocked by that color, damage prevention from that color. Naming white protects your own creatures from your own cards (used rarely). Naming red: blocks Lightning Axe, Play with Fire, Fiery Impulse, sweepers. Naming black: blocks Fatal Push, Thoughtseize-adjacent effects. Naming blue: blocks Into the Flood Maw, Make Disappear targeting your creatures.
The Body
Dauntless Bodyguard — Sacrifice for Indestructible
{W} Human — when it enters, attach it to another creature. Sacrifice Bodyguard: the attached creature gains indestructible until EOT. Protect Adeline or Thalia's Lieutenant from targeted removal or combat damage by sacrificing Bodyguard. This is a two-for-one in your favor: they use a removal spell AND you sacrifice a 2/1 body, but your threat survives. Against sweepers: sacrifice Bodyguard to give Adeline indestructible — Supreme Verdict doesn't destroy her.
Chapter 03

GAME
FLOW

"T1 one-drop. T2 Thalia or Lieutenant. T3 Adeline attacks into tokens. T4 Brave the Elements — unblockable attack for lethal."
Ideal Curve
T1–T4 Kill
T1: Recruitment Officer (2/1) or Dauntless Bodyguard (2/1). T2: Thalia's Lieutenant — Lieutenant enters, pumps the T1 Human +1/+1. Both creatures now at 3/2 and 2/2. T3: Adeline (now 2/2 from Lieutenant trigger) — attack, Adeline trigger creates a 1/1 token tapped attacking (Lieutenant pumps again). T4: second attack with 4 creatures, then Brave the Elements naming the color of their blockers — everything unblockable for the turn. Lethal.
Mulligan
Need: 2 Lands + 2 One-Drops or Curve
Keep: any hand with 2-3 Plains, a one-drop Human, and a 2-3 drop. Mulligan: 1 land (can't function), 4+ lands (too slow to deploy threats in time), 0 one-drops (the deck needs to curve from T1). At 6: 2 lands + Recruitment Officer + any 2-drop is excellent. At 5: 2 lands + Officer + Thalia's Lieutenant is a correct keep. One-drops are the most critical opening cards — prioritize hands that have at least two creatures to deploy T1-T2.
Brave the Elements Timing
Declare Attackers, Then Cast Brave
Brave the Elements is an instant — cast it AFTER declaring attackers. Sequence: declare all your creatures as attackers → opponents declare blockers → before damage, cast Brave the Elements naming the blocker color. Result: all your white creatures have protection from that color — blockers are removed from combat (they can't block white creatures with protection from their color). Your entire attack resolves unblocked. The opponent can't respond with block assignments after Brave resolves.
Coppercoat Vanguard Ward
Every Human Costs {1} Extra to Target
Coppercoat Vanguard gives all other Humans ward {1}. This means: targeting any of your Humans with a spell or ability requires paying {1} additional, or the spell is countered. In practice: Fatal Push targeting your 1-drop now costs {B}{1}. Lightning Axe targeting Adeline costs {R}{1}. On T2 with Thalia also in play, every answer they have costs {1} extra (Thalia) + {1} more (Ward) = {2} extra per interaction. This makes answering your board prohibitively expensive.
Chapter 04

CORE
ENGINES

"Lieutenant pumps every Human that enters. Vanguard gives everyone +1/+1 and ward. Adeline generates bodies every attack. Three engines compounding simultaneously."
Engine 1 — Counters
Thalia's Lieutenant Counter Loop
Every Human entering play under your control puts a +1/+1 counter on Thalia's Lieutenant. Lieutenant entering play puts a +1/+1 counter on EACH other Human. With Adeline generating 1-2 tokens per combat, Lieutenant grows by 1-2 counters per attack step. A Lieutenant that's been in play for 2-3 attacks is a 6-8/6-8 creature. The counter loop is self-reinforcing — more Humans entering → bigger Lieutenant → bigger whole team.
Engine 2 — Tokens
Adeline's Combat Token Generation
Whenever you attack, Adeline creates 1/1 Human tokens (one per opponent) tapped and attacking. In a 1v1 game: one token per attack. Each token triggers Lieutenant (+1 counter), enters with ward from Vanguard (+1/+1), and attacks immediately. Two Adeline attacks with Lieutenant in play creates 2 tokens — each of which pumps Lieutenant 2 more times (from the two enters). Token generation compounds rapidly with both lords on board.
Engine 3 — Protection
Brave the Elements + Ward
Coppercoat Vanguard's ward {1} forces opponents to pay {1} extra per targeting spell. Brave the Elements provides a full-color protection layer at {W} instant speed. Together they create a two-layer defense: ward taxes every individual removal spell, and Brave blanks all removal of a chosen color in a single turn. Against removal-heavy decks, this combination means you can commit your entire board knowing Brave prevents it from being answered profitably.
Chapter 05

LINES &
TACTICS

"Brave after declaring attackers. Bodyguard sacrificed at the right moment. Adeline attacks even into walls."
Line 01 — Adeline Attacks Even If She'd Die in Combat
Adeline's token-creation trigger fires whenever you attack — not when she deals damage or survives. If Adeline attacks into a 4/4 that would kill her in combat, she STILL creates her token before combat damage resolves (the trigger goes on the stack when you declare attackers, which is before damage). Always attack with Adeline. The token generation is more valuable than keeping her back as a blocker. Even a suicidal Adeline attack creates a Human token that pumps Lieutenant and gets Vanguard's buff.
Line 02 — Dauntless Bodyguard Attachment: Choose the Right Target
Dauntless Bodyguard attaches to a creature when it enters. Choose the highest-value threat: Adeline (your token generator — protect her from removal), Thalia's Lieutenant (your counter engine — protect her from sweepers), or Coppercoat Vanguard (your ward provider — protect him from Fatal Push). The correct attachment depends on the matchup: vs removal-heavy (Rakdos): attach to Adeline. Vs sweeper-heavy (control): attach to Lieutenant. Vs aggro racing: attach to Vanguard. Never attach to a Recruitment Officer or Kytheon — the 2/1 is less valuable than the lords.
Line 03 — Brave the Elements vs Sweepers: Name the Right Color
When the opponent casts a sweeper, respond with Brave the Elements naming the correct color. Supreme Verdict (WU — damage prevention doesn't apply since it destroys, not deals damage — but protection prevents "being dealt damage" AND "being the target of spells" for the chosen color. Protection from white counters Supreme Verdict if it's white. However, "destroy all creatures" isn't targeting — it hits everyone. Brave the Elements protection specifically says the creature "can't be blocked" and "can't be targeted" and "damage prevention" — it doesn't grant indestructible. Against Supreme Verdict, use Dauntless Bodyguard (sacrifice for indestructible), not Brave. Against Anger of the Gods (red 3 damage), name red with Brave — damage from red spells is prevented, creatures survive.
Line 04 — Recruitment Officer's Outpost Recruit: Use at 4 Mana in the Mid-Game
Recruitment Officer's activated ability costs {3W}: look at top 4, keep a Human. This is a mid-game engine for refueling after your initial board is answered. Use it on T4-T5 when your board has been cleared by a sweeper and you need to reload. The {3W} cost means you need 4 mana — it's not a T1 action (just deploy it as a 2/1 and continue). In the late game with 5+ mana and no action, activate twice to dig 8 cards deep for your key Humans (Adeline, Lieutenant, Vanguard).

SIDEBOARD
GUIDE

"Your sideboard addresses sweepers (Guardian of Faith), GY decks (Rest in Peace), and attrition (Wedding Announcement)."
P1 — vs Sweepers
Guardian of Faith ×2
3/2 Spirit Human for {1WW} with flash. When it enters, exile any number of target creatures you control. When Guardian leaves the battlefield, return those exiled creatures. This converts a Supreme Verdict into a tempo loss for your opponent: respond to the Verdict by flashing in Guardian, exiling your key creatures, surviving the wrath, then they return when Guardian dies. Against UW Control's sweeper suite, Guardian completely nullifies Verdict at the cost of only the creatures you chose not to save. Often used to save just your lords.
P2 — vs Attrition/Control
Wedding Announcement ×2
Enchantment for {2W}. Chapter 1: put a 1/1 white Human Soldier into play. Chapter 2: same. Chapter 3: same, then transform into Wedding Festivity — all creatures you control get +1/+1. Three Humans over three turns is a sweeper-proof threat source. Unlike creatures, it doesn't die to sweepers until it's already generated 2-3 bodies. Against control where every sweeper clears your board, Wedding Announcement ensures you always have the next threat coming. Chapter 3 pump can be lethal with a developed board.
P3 — vs GY Decks
Rest in Peace ×2
Enchantment that exiles all GYs and prevents future GY use. Against Greasefang: Parhelion II can't be returned. Against Arclight Phoenix: Phoenixes can't come back. Against Lotus Field: Dig Through Time targets removed. Deploy on T2 alongside a one-drop creature to deny their GY engine before it sets up. Note: RIP also exiles your creatures from GY — Dauntless Bodyguard attached to a creature can't have the creature returned if RIP is in play. Plan around this if relevant.
P4 — Enchantment Removal
Portable Hole + Ossification
Portable Hole: {W} enchantment — exile target nonland permanent with MV 2 or less until Portable Hole leaves. Hits: Thalia, Coppercoat Vanguard, Bloodtithe Harvester, Cori-Steel Cutter (before transformation), Eidolon of the Great Revel. Ossification: {1W} enchantment — exile target creature or planeswalker. Note: Ossification requires you to control a Plains (easy in mono-white). Both are enchantments — stronger against decks with limited enchantment removal, weaker against Selesnya Company (Hopeful Initiate) or anything with Boseiju.
Chapter 09

HEURISTICS

"Brave after declaring attackers. Adeline always attacks. Bodyguard on the right target. Thalia taxes — use the tempo."
  1. 01Brave the Elements is cast AFTER declaring attackers, before damage. The correct window is after blockers are declared. Declare your attackers → opponent assigns blockers → before combat damage, cast Brave naming the blocker color. All blockers are removed from combat (protection from that color means they can't block). Your entire attack is unblockable for that turn.
  2. 02Adeline always attacks — even into creatures that would kill her. Adeline's token trigger fires when you attack, before combat damage. She creates the token even if she dies in combat. Always attack with Adeline — the token generation is more valuable than preserving her as a blocker, and a suicidal Adeline attack still creates a body for Lieutenant and Vanguard.
  3. 03Dauntless Bodyguard: attach to Adeline vs removal, Lieutenant vs sweepers. Against removal-heavy decks: attach to Adeline (she's the #1 removal target). Against sweeper decks: attach to Lieutenant (protecting the counter engine matters most). Against aggro racing: attach to Coppercoat Vanguard (preserving ward for the whole team is most valuable). Never attach to a 1/1 token or Recruitment Officer.
  4. 04Brave the Elements vs Supreme Verdict: Brave doesn't grant indestructible. Brave the Elements grants protection from a color — not indestructible. Supreme Verdict says "destroy" which isn't dealing damage and doesn't require targeting. Protection doesn't prevent "destroy" effects. Against Supreme Verdict: use Dauntless Bodyguard (sacrifice for indestructible) or Guardian of Faith (SB, flash — exile creatures before Verdict resolves). Brave beats Anger of the Gods (red damage) but not Verdict (white destroy).
  5. 05Coppercoat Vanguard ward {1} costs — don't forget to make them pay it. Whenever an opponent targets one of your other Humans, they must pay {1} or the spell is countered. This is a triggered ability — you must prioritize it and announce "ward triggers." Opponents who cast removal without realizing ward exists will learn — but in competitive play, remind them ward triggers so the interaction resolves correctly.
  6. 06Thalia taxes your own Brave the Elements {1} — hold extra mana when planning to Brave. Thalia taxes ALL noncreature spells including your own. Brave the Elements normally costs {W}; with your own Thalia in play, it costs {1W}. This is only relevant when you're at exactly {W} available mana and planning to Brave. Hold an extra {1} on turns you plan to use Brave with Thalia in play.
  7. 07Kytheon, Hero of Akros: attack with three or more creatures to transform into a planeswalker. Kytheon becomes Gideon, Battle-Forged at EOT if you attacked with three or more creatures this turn. As a planeswalker, he provides an additional finisher axis. Against removal-heavy decks, flipped Kytheon (with his indestructible ability) is extremely difficult to answer. Attack with your full board on T3-T4 when Kytheon has been in play to enable the flip.
  8. 08Castle Ardenvale: activate at instant speed to generate bodies mid-combat or after sweepers. Castle Ardenvale activates for {3W}: create a 1/1 white Human token. Use at instant speed after their sweeper resolves (with mana up) to immediately rebuild. Use during combat to create a blocker for an unexpected attack. With 5+ mana available, activate twice in a turn to generate two tokens for Lieutenant triggers.
  9. 09Wedding Announcement generates bodies through multiple sweepers — deploy early vs control. Wedding Announcement creates a Human token on Chapter 1 (T2 of being in play) and Chapter 2 (T3). If they sweep the board on T4, Announcement has already generated 2 bodies and continues on T5 with Chapter 3. Against control, Wedding Announcement is your resilient value engine — deploy it on T3 whenever you expect a sweeper is coming.
  10. 10Recruitment Officer mid-game activation: dig at 4 mana, not T1. Recruitment Officer's {3W} ability is most powerful in the mid-game when your hand is depleted. Activate on T4-T6 when you need Adeline, Lieutenant, or another specific Human. On T1, just play Officer as a 2/1 attacker — the activation will come later. In the late game with 8 mana available, activate twice on the same turn to dig 8 cards deep.
Chapter 10

TUNING
NOTES

"4 Adeline, 4 Thalia's Lieutenant, 4 Coppercoat Vanguard, 4 Brave the Elements are immovable. Everything else adjusts."
Lock
4 Adeline + 4 Lieutenant + 4 Vanguard
These three form the deck's tribal engine. Adeline generates bodies, Lieutenant pumps the team, Vanguard provides +1/+1 and ward to everything. All three at 4 copies ensures you draw at least two of the three in every game. Never cut any.
Lock
4 Brave the Elements
The deck's primary protection spell and combat trick. Brave is what allows the go-wide Human strategy to work against removal. 4 copies ensures you draw it in most games. Never cut below 4.
Lock
4 Thalia, Guardian of Thraben
The deck's primary disruption piece. Thalia taxing noncreature spells is what enables the Human aggro strategy against combo and control. 4 is mandatory — finding Thalia consistently on T2 is critical.
Flex 3–4
Recruitment Officer
4 is the reference number. Can trim to 3 in metas where the 2/1 body is less relevant and you need more aggressive one-drops. The mid-game activation is most valuable in long games vs control — if your meta is all aggro mirrors, Officer's activation matters less.
Flex 2–4
Removal (Declaration + Fateful Absence)
The removal suite adjusts to meta threats. Declaration in Stone handles go-wide (exiles multiple copies). Fateful Absence handles any creature or planeswalker. In aggro-heavy metas, reduce removal to 2 total and add more creatures. In control-heavy metas, increase to 4 total for more interaction.
Utility Lands
Castle Ardenvale + Mutavault
Castle Ardenvale generates 1/1 Humans at instant speed (enters tapped — doesn't ETB under Mutavault's "land" condition). Mutavault can animate as a 2/2 all-creature and is itself a Human when a creature. 2 of each is the correct ratio. 3+ utility lands risks missing critical Plains for white mana in opening hands.

EVENT
PREP

"Know which color to name with Brave. Know when Bodyguard saves vs sacrifices. Know the sweeper interaction."
Know Cold
Brave the Elements — Name by Matchup
vs RDW: name red — blocks Fiery Impulse, Lightning Strike, Eidolon triggers, and all burn. vs Rakdos Midrange: name black — blocks Fatal Push, Power Word Kill, Dreadbore. vs UR Cutter: name blue — blocks Into the Flood Maw, Make Disappear targeting your creatures. vs UW Control: name white — blocks only their white removal (March of Otherworldly Light); name blue for their counterspells. vs Selesnya (mirror-ish): name green — your white creatures can't be blocked by green. In combat: name the color of their blockers to make your attack unblockable.
Know Cold
Sweeper Defense Sequence
Supreme Verdict incoming: sacrifice Dauntless Bodyguard targeting Adeline or Lieutenant (indestructible survives Verdict). Alternatively, flash in Guardian of Faith in response, exiling your key creatures before Verdict resolves. Temporary Lockdown (exiles MV≤2): no answer from these creatures themselves — Brave doesn't help, Bodyguard doesn't help. In game 2 vs UW Control, don't overcommit creatures with MV≤2 when you see 4 mana open. Anger of the Gods (deals 3 damage): Brave the Elements naming red prevents damage from red spells — your creatures survive.
Verify
SB Matches Expected Field
Heavy control (UW): Wedding Announcement × 2 + Guardian of Faith × 2 + Reidane. Heavy GY (Greasefang, Phoenix): Rest in Peace × 2. Heavy aggro/creature mirrors: Portable Hole × 2 + Ossification × 2. Heavy midrange (Rakdos): Ossification × 2 + Wedding Announcement × 2. The 15-card SB covers all matchups — confirm you know which 4-5 cards come in for each deck you expect.
Round 1 Tell
T1 Land Read
