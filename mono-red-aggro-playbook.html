Chapter 01

DECK
IDENTITY

"You don't play around removal. You play faster than they can cast it."

Mono-Red Aggro is Standard's fastest deck. Every creature in the 75 has haste or generates immediate value. Emberheart Challenger is a 2/2 haste prowess that grows whenever you target it. Burnout Bashtronaut is a 2/1 haste that copies itself when it attacks — one card becomes two attackers. Nova Hellkite is a 4/4 haste flying dragon that deals 2 damage on entry. Everything attacks the turn it enters.

The burn suite closes games that stall. Burst Lightning is 2 damage for {R} or 4 damage with kicker. Sear is 4 damage for {1}{R} to any creature or planeswalker. When opponents stabilize at 8 life, three burn spells end the game without combat. The deck kills opponents who never see the final blow coming.

Raphael, the Nightwatcher is the top-end resilience piece — he generates card advantage every turn and represents a 5/4 lifelink body that opponents must answer. Inti, Seneschal of the Sun exiles the top card on each attack, giving you resources to cast even when your hand is empty. Against control decks that answer everything, these two provide inevitability.

Why Mono-Red in 2026

Speed
T3–T4 Kill Clock
Faster than every other deck in Standard. Against Mono-Green and Simic Rhythm who are setting up their engine, you're attacking for 8+ on turn 3. Against control that's trying to sculpt a hand, you're dealing 20 before they assemble their stabilization tools.
Consistency
All Threats, No Blanks
Every card in the main deck is either a hasty threat or a damage spell that kills something. There are no setup cards, no ramp, no card draw that doesn't also deal damage. Every card in your opening hand does something immediately.
Versatility
Burn Goes to Face or Kills Creatures
Burst Lightning and Sear are both removal AND reach. A Sear that kills their Gene Pollinator is just as good as a Sear to face. Against decks you can't race, pivot to controlling the board with burn and win with a single unblocked Nova Hellkite.

When NOT to Register Mono-Red

Avoid if: The expected field is heavy on lifelink (Voice of Victory, Ajani tokens from Azorius Flash) and avatar effects. Multiple Sunspine Lynx (SB) help vs lifelink decks, but if you're facing 4+ Voices of Victory across multiple rounds, your racing math becomes unreliable. Also avoid if Avatar's Wrath is heavily played — it sweeps your entire board at one mana per creature killed.

Key Card Notes

The Prowess Engine
Emberheart Challenger
2/2 haste prowess. Valiant: whenever it becomes the target of a spell or ability you control, it gets a +1/+1 counter permanently. Targeting Emberheart with Burst Lightning, Shock, or Sear triggers Valiant — the counter stacks. A T2 Emberheart targeted by a T2 Shock is a 3/3 haste for an effective 3 mana total. Prowess stacks on top. By T4, this card is often a 5/5+ hasty attacker.
The Token Doubler
Burnout Bashtronaut
2/1 haste. Whenever it attacks, create a token copy of Burnout Bashtronaut that's tapped and attacking. One attacker becomes two. Two Bashtronauts become four. In multiples, Bashtronaut's attack trigger creates an attacking token of EACH Bashtronaut — exponential scaling. A second Bashtronaut's attack creates copies of both, giving you 4 attackers from 2 cards. The ceiling is enormous.
The Reach
Burst Lightning + Sear
Burst Lightning: {R} — 2 damage, or {5}{R} — 4 damage with kicker. Sear: {1}{R} — 4 damage to any creature or planeswalker. These are both removal AND reach. At 6 life: Burst Lightning + Sear = 6 damage to face = game. They also trigger Emberheart Challenger's Valiant when targeting your own creatures. Never tap a non-land mana source — 6 lands + kicker Burst Lightning is viable in longer games.
The Top End
Nova Hellkite + Raphael
Nova Hellkite: 4/4 flying haste Dragon for 5 mana. ETB: deals 2 damage to any target. Immediately relevant damage on entry + 4 flying damage on attack = 6 damage the turn it enters. Raphael, the Nightwatcher: generates card advantage each turn while applying pressure. Boards in vs control — provides both a 5/4 lifelink body and ongoing resource generation that outlasts removal.
Chapter 03

GAME
PLAN

"T1 creature. T2 creature + spell. T3 attack for lethal. That's the plan. Execute it."
On the Play
T1 Creature Is Mandatory
T1: Fanatical Firebrand or Hired Claw. On the play, any hand without a T1 haste creature is a likely mulligan. That one point of damage on turn 1 matters. T2: Emberheart Challenger + Shock targeting Emberheart (prowess trigger + Valiant counter = 4/4 haste effectively). T3: Burnout Bashtronaut (attack with Bashtronaut, token copy of Bashtronaut attacks too). Two creatures have been attacking since T1 — they're at 12 or less.
Burn Clock
Face vs Creature Decision
Every burn spell faces this decision: go to face or kill a blocker? Rule: kill the blocker IF killing it lets you deal more damage this attack than the spell to face. Example: they have a 2/4 blocker and you have Emberheart (3/3) + Sear. Sear to face = 4 damage. Sear kills the blocker + Emberheart attacks unblocked = 3 damage. Cast Sear to face. The math governs — always calculate before casting.
Mulligan
7 or 6, Never 5
Keep on 7: any hand with a T1 creature + T2 play + lands. Keep on 6: hands with 2 one-drops + burn, or 1 one-drop + Emberheart + burn. Hard mulligan: no T1 creature on the play, 5+ lands, no haste creatures whatsoever. At 5 you need two haste creatures that attack immediately — anything slower is a game loss.
Stalled Board
Pivot to Pure Burn
When blocked by large creatures (Ouroboroid 4/4s, Drowner 3/2s), stop attacking into unfavorable blocks. Burn their life total directly. 3 burn spells = 8-10 damage to face. A Nova Hellkite for 2 ETB + 4 flying = 6 unblocked damage. When the ground stalls, win through the air or through burn to face. Don't attack into large blockers and lose your threats.
Chapter 04

CORE
ENGINES

"Burnout Bashtronaut token copies also attack and also create tokens when THEY attack."
Engine 1 — Kills T3
Burnout Exponential
T2: Two Burnout Bashtronauts. T3 attack: each creates a tapped attacking token of itself. You have 4 attackers. Next turn: each of the 4 attacks, creating 4 more = 8 total. The exponential growth means a stalled board is solved in 1-2 turns with Bashtronauts. Opponents must answer each one individually or lose to the math.
Engine 2 — Pumps to Win
Emberheart Valiant Stack
Emberheart Challenger with 3 Valiant counters is a 5/5 haste prowess. Target it with Burst Lightning (Valiant trigger → 6/6), cast Shock (prowess → +1/+1 until EOT → 7/7 attacking). Two burn spells targeting Emberheart = 7+ damage in one combat. The Valiant trigger is permanent; prowess is temporary but stacks in-combat.
Engine 3 — Late Game
Inti + Nova Hellkite
Against decks that match your early pressure: Inti exiles top card on attack and lets you cast it for free if creatures attacked. Nova Hellkite provides 6 damage the turn it enters (2 ETB + 4 flying). In slower games after burn runs low, Inti refuels and Hellkite closes without needing to attack through ground blockers.
Chapter 05

LINES &
TACTICS

"Calculate the damage BEFORE casting the burn. Know your kill turn before you commit."
Line 01 — Burnout Bashtronaut Token Copies Also Have the Attack Trigger
When Burnout Bashtronaut attacks, it creates a token copy of itself that's tapped and attacking. That token ALSO has the attack trigger. But it's already tapped and attacking this turn — so the token's trigger fires the NEXT time it attacks. With 2 Bashtronauts attacking T2: 2 tokens created. T3: those 2 tokens attack and each creates a token = 4 Bashtronauts + 2 tokens attacking = 6 attackers total. The doubling is one-turn delayed for tokens, not immediate.
Line 02 — Emberheart Challenger Valiant Triggers Only Once Per Turn
Valiant says "whenever this creature becomes the target of a spell or ability you control for the FIRST time each turn." Only the first targeting each turn triggers Valiant. If you cast Shock targeting Emberheart (Valiant triggers, +1/+1 counter), then cast Burst Lightning also targeting Emberheart — Burst Lightning does NOT trigger Valiant again. One counter per turn maximum. However, Burst Lightning's prowess pump (+1/+1 until EOT) still applies. Multiple-targeting is fine for prowess; only the first matters for Valiant.
Line 03 — Burst Lightning Kicker Timing
Burst Lightning with kicker costs {5}{R} = 6 total mana. The kicker is paid when you cast the spell — you declare kicker before it resolves. In early turns (1-3), always cast unkicked (2 damage for R). In late turns (6+ mana), kicked Burst Lightning is 4 damage for 6 mana while triggering prowess on any creature in play. The kicker option is specifically for when the opponent is at 4 life and you need one spell to kill them when they have blockers everywhere.
Line 04 — Fanatical Firebrand Tap Ability — Exile After Use
Fanatical Firebrand: {T}: it deals 1 damage to any target, then exile Fanatical Firebrand. This is a sacrifice effect — you get one activation, then it's gone. Use cases: (1) Deal the final 1 damage to face when opponent is at 1 life. (2) Kill a 1-toughness blocker before combat. (3) Trigger Emberheart Challenger's Valiant for a free +1/+1 counter (targeting your own creature with a {T} ability is a valid Valiant trigger). Don't waste the activation on irrelevant targets — it only fires once.
Chapter 06

ROLE
ASSIGNMENT

"You are always the aggressor. The only question is whether you're racing or burning."
vs Midrange
Pure Speed — Race
Against Dimir, Simic, Bant — they're setting up engines. You're attacking for 8 before their engine is active. Don't interact with their setup at all. Kill them before Ouroboroid hits. Burn goes face 100% of the time unless killing a blocker creates more damage that turn. Fast and clean.
vs Aggro Mirrors
Attack + Block Math
Against Boros Aggro mirror or Izzet Prowess — track life totals carefully. Racing math: if you're both at the same life total and you attack into their attack next turn, who dies first? Burnout Bashtronaut tokens give you more attackers than they have blockers. Slagstorm (SB) sweeps their 1/1 tokens.
vs Control
Resilience + Burn
Against Jeskai and Azorius Flash — curve out through their counters. Deploy threats every turn until one sticks. Raphael (SB) provides card advantage to rebuild after sweepers. Inti exiles top card for free casts. Keep enough burn in hand to close from 8 life without attacking.

MONO-GREEN LANDFALL

Favorable

They're slower. T1-T3, they're ramping and setting up their engine. You're attacking for 8-10 damage. Sapling Nursery doesn't come online until T4-T5. By then you're presenting lethal or near-lethal through burn. Llanowar Elves and Badgermole Cub are 1/1 and 2/2 — Burst Lightning kills the Elves, your creatures trade with the Cub.

Priority: Go face as early as possible. Burn does not kill their Mightform Harmonizer (8 toughness) — don't try. Kill their mana dorks (Burst Lightning on Llanowar Elves is always correct) to slow their engine and give yourself extra attack steps. Nova Hellkite's flying bypasses their ground creatures entirely.

Needle: Sunspine Lynx (SB) — if they're gaining life from Beza or other effects, Lynx shuts off lifegain. Twisted Fealty gives you control of their Mightform Harmonizer for a turn — attack with their 8/8 into their own board.

GUIDE

"Sunspine Lynx is mandatory against any deck with lifegain — it shuts off the entire mechanic permanently."
P1 — Always
Sunspine Lynx ×3
Prevents opponents from gaining life. In against: Azorius Flash (Voice of Victory lifelink), Izzet Lessons (Monument to Endurance life gain), any deck with Ajani, Beza, or other life gain. The effect is permanent and applies to ALL life gain including combat damage lifegain. Mandatory in at least 3 of your 8 expected matchups.
P2 — Sweepers
Slagstorm ×2
Sorcery: deals 3 damage to each creature OR 3 damage to each player. The creature mode sweeps Izzet Prowess tokens (1/1 Otters), Boros Aggro 1-drops, Mono-White tokens. The player mode is an additional Fireball effect for 3 to face. Note: sweeper mode also kills YOUR creatures. Plan Slagstorm timing carefully.
P3 — Theft
Twisted Fealty ×2
Gain control of target creature until EOT, and it gains haste. Attack with their best creature into their own side, then it goes to your control permanently? (check oracle — may be until EOT only). In against: Mono-Green (steal Harmonizer), Dimir (steal Drowner), Azorius (steal Floodpits Drowner). One card that neutralizes their best threat while giving you an attacker.
P4 — GY Hate
Ghost Vacuum ×2
Artifact that exiles cards from graveyards. In against: Izzet Lessons (Monument to Endurance), Dimir Excruciator (any GY strategies), Simic Rhythm (Marang River Regent death trigger). Cheap enough to cast alongside your threats.

Chapter 09

HEURISTICS

"Always calculate lethal before casting the first burn spell. Know the finish line before you commit."
  1. 01T1 haste creature is mandatory on the play. That first point of damage on turn 1 represents a full turn of tempo. On the play, mulligan any hand without a T1 haste creature unless the hand is otherwise exceptional (2 Emberheart + lots of burn for T2 is almost as fast).
  2. 02Emberheart Valiant only triggers once per turn. The first spell that targets Emberheart triggers Valiant (+1/+1 counter). Subsequent spells targeting it the same turn only trigger prowess (+1/+1 until EOT). One counter per turn maximum — plan accordingly.
  3. 03Burnout Bashtronaut tokens attack-trigger on THEIR attack, not the turn they enter. Token enters tapped and attacking — the trigger fires next combat when THEY attack. 2 Bashtronauts on T2 → attack T3 → 4 attackers, but token triggers don't apply until T4. Plan the doubling one turn ahead.
  4. 04Burn goes face unless killing a blocker creates more damage this combat. Default burn to face. Exception: kill the blocker if unblocked damage + burn exceeds blocked damage + burn. Always run the math before casting. Novice error: killing a 2/4 blocker with Sear when Sear to face + blocked swing deals more total damage.
  5. 05Nova Hellkite's ETB damage can go to any target — face, creature, or planeswalker. The 2 damage on entry doesn't have to go to face. Kill a Kaito, finish off a 2-life creature, or remove a 1/1 token. Always evaluate the best use of the 2 ETB damage before choosing the target.
  6. 06Slagstorm also kills YOUR creatures. When you resolve Slagstorm's sweep mode (3 damage to each creature), your Fanatical Firebrand (1/1), Hired Claw, Burnout Bashtronaut tokens, and any 1-2 toughness creatures you control also die. Time Slagstorm AFTER you've attacked with your small creatures, not before.
  7. 07Sunspine Lynx only stops opponents from gaining life — it doesn't stop life loss. Your own burn spells that deal damage to opponents are still damage, just without the opponent gaining life. Lynx specifically prevents "gaining life" triggers and effects — not damage prevention or redirects.
  8. 08Fanatical Firebrand's {T} activation exiles it permanently. One use. Don't tap Firebrand for 1 damage to face when you can attack for 1 damage with it AND still have the activation available. Use the activation only when you can't attack (it's tapped, summoning sick in rare cases, or can't get through).
  9. 09Inti exiles the top card when a creature you control attacks — you can cast it that turn for free. When Inti is in play and you attack, you get to cast the exiled card for free. This means your draw step is replaced by a free cast every attack. Against control, Inti + any creature attacking = two spells per turn in a High Noon game.
  10. 10Rockface Village can tap for red mana as a creature. Rockface Village is a land that can become a red creature. In late game, it's an additional attacker. More importantly, it produces red mana — don't forget it counts toward your burn spell math. With 14 Mountains + 4 Rockface + 2 Soulstone = 20 mana sources.
Chapter 10

TUNING
NOTES

"Never go below 16 haste creatures. Speed is the deck's identity — compromise it and you're just a worse aggro deck."
Lock
8 Burn Spells Minimum
Burst Lightning + Sear at 4 each = 8 burn spells. This is the minimum for reliable reach. Without 8 burn spells, you can't close games from 8 life. The deck requires the burn to be functional — don't cut below 8 total.
Lock
4 Emberheart + 4 Burnout
The two most powerful threats. Full playsets of both. Emberheart provides the scaling clock (Valiant counters + prowess). Burnout provides the go-wide exponential (token doubling). Missing either reduces your ceiling and floor simultaneously.
Flex 0–2
Raphael + Inti
Top-end resilience. In aggro-heavy metas, cut both — they're slow for a race. In control-heavy metas, run 2-3 combined — they provide the inevitability control matchups require. Raphael's lifelink also matters vs Azorius Flash's lifelink strategy.
Flex 0–2
Stingerback Terror
The mainboard flex creature. In aggro metas, replace with Fanatical Firebrand × 4 for more T1 plays. In midrange metas, Stingerback Terror's resilience matters. If you're seeing lots of sweepers, cut it entirely for burn spells.
Consider
4 Fanatical Firebrand main
Some builds run 4 copies main. The T1 1-damage attack matters and the {T}: deal 1 damage activation provides the last point of reach. In fast meta matchups (Prowess, Boros Aggro mirrors), the T1 Firebrand's early damage is decisive.
Register When
Meta Is Slow + No Lifegain
If Izzet Lessons (Monument life gain) and Azorius Flash (Voice lifelink) are below 10% combined, register Mono-Red. If those two are dominant, reconsider — Sunspine Lynx helps but you're still fighting uphill. Best in metas of midrange decks without life gain.
Chapter 11

EVENT
PREP

"Know your kill turns before the match starts. Mono-Red is won in deckbuilding and pregame math."
Memorize
Lethal Math From Common Life Totals
8 life: Burst Lightning (2) + Sear (4) + any 2-power attacker = dead. 10 life: Nova Hellkite ETB (2) + Sear (4) + flying Hellkite (4) = dead the turn Hellkite enters. 12 life: 3 attackers at 4 power + Burst Lightning kicked (4) = dead. Know these numbers by reflex.
Know Cold
Slagstorm Kills Your Creatures Too
Attack with all small creatures first, then resolve Slagstorm. Never Slagstorm into your own board without attacking. The board wipe hits everything — always attack before wiping.
Verify
SB Priority for Expected Field
Lifegain-heavy field (Azorius + Lessons): max 3 Sunspine Lynx mandatory. Aggro-heavy: max 2 Slagstorm + 2 Abrade. Midrange/control: 2 Twisted Fealty + 2 Ghost Vacuum. Confirm your 15 against the expected field.
Round 1 Tell
T1 Land Read
Forest = Mono-Green or Simic — go face completely, burn for them if possible. Hallowed Fountain = Azorius Flash — board Sunspine Lynx immediately. Steam Vents = Izzet (Prowess/Lessons/Spellementals) — race or control depending on what they do T1-T2. Watery Grave = Dimir — go wide with Bashtronauts.
