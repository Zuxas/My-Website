who i am

team resolve

Team Resolve is a competitive MTG team built around player development, structured testing, and long-term progression. The goal is Regional Championship participation and Pro Tour qualification — not just for me, but for every player on the team.

We run gauntlets, build full prep packages for each RC, track matchup data, and refine our processes each season. The philosophy:

  • Endure — Stay consistent through variance. Lose the right way and keep going.
  • Adapt — Adjust to the meta faster than your opponents do.
  • Delve — Go deeper than surface-level play. Surface-level loses to someone who studied.
