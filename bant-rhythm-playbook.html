Chapter 01

Deck
Identity

"Sixteen mana creatures. Four Nature's Rhythm tutors. One Craterhoof Behemoth. You produce more mana than any deck in Standard, you tutor any creature you need, and you kill in one attack step."

Bant Rhythm is Standard's premier mana-dork aggro deck with a toolbox finish. It won RC Turin 2026 (1,025 players, 15-2) in the hands of Andrea Fortunati, beating Esper Pixie in the finals. The deck runs 16 mana creatures that accelerate you 2-3 turns ahead of the curve, then deploys haymaker threats that no fair deck can answer in time. Nature's Rhythm ({X}{G}{G}) tutors any creature directly to the battlefield, and its harmonize ability lets you cast it AGAIN from the graveyard. Ouroboroid puts +1/+1 counters equal to its power on your ENTIRE board at the start of combat — every turn. Craterhoof Behemoth ends the game in one attack step.

The deck's identity is defined by a tension: you need to deploy enough mana creatures to accelerate, but every mana creature you draw late is a dead card. Nature's Rhythm solves this by turning excess mana into tutored threats. Badgermole Cub solves it by doubling the mana your creatures produce. The result: you're faster than aggro (T4-5 kills), more consistent than combo (16 mana creatures + 4 tutors), and more resilient than midrange (harmonize lets you rebuild from a sweeper).

The Key Cards — Oracle Verified

The Tutor Engine
Nature's Rhythm
{X}{G}{G} Sorcery. Search library for creature MV ≤ X, put onto battlefield. Harmonize {X}{G}{G}{G}{G}: cast from GY for this cost. You may tap a creature to reduce generic cost by its power. Then exile. Two tutor effects from one card. T3 with Cub mana: Nature's Rhythm for X=4 = Ouroboroid or Quantum Riddler directly to battlefield. Later: harmonize from GY tapping your 5-power Ouroboroid to reduce cost by 5.
The Exponential Threat
Ouroboroid
{2}{G}{G} 1/3 Plant Wurm. "At the beginning of combat on your turn, put X +1/+1 counters on each creature you control, where X is this creature's power." Enters as 1/3. First combat: each creature gets 1 counter. Ouroboroid is now 2/4. Second combat: each creature gets 2 counters. Ouroboroid is now 4/6. Third combat: 4 counters each. EXPONENTIAL growth. With 4 creatures in play, turn 3 of Ouroboroid = +16 total counters across the board.
The Finisher
Craterhoof Behemoth
{5}{G}{G}{G} 5/5 Beast. Haste. "When this enters, creatures you control gain trample and get +X/+X until EOT, where X = creatures you control." With 6 creatures: each gets +6/+6 and trample. A board of 1/1 mana dorks becomes 7/7 tramplers. Total damage from 6 creatures: 6 × 7 = 42 trampling damage. Nature's Rhythm for X=8 finds Craterhoof directly to battlefield. The one-card kill.
The Mana Doubler
Badgermole Cub
{1}{G} 2/2 Badger Mole. ETB: earthbend 1 (target land becomes a 0/0 creature with haste that's still a land, put a +1/+1 counter on it, returns to battlefield tapped if it dies/is exiled). "Whenever you tap a creature for mana, add an additional {G}." This doubles every mana creature. Llanowar Elves: {G} + {G} = {G}{G}. Gene Pollinator: any color + {G}. Spider Manifestation: {R}/{G} + {G}. With Cub, your T3 mana is often 5-6.
The Card Advantage Engine
Quantum Riddler
{3}{U}{U} 4/6 Sphinx. Flying. ETB: draw a card. "As long as you have one or fewer cards in hand, if you would draw one or more cards, draw that many plus one instead." Warp {1}{U}: cast for {1}{U} from hand (enters as a 1/1 instead). The draw doubling is active when you've dumped your hand — which this deck does quickly. Warp mode lets you deploy early as a 1/1 body that still doubles future draws. Nature's Rhythm for X=5 puts the full 4/6 version directly onto the battlefield.

Oracle-Verified Edge Cases

Nature's Rhythm Harmonize — Tap Creatures to Reduce Cost
"You may tap a creature you control to reduce that cost by an amount of generic mana equal to its power." You tap ONE creature per harmonize cast. An Ouroboroid that's grown to 5/X reduces the harmonize cost by 5 generic mana. Harmonize for X=5 normally costs {5}{G}{G}{G}{G} = 9 mana. With a 5-power creature tap: {G}{G}{G}{G} = 4 mana + the creature tap. This makes the second tutor surprisingly affordable in the mid-game. The creature tapped this way doesn't add mana — it just reduces the harmonize cost.
Ouroboroid — Counters Scale with Its Own Power, Including Itself
Ouroboroid puts counters on EACH creature you control — including itself. T1: Ouroboroid is 1/3. Combat trigger: each creature gets 1 counter. Ouroboroid is now 2/4. T2: combat trigger: each creature gets 2 counters. Ouroboroid is now 4/6. T3: 4 counters each. Ouroboroid is 8/10. The growth is EXPONENTIAL (power doubles each turn). With a second Ouroboroid via Mockingbird: both triggers fire — the first puts X counters (based on first Ouroboroid's power), then the second puts X counters (based on second's power). Double exponential.
Badgermole Cub Earthbend — The Land Becomes a Creature
Earthbend 1: target land becomes a 0/0 creature with haste (still a land), put a +1/+1 counter on it. It's a 1/1 land creature. It CAN attack (haste). It CAN be killed by creature removal. It CAN tap for mana AND attack in the same turn (tap for mana pre-combat, attack post-combat). "When it dies or is exiled, return it to the battlefield tapped" — the land is resilient. Sweepers kill it but it comes back tapped. It's essentially a free 1/1 haste that replaces itself.
Mockingbird — Copies Anything, Gains Flying
{X}{U} 1/1 Bird Bard. Flying. "Enter as a copy of any creature with MV ≤ mana spent to cast this, except it's a Bird in addition to its other types and has flying." Copy opponent's best threat. Copy your own Ouroboroid (now you have two exponential combat triggers). Copy Craterhoof (two ETBs = double +X/+X). The copy has flying regardless of what it copies — a flying Ouroboroid or flying Craterhoof. X = total mana spent, not just X in the cost. Mockingbird for {5}{U} (6 mana spent) copies anything MV ≤ 6.
Gene Pollinator — Taps ANY Untapped Permanent
{G} 1/2 Artifact Creature. "{T}, Tap an untapped permanent you control: Add one mana of any color." The permanent you tap doesn't need to be a creature — it can be a land, artifact, enchantment, or planeswalker. This effectively turns any untapped permanent into a mana source. With Badgermole Cub: Pollinator taps itself + taps another permanent → {any color} + {G} (from Cub's doubling). Gene Pollinator is also an artifact creature — relevant for metalcraft, artifact synergies, and Brightglass Gearhulk's tutor.
Formidable Speaker — Creature Tutor on ETB (Not Fauna Shaman)
{2}{G} 2/4 Elf Druid. "When this enters, you may discard a card. If you do, search library for a creature card, reveal it, put into hand." This is an ETB trigger, not an activated ability — it happens once on entry. Discard is optional ("you may"). The tutored creature goes to HAND (not battlefield — that's Nature's Rhythm). The {1}{T} untap ability is a separate utility: untap a mana creature for extra mana, or untap an attacker post-combat for blocking. Nature's Rhythm for X=3 puts Formidable Speaker directly onto battlefield → ETB triggers → discard a card → tutor a creature to hand.
Spider Manifestation — Untaps Whenever You Cast MV 4+
{1}{R/G} 2/2 Spider Avatar. Reach. "{T}: Add {R} or {G}." "Whenever you cast a spell with MV 4 or greater, untap this creature." Tap for mana → cast a 4+ MV spell → Spider untaps → tap for mana again. With Badgermole Cub: each tap adds {R/G} + {G}. Casting Ouroboroid (MV 4): Spider untaps → tap again → 4 total mana from one Spider in one turn. Casting Quantum Riddler (MV 5) or Craterhoof (MV 8): same untap. Chain multiple big spells in one turn.
Brightglass Gearhulk — Tutors Two MV ≤ 1 Permanents
{G}{G}{W}{W} 4/4 First strike, trample. "When this enters, search library for up to two artifact, creature, and/or enchantment cards with MV 1 or less, reveal them, put into hand." Finds: Llanowar Elves (MV 1 creature), Gene Pollinator (MV 1 artifact creature), Seam Rip (MV 1 enchantment), Meltstrider's Resolve (MV 1 enchantment), Soul-Guide Lantern (MV 1 artifact). Two tutors + a 4/4 first strike trample body. The {W}{W} cost requires Temple Garden or Starting Town.

Chapter 03

Gameplan

"Ramp → Tutor → Kill. The deck does three things and does them faster than anything in the format."

The Ideal Curve

Turn 1
Mana Creature
Llanowar Elves, Gene Pollinator, or Spider Manifestation. Any 1-drop that produces mana. With 12 one-drop mana creatures in the deck, you have a T1 dork ~87% of games on 7 cards. This is the deck's foundation — without T1 acceleration, you're a turn behind.
Turn 2
Badgermole Cub or Second Dork
Cub on T2 doubles your mana going forward. With T1 elf + T2 Cub: T3 mana = land + elf ({G}{G} with Cub) + Cub ({G}) + land = 5+ mana on T3. Without Cub: deploy a second mana creature. Two dorks + 2 lands = 4 mana on T3, still enough for Nature's Rhythm X=2.
Turn 3
Nature's Rhythm for the Haymaker
With 5+ mana: Nature's Rhythm X=4 → Ouroboroid directly to battlefield. X=3 → Formidable Speaker (ETB: discard, tutor creature to hand). X=5 → Quantum Riddler (4/6 flying, draw a card). The flexibility of Nature's Rhythm means T3 is always a massive threat regardless of what you need.

Mulligan Guide

Always Keep
Dork + Cub + Payoff
Any hand with a T1 mana creature, Badgermole Cub, and either Nature's Rhythm or a big threat is a snap keep. You'll have 5+ mana on T3 with a tutor or threat ready. Even without the payoff: dork + Cub + 2 lands keeps itself because you'll draw into Nature's Rhythm or a threat within 2-3 draws.
Mulligan
No Mana Creature
A hand with lands and big threats but no mana creature is a mulligan. Playing Ouroboroid on T4 instead of T3 (one turn slower) is the difference between winning and losing against Prowess. The deck needs T1 acceleration to function. Also mulligan: all mana creatures + no payoff and no Nature's Rhythm. You'll flood the board with 1/1s that do nothing.

Chapter 05 — Lines & Tactics

Lines &
Tactics

"Every edge case documented. Every 1-in-5000 interaction verified."
Mockingbird X Cost — "Mana Spent" Not MV
"MV less than or equal to the amount of mana SPENT to cast this creature." Mana spent includes colored mana. Mockingbird for {4}{U} = 5 mana spent. It can copy any creature MV ≤ 5. If you have Badgermole Cub and tap Elves for {G}{G}, you still only SPENT the mana on Mockingbird's cost — the extra {G} from Cub doesn't count as "mana spent to cast" if it wasn't used on Mockingbird. Only mana that actually paid for the spell counts.
Quantum Riddler Warp — Enters as 1/1, Not 4/6
"Warp {1}{U}" lets you cast Quantum Riddler for {1}{U} instead of {3}{U}{U}. It enters as a 1/1 (warped version). The ETB draw and the card-doubling ability still work regardless of Warp. A 1/1 that draws a card on entry and doubles future draws when your hand is empty is still excellent for {1}{U}. Nature's Rhythm for X=5 bypasses Warp entirely — it puts the full 4/6 version directly onto the battlefield.
Voice of Victory (SB) — Opponents Can't Cast Spells on Your Turn
{1}{W} 1/3 Human Bard. Mobilize 2. "Your opponents can't cast spells during your turn." This shuts off: all instant-speed interaction, combat tricks, flash creatures. They can't Torch the Tower your attacker, can't counter your Nature's Rhythm, can't flash in blockers. Board in against: Jeskai Control (shuts off all their counterspells on your turn), Izzet Prowess (no combat tricks). Mobilize 2 creates two 1/1 tokens on attack — more bodies for Craterhoof count.
Doorkeeper Thrull (SB) — ETBs Don't Trigger
{1}{W} 1/2 Flash Flying Thrull. "Artifacts and creatures entering don't cause abilities to trigger." This stops: Heartfire Hero valiant (triggered by targeting), Ouroboroid's combat counter trigger on the OPPONENT'S Ouroboroid (if mirror), Orcish Bowmasters ETB (no ping, no Orc Army), all ETB effects. Board in against: any deck relying heavily on ETB triggers. Flash means you can deploy it during their turn before their key ETB creature.
Wistfulness (SB) — Two Modes Based on Colors Spent
{3}{G/U}{G/U} 6/5 Elemental Incarnation. "If {G}{G} was spent: exile target artifact or enchantment." "If {U}{U} was spent: draw 2, discard 1." You CAN get BOTH modes if you spend {G}{G}{U}{U} + {1} = {1}{G}{G}{U}{U}. Naturalize + card filter in one creature. Evoke {G/U}{G/U}: sacrifice on entry but ETBs still trigger. Evoke for {G}{G} = free Naturalize. Evoke for {U}{U} = draw 2 discard 1 for 2 mana.
Spider-Sense (SB) — Web-Slinging Reduces Cost
{1}{U} instant. "Web-slinging {U}: cast for {U} if you also return a tapped creature you control to hand." Counter target instant, sorcery, or triggered ability. For {U} you bounce your own tapped mana dork (replay next turn) and counter their spell/trigger. This turns a used dork into a free counterspell. Board in against combo (counter their key spell), against Izzet (counter their burn/cantrip mid-combat).
Unable to Scream (SB) — Permanent Shutdown on Any Creature
{U} Aura. "Enchanted creature loses all abilities and is a Toy artifact creature with base P/T 0/2." This permanently neutralizes any creature — Ouroboroid becomes a 0/2 Toy. Screaming Nemesis becomes a 0/2 Toy. No abilities, no threat, no combat relevance. For {U}. The "can't be turned face up" clause is relevant against manifest/morph (Standard doesn't have much of this currently but the clause exists).
Sab-Sunen, Luxa Embodied (SB) — Even/Odd Counter Math
{3}{G}{U} 6/6 Legendary God. Reach, trample, indestructible. "Can't attack or block unless it has an even number of counters (zero is even)." Starts with 0 counters (even) → CAN attack immediately. First main phase: +1/+1 counter (now 1 = odd) → can't attack, BUT draws 2 cards. Next turn: another counter (2 = even) → can attack as 8/8 indestructible trample. Alternates: odd turns draw 2, even turns attack. A Nature's Rhythm for X=5 puts this indestructible god directly onto the battlefield.

Matchup Guide

MatchupRoleVerdictKey Card
Izzet ProwessRamp AggroEvenMeltstrider's Resolve — fight removal that also buffs

Race matchup. They're faster on raw damage (T3 kills possible with Slickshot), but you're bigger. An Ouroboroid that survives one combat step makes your board larger than theirs. Meltstrider's Resolve on a 3-power creature fights and kills their Slickshot (1 toughness regardless of pump) while giving +0/+2 to survive. Their removal (Burst Lightning, Torch the Tower) kills your dorks but can't efficiently answer Ouroboroid or Quantum Riddler. Post-board: Voice of Victory shuts off their combat tricks entirely.

% Point Edges

+3 pts: Resolve on Ouroboroid fighting Slickshot. Slickshot is always 1 toughness regardless of +2/+0 pumps. Your 1/3 Ouroboroid fights it and wins, gaining +0/+2 = 1/5. Slickshot dies. Your Ouroboroid lives and grows next combat.

Mono-Green LandfallCombo FinishFavorableCraterhoof — your creatures are bigger, and you have the one-card kill

Both decks go wide with creatures. Both grow their boards. But you have Craterhoof — the instant kill they don't have. You also have Nature's Rhythm to tutor it. Their Scythecat and Innkeeper's Talent grow creatures, but Ouroboroid grows your ENTIRE board exponentially. In a stalled board: Nature's Rhythm for Craterhoof ends the game. They have no equivalent finisher.

% Point Edges

+4 pts: Craterhoof into a stalled board. Both sides have 5+ creatures. You tutor Craterhoof. They don't have an equivalent. +5/+5 and trample to everything = lethal through their blockers.

Dimir MidrangeRamp AggroEvenNature's Rhythm — they can't interact with a sorcery-speed tutor

They have removal (Go for the Throat) and hand disruption (Duress). Their plan is to pick apart your hand and answer your threats one-by-one. Your plan: produce more threats than they have answers. Nature's Rhythm puts a creature directly onto the battlefield — it can't be Duressed (sorcery, not creature) and the creature enters without being cast (dodges Counterspell on the creature itself). Post-board: Sab-Sunen is indestructible — their removal can't answer it.

Izzet LessonsToolboxSlightly UnfavorableVoice of Victory — shuts off instant-speed interaction

Lessons has Torch the Tower to kill your dorks, Learn effects for card advantage, and flexible interaction. They're also faster to close than pure control. The matchup is slightly unfavorable G1 because their removal efficiently answers your mana creatures and slows your engine. Post-board: Voice of Victory ("opponents can't cast spells during your turn") shuts off all their instant-speed interaction. Spider-Sense gives you countermagic for their key spells.

Jeskai ControlRamp AggroFavorableNature's Rhythm — overwhelm their answer count

Control decks want to answer one threat at a time. You present 4+ threats per turn. Sunfall sweeps your board but you rebuild faster than they can find another sweeper. Nature's Rhythm into Craterhoof is a one-card kill they need a counterspell for — if they don't have it, game over. Harmonize from GY means one Nature's Rhythm = two tutor effects. They can't counter both. Post-board: Voice of Victory + Sab-Sunen (indestructible) makes their life miserable.

Chapter 09

Heuristics

  1. 01
    T1 mana creature is non-negotiable. Mulligan any hand without a T1 mana dork. The deck needs acceleration to function. 12 one-drops = ~87% to have one in a 7-card hand.
  2. 02
    Badgermole Cub on T2 doubles everything. If you have Cub and a choice between playing Cub T2 or another dork T2: always Cub. The mana doubling compounds — T3 with Cub is 5+ mana vs 4 without.
  3. 03
    Nature's Rhythm X should match the game state, not just the biggest threat. Don't always tutor Craterhoof. X=3 for Formidable Speaker (tutor another creature to hand) when you need to build. X=4 for Ouroboroid when you need a board that grows. X=8 for Craterhoof only when you have 5+ creatures and need to win THIS turn.
  4. 04
    Ouroboroid grows exponentially. Protect it for one turn and the game is over. If Ouroboroid survives two combat steps, your board is +3/+3 across everything. Three steps: +7/+7. Don't block with Ouroboroid — ever. It needs to live.
  5. 05
    Mockingbird copying Ouroboroid is the strongest play in the deck. Two exponential combat triggers = double counters per turn. Both triggers resolve at beginning of combat. Each Ouroboroid sees the other's grown power for the next turn. Copy your own Ouroboroid, not the opponent's best creature, unless their creature wins immediately.
  6. 06
    Spider Manifestation untaps on MV 4+ cast — chain big spells. Tap Spider for mana → cast MV 4+ → Spider untaps → tap again → cast another spell if you have mana. With Cub: 4+ mana per Spider per big spell cast.
  7. 07
    Harmonize from GY is your insurance policy. If Nature's Rhythm resolves and gets the creature you need, it goes to GY. Later: harmonize finds a SECOND creature. One card = two tutors. Plan your first Nature's Rhythm knowing you get a second cast later.
  8. 08
    Gene Pollinator taps any untapped permanent, not just creatures. A land that hasn't tapped yet, an artifact that isn't being used, even a planeswalker — all valid targets for Pollinator's mana ability. This makes Pollinator your most flexible mana source.
  9. 09
    Formidable Speaker's discard is optional — but you should almost always discard. The tutor is worth more than almost any card in your hand. Discard a land or a redundant mana creature to find Craterhoof or Ouroboroid.
  10. 10
    Craterhoof counts itself. Hoof + 5 other creatures = X=6. Each creature gets +6/+6. Hoof itself is 11/11 haste trample. Don't forget to count Hoof in the X calculation.
Chapter 10

Tuning

Bant vs Simic
The White Splash
Fortunati's build splashes white for: Temple Garden, Hushwood Verge, Brightglass Gearhulk, Seam Rip, and SB cards (Voice of Victory, Doorkeeper Thrull, Unable to Scream). The white adds powerful interaction and tutoring that pure Simic can't access. The cost: slightly less consistent mana (need {W} sources). In a meta where interaction matters (control, mirrors): Bant is better. In a pure race meta: Simic is leaner.
Ouroboroid Count: 1 vs 2+
Toolbox vs Redundancy
Fortunati runs only 1 Ouroboroid. With 4 Nature's Rhythm + 3 Formidable Speaker, you have 7 ways to find it. Running 2 adds redundancy (draw it naturally more often) but dilutes your silver bullet slots. In a diverse meta: 1 Ouroboroid + more silver bullets. In a creature-stall meta: 2 Ouroboroid to guarantee the exponential engine.
Keen-Eyed Curator Main vs SB
