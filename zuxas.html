Who I Am

"I approach competition the same way I approached my time as a Fire Controlman: structured, analytical, and focused on execution."

I'm Jermey Wallace — gamer handle Zuxas. Competitive Magic: The Gathering player, team captain of Team Resolve, Navy veteran, husband, and father of two daughters. Five Regional Championship qualifications. The grind is ongoing.

I served from 2012 to 2018 as a Fire Controlman working on advanced combat systems: TAS, MK23, SPQ-9B, NATO Seasparrow, and RAM. That background shaped how I think about preparation, systems, and performing under pressure — skills that translate directly to high-level competitive Magic.

Started playing during Saviors of Kamigawa. Deep competitive history across Pioneer, Modern, and Standard. I also built the MTG Meta Analyzer — a custom Python desktop tool that pulls tournament data from multiple sources, tracks archetype performance, and generates sideboard guides. It's part of how Team Resolve stays ahead of the field.

The Philosophy

  • EndureStay consistent through variance. Lose the right way and keep going. A bad beat is information. Process it and return to the table.
  • AdaptAdjust to the meta faster than your opponents do. Deck selection is a skill. The player who reads the room correctly wins more than the player who pilots perfectly.
  • DelveGo deeper than surface-level play. Surface-level loses to someone who studied. Every matchup has a correct Meddling Mage name, a correct Vial counter count, a correct attack sequence.

The Pet Deck

5-Color
Humans

"So you want to race, do you? Every creature is a question they have to answer. I have 37 of them."

5-Color Humans is my personal pet deck — not tier 1, not the optimal choice for any given event, and absolutely the most fun deck to sleeve up in Modern. It combines an aggressive creature-based gameplan with a disruption package that breaks opponent's game plans simultaneously. Aether Vial makes the creatures uncounterable and deploys them at instant speed. Meddling Mage names their win condition. Lavinia stops their free spells. Norin the Wary generates free counter pumps on Champion of the Parish and Thalia's Lieutenant every single turn for zero mana. Satoru, the Infiltrator draws a card every time Vial cheats in a creature.

I run it at local events. I recommend it to anyone who wants to play a deck that demands they know every interaction at the table — their own as much as their opponent's.

Key Synergies

The Core Loop
Norin + Champion + Lieutenant
Norin exiles whenever any spell is cast or opponent attacks, returns at next end step. With Champion and Lieutenant in play: each Norin return = +1/+1 on both. Two full trigger cycles per round (their turn + your turn) = +2/+2 on each for zero mana investment. By turn 5 both can be 6/6+ without ever spending a card on them.
Card Draw Engine
Satoru + Vial
Satoru draws when nontoken creatures enter if no mana was spent. Vial puts creatures onto the battlefield — no mana spent. Every Vial activation with Satoru in play replaces the card used. Instant-speed deployment at their end step = a draw. Their counterspells literally cannot interact. This is the deck's answer to the "you run out of gas" criticism.
Protection Package
Jirina Sacrifice + Sylvan Safekeeper
Jirina: sacrifice in response to sweeper → whole team gains hexproof and indestructible. Supreme Verdict resolves, does nothing. Sylvan Safekeeper: sacrifice a land → target creature gains shroud. Two completely independent protection axes mean Path to Exile, Supreme Verdict, and single-target removal all have answers available every turn.
Discard Synergy
Razorkin + Territorial Opponents
Razorkin Needlehead deals 1 damage whenever any opponent draws a card. Against card-draw-heavy decks (Satoru, Looting, UW Control's Snapcaster), Razorkin passively deals 3-6 damage over a game. First strike during your turn means it also wins most 2/2-or-less combat trades cleanly. An overlooked reach source.
Wide Board Payoff
Adeline + Guide of Souls Energy
Adeline's power = number of creatures you control. On a board of 5 other creatures she attacks as a 6/4 and creates attacking Human tokens (one per opponent attacked). Each token triggers Guide of Souls for energy. Guide then converts energy into +1/+1 counters and flying on your largest attacker. Adeline deploys into a developed board; she's not a threat, she's a multiplier.
Instant-Speed Hate
Vial + Silver Bullets
Vial at 2: flash Meddling Mage at their end step naming their combo piece — already named before their draw step. Vial at 3: flash Jirina to exile their GY before they reanimate. Vial at 4: flash Witch Enchanter to destroy their Blood Moon. Every tick count opens a new threat axis. The opponent never knows which answer is coming until it's already in play.
Counter Tax Layer
Unsettled Mariner
Whenever you or a permanent you control becomes the target of a spell or ability, Mariner taxes it {1}. Every single targeted removal spell — Path to Exile, Fatal Push, Galvanic Discharge, Solitude — costs one extra mana. Against Boros Energy this makes Galvanic Discharge cost {1} more, slowing their removal math on turns 2-3. Against Control it compounds every answer they have to pay.
Graveyard Hate Built-In
Jirina ETB + Cavern Naming
Jirina's ETB exiles target player's graveyard — this is your maindeck GY hate that also triggers Champion and Lieutenant. Against Living End: Vial Jirina at their end step before cascade, exile their cycled creature pile. Against Grixis Reanimator: exile Archon before they Persist or Goryo's. The key is timing — Vial her in at instant speed, not on your main phase.
Lavinia Lock
Free Spells Blanked
Lavinia counters any spell cast without spending mana. Living End via Cascade = countered. Force of Negation = countered. Evoke Solitude = countered. Summoner's Pact = countered. A resolved Lavinia + Meddling Mage naming the opponent's primary win condition is a two-card soft lock for {W}{U} and {W}{U} respectively. Both are Vial-deployable at 2 counters.
