"Endure. Adapt. Delve." — The three principles that govern how we prepare, test, and compete.

The Mission

Team Resolve exists to compete at the highest level of local and regional Magic: The Gathering. Every decision we make — deck selection, sideboard construction, event scheduling — runs through a structured preparation process backed by real match data. The goal is Pro Tour qualification. The process is what gets us there.

Endure
Stay consistent through variance. Lose the right way and keep going. A bad beat is information — process it and return to the table.
Adapt
Adjust to the meta faster than opponents do. Deck selection is a skill. The player who reads the room correctly wins more than the perfect pilot.
Delve
Go deeper than surface-level play. Every matchup has a correct Meddling Mage name, a correct attack sequence, a correct sideboard line. We find it.

How We Prepare

Before any RC registration, every candidate deck runs a structured gauntlet: win rates against the expected top 8 matchups, sideboard game simulations across 3+ sessions, and full matchup-specific heuristics written out before the event. If the numbers aren't there, the deck doesn't go to the table.

All playbooks, meta data, and prep sheets are public. The information should be out there. Better-prepared players make better tournament fields, and sharing the process is how the community gets stronger.
