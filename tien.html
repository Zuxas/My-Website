Background

How long have you been playing?
Since Neon Dynasty — February 2022. Less than four years ago. What's happened since then would take most players a decade to accomplish.
What got you into competitive play?
Couldn't lose at the kitchen table. His friends told him he needed a format with real restrictions. He picked Modern. Then proceeded to learn the game much faster than most people do and immediately wanted to grind.

Results

Proudest result?
Winning his first RCQ after playing for just over a year. Then making it a pattern — he's qualified for every competitive season he's cared about in both Modern and Pioneer since. He has not failed to qualify for a season he's genuinely tried. That's not a streak that happens by accident.

The Player

Tien is a greedy combo player. Not reckless — greedy. There's a difference. Reckless players take bad lines and lose to variance. Greedy players push margins that other pilots leave on the table, and know exactly which margins those are. The "just cut a land" mentality isn't sloppiness. It's a calibrated read on what the deck actually needs versus what conventional wisdom says it needs. With Mono-U Belcher, he's right.

"What do I die to. How do I win. What does a winning game look like. How do I thread the needle to steal the match." — His framework, in order
How do you think at the table?
Four questions before anything else: What do I die to. How do I win. What does a winning game look like. How do I thread the needle. He knows his decklist cold. He can reconstruct a majority of his opponent's list from memory before seeing a card. The game is already partially solved before he sits down.
What do most players get wrong?
Pressure points. Most players don't have a clear model for when the situation forces them to go for it versus when they have margin to sculpt a better position. Tien does. He knows which turns are free and which aren't — and that creates decisions his opponents never see coming.
The Deck He Always Returns To
Mono-U Belcher — since MH3. Consistent combo that uses interaction and free spells to win on turn 4, but can drop into a draw-go control game when needed. Fitting deck for a pilot who wants to play fewer lands than everyone else and be right about it.
How He Ended Up on Team Resolve
He kept beating Zuxas. Zuxas bought him a hat. The rest followed.

2026 Season

Goal for this RC season?
Keep the streak. He hasn't failed to qualify for a competitive season he's tried yet. Based on everything above, that's not changing.
