Player Profile

Tien

"Just cut a land."

Tien is a greedy combo gamer. His advice on any mana problem, any slow draw, any clunky hand: just cut a land. He knows the fastest theoretical kill turn of every combo deck in the format and is always two lands away from the configuration that goes off on turn two. The upside: deep mechanical knowledge of stack interactions, timing windows, and combo sequencing at a level most players never develop. When the deck works, it's untouchable.

The "just cut a land" variance is the price of admission for the ceiling that combo offers. Tien pays it willingly every time.

Playstyle

Identity
Greedy Combo
Tien's approach: identify the most powerful combo available, build toward the theoretical maximum, and cut a land to get there. If the deck can go off on turn two, that's the version he builds. "Just cut a land" is both advice and philosophy — the ceiling is always worth chasing, and the mana base is always the first thing to sacrifice to reach it.
