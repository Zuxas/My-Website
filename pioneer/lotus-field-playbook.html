Chapter 01

DECK
IDENTITY

"This is the only deck in Pioneer that generates unlimited mana with lands alone. Once you have two Lotus Fields and one Hidden Strings, every subsequent turn you can cast every spell in your deck. The opponent is playing a fair game while you break the rules of mana entirely."

Lotus Field Combo is Pioneer's most complex and uniquely powerful combo deck. The engine is built around Lotus Field — a land that enters tapped, requires sacrificing two lands when it enters, has hexproof, and taps for three mana of any one color. On its own it's a difficult setup. But with Thespian's Stage copying it (Stage already entered the battlefield, so no sacrifice trigger), you have two lands that each tap for three mana — six mana total from two lands.

From there, Hidden Strings ({1U} instant: untap two target permanents, Cipher it onto a creature) costs two mana and untaps both Lotus Fields, generating four net mana each activation. Pore Over the Pages ({4U}: draw 3, discard 1, untap 2 lands) also generates net mana while drawing cards. Chain enough of these with Vizier of Tumbling Sands (cycle {2U}: untap target land) and you assemble enough mana and cards to cast Emergent Ultimatum — an {BBBGGGG} sorcery that finds three cards from your library, lets the opponent choose one to exile, and you cast the other two for free.

The Ultimatum line resolves into Omniscience (cast all spells for free) and the ability to cast every remaining card in your hand and deck. From there, Fae of Wishes's Granted adventure fetches whatever win condition you need from your sideboard. The deck is unusual in Pioneer because it's completely immune to most creature removal, plays through typical counterspells via Fae of Wishes fetching answers, and wins in a way no other Pioneer deck can replicate.

Why Lotus Field At 4% Pioneer

Unique
Hexproof Lands Can't Be Targeted
Lotus Field has hexproof — it can't be targeted by spells or abilities opponents control. Field of Ruin can't destroy it. Single-target removal doesn't exist for it. The primary hate is Damping Sphere (stops multi-mana lands) and High Noon (stops untapping). Most decks have zero maindeck answers.
Speed
T4–5 Consistent Kill
Arboreal Grazer on T1 + Lotus Field on T2 + Thespian's Stage on T3 + Hidden Strings loop on T4 = kill on T4. The combo kills faster than most control decks can marshal answers. Greasefang's T3 is faster, but Lotus beats every non-combo deck before they stabilize.
Resilience
Fae of Wishes is a Toolbox
Fae of Wishes's Granted adventure searches your sideboard for any card. This means your 15-card sideboard is a toolbox of every answer, win condition, and backup you could need. Hate card ruining your plan? Granted fetches Boseiju, Who Endures. Need a sweeper? Granted finds it. Opponents must answer both your combo AND your toolbox.

GAME
FLOW

"Survive. Find Lotus Field. Copy it with Stage. Untap with Hidden Strings. Cast Emergent Ultimatum. Win."
Setup (T1–T3)
Land Assembly + Survival
T1: Arboreal Grazer (put Lotus Field from hand onto battlefield tapped) OR Sylvan Scrying (find Lotus/Stage). T2: Play Thespian's Stage. Arboreal Grazer blocks 1-power attackers as a 0/3. T3: Play Lotus Field — sacrifice exactly two non-Lotus lands. Now have one Lotus Field producing {3}. Don't cast Hidden Strings yet — save for the full combo turn.
Assembly (T4)
Stage Copies Lotus
Tap both Lotus Field + Stage for 6 mana floating. Activate Stage: pay {2}, copy Lotus Field. Stage's ETB as Lotus Field says sacrifice two other lands — sacrifice 2 basics. Now have 6 mana floating and 2 Lotus Fields (both Hexproof). This is the pivotal moment: you now generate 6 mana per turn and the engine is assembled. Use remaining mana for Impulse/Vizier cycle.
Combo (T5+)
Hidden Strings → Cipher Loop → Win
Cast Hidden Strings, untap both Fields, Cipher on Grazer. Attack with Grazer. Cipher triggers — untap both Fields for free. Use 6 mana for Pore Over the Pages (draw 3, untap Fields again). Repeat 2-3 times. Now have 15-20 mana and enough cards. Cast Dig Through Time (delve, {U}) → find Emergent Ultimatum → win.
Mulligan
Need a Path to Lotus Field by T3
Keep: any hand with Lotus Field, OR Sylvan Scrying/Arboreal Grazer to find it, and 3 lands including one GU dual. Mulligan: no path to Lotus before T5, hands with 5+ lands and 0 spells, hands with only finishers. At 6: Grazer + Lotus + 2 basics is excellent. At 5: Sylvan Scrying + 2 lands + 1 untap = keepable. At 4: Lotus + Stage + 2 basics + Impulse is functional.

Engine 1 — Mana Base
Lotus Field × 2 = 6 Mana
Two Lotus Fields produce 6 mana of any color. Hidden Strings costs {1U} = 2 mana → net +4. Pore Over the Pages costs {4U} = 5 mana, untaps 3 Fields (or 2 + basics) → net +1 and draws 3. Engine 2 — Cipher Multiplier
Hidden Strings on Grazer
Cipher: exile Hidden Strings encoded on Arboreal Grazer. Whenever Grazer deals combat damage to a player, copy Hidden Strings and cast it for free. Chapter 05

LINES &
TACTICS

"Float mana before sacrifices. Cipher on Grazer only. Set Ultimatum so every split wins."
Line 01 — Float Mana Before Every Land Sacrifice
When Stage copies Lotus Field, the ETB "sacrifice two other lands" triggers on the stack. Before it resolves: tap Lotus Field AND Stage for 6 mana floating. THEN let the sacrifice trigger resolve (sacrificing two basics). You now have 6 mana floating and two Lotus Fields in play. If you sacrifice first without floating, you lose those mana entirely for the turn. This is the single most common mistake in the deck and costs tempo every time it happens.
Line 02 — Cipher on Grazer, Not Vizier
When encoding Cipher from Hidden Strings, always choose Arboreal Grazer over Vizier of Tumbling Sands. Grazer (0/3) survives combat against most 1-2 power attackers and deals 0 combat damage when attacking into a wall — meaning it CAN'T trigger Cipher (requires dealing combat damage). Plan around this: if they have a 0/4 or larger defender, use Vizier's tap ability or Pore Over the Pages instead of relying on Grazer attacking through. Vizier as the Cipher target is better if Grazer dies but Vizier doesn't attack for damage either.
Line 03 — Emergent Ultimatum — Set Up the Unsplittable Win
The opponent separates your three targets into two piles and you choose one pile. Structure it so every split wins: Omniscience + Peer into the Abyss + Thassa's Oracle. If they give you Omniscience + Peer: cast both free, draw half your deck, Oracle wins. If they give you Omniscience + Oracle: cast both free, draw cards with Oracle's devotion check. If they give you Peer + Oracle: cast Peer (draw half deck, lose half life) → cast Oracle → win. All three splits win. Practice this before every event.
Line 04 — Damping Sphere: Blast Zone or Boseiju Before Comboing
Damping Sphere makes each additional spell cost {1} more. Against your combo: Hidden Strings costs {3}, Pore costs {6}. Your net mana gain drops dramatically. Answer sequence: (1) Activate Blast Zone for {2} mana to add 2 charge counters, then activate for {0} → destroys all MV-2 permanents including Damping Sphere. OR (2) Wish for Boseiju, Who Endures → destroy target artifact at instant speed. Then proceed normally. NEVER attempt the full combo through Damping Sphere — the math doesn't work.

HEURISTICS

"Float mana before sacrificing. Cipher on Grazer. Destroy hate before comboing. Ultimatum: every split wins."
  1. 01Always float mana before Lotus Field ETB sacrifice resolves. Before the "sacrifice two other lands" trigger resolves when Stage copies Lotus, tap Lotus AND Stage for 6 mana. The most common mistake — sacrificing lands before floating costs you the entire turn's mana.
  2. 02Activate Thespian's Stage the same turn you play it. Stage is the only targetable piece of your combo. If they see it on the field for a full turn, they bounce or destroy it before you copy Lotus. Play Stage, float mana, immediately activate Stage's copy ability — don't give them a turn to answer it.
  3. 03Cipher on Arboreal Grazer (0/3) — it survives combat and blocks. Cipher on Vizier loses the creature to 1/1s attacking back. Cipher on Grazer keeps the Cipher encoded through multiple combat phases. The Cipher only fires when dealing combat damage to a player — plan your attack to ensure Grazer actually connects.
  4. 04Structure Emergent Ultimatum so every two-card pile wins. Standard package: Omniscience + Peer into the Abyss + Thassa's Oracle. Any two wins. Don't pick three cards where one clean split loses — opponents will always find the losing pile if it exists.
  5. 05Destroy Damping Sphere before attempting the combo. The Sphere makes each additional spell cost {1} more. Through Sphere, your net mana gain drops too low to generate the mana needed. Use Blast Zone (MV 2 = activate for {2}) or wish for Boseiju. Never start the combo sequence through an active Damping Sphere.
  6. 06Vizier of Tumbling Sands: cycle (draw + untap) beats tap ability. Cycling Vizier for {1G}: draw a card AND untap a Field (+3 mana). Net: +1 mana, +1 card. Using the tap ability: untap a Field (+3 mana) but no draw. Cycle almost always — except when you'd be forced to discard a critical card to hand size.
  7. 07Dig Through Time: exile basics from your GY, not combo pieces. The sacrificed basics from Lotus ETB go to your GY and are the perfect delve fuel. Exile: basic lands, spent Impulses, spent Vizier cycles. Never exile Arboreal Grazer from GY (you might need it as a second Cipher target) or Lotus Field (shouldn't be in GY but check).
  8. 08Fae of Wishes: save Granted for the right moment, don't deploy Fae as a creature. A 1/4 blocker is almost never worth the card. Save Fae to cast Granted either: (1) pre-combo to find a sweeper vs aggro, or (2) mid-combo to find Omniscience. Deploying Fae as a creature wastes the critical wish effect.
  9. 09Blast Zone answers Damping Sphere at MV 2 — plan for it. Blast Zone enters with one charge counter. Activate for {2}: add 2 counters (now has 3). Activate for {0}: destroy all nonland permanents with MV equal to the number of charge counters = MV 2. Total cost: {2} + Blast Zone itself. This is your on-board answer that doesn't use the wishboard.
  10. 10Behold the Beyond ({5BBB}) is your backup finisher when Cipher fails. If Grazer is removed before Cipher resolves, you may have 12-15 mana from natural Fields. Cast Behold the Beyond (black available from Fields). Draw 7 cards. Find everything you need. This costs 8 mana and skips the Cipher loop entirely — plan for this line when Grazer dies early.

TUNING
NOTES

"3 Lotus, 4 Stage, 4 Hidden Strings, 4 Pore are immovable. Everything else responds to meta."
Lock
3 Lotus + 4 Stage + 4 Hidden Strings + 4 Pore
The four irreducible combo pieces. 3 Lotus is correct (Stage copies it; 4 is dead draws in multiples). 4 Stage ensures you always find the copy. Hidden Strings + Pore are both mandatory — neither alone generates enough mana for the combo turn. Never cut any of these.
Lock
4 Dig Through Time + 4 Sylvan Scrying
Dig finds every missing piece at {U} after filling GY. Sylvan finds Lotus or Stage on T2 ensuring the land assembly. Both run as 4-of to maximize the probability of seeing them in the setup turns. Cutting either below 4 makes hands inconsistent.
Flex 3–4
Fae of Wishes
3 is reference. 4 in metas where the wishboard provides essential answers you need early (heavy aggro). 3 in metas where you combo before needing the wishboard. More Fae means more mid-combo wish targets but slower setup turns.

Chapter 09

HEURISTICS

"Lotus has hexproof. Stage copying it has no sacrifice trigger. Hidden Strings nets +4. Know these facts perfectly."
  1. 01Thespian's Stage copies Lotus Field WITHOUT sacrificing two lands. Lotus Field's sacrifice trigger only fires when it enters the battlefield. Stage entered before it activated its ability. Activating Stage to become a copy of Lotus Field does not trigger the sacrifice. This is the fundamental rule the entire combo is built on — know it cold.
  2. 02Lotus Field has hexproof — opponents cannot target it. Field of Ruin, Ghost Quarter, and all targeted removal/bounce cannot target Lotus Field. The primary hate is nontargeted: Damping Sphere (restricts tapping), High Noon (prevents untapping). Know which effects are targeted (can't work) vs nontargeted (can work).
  3. 03Into the Flood Maw bouncing Lotus Field triggers the sacrifice. If your Lotus Field is bounced back to your hand, when you replay it, the "enter the battlefield" sacrifice trigger fires again — you sacrifice two more lands. Bouncing Lotus Field is catastrophic. Don't let it happen when you're ahead. Execute the combo turn before they can bounce.
  4. 04Hidden Strings generates +4 mana per cast. Pore Over the Pages generates +2 mana and draws 3. Know the net mana values: Hidden Strings ({1U}) + two Fields (6) = net +4. Pore ({4U}) + two Fields (6) = net +2 and 3 cards drawn. Vizier cycle ({2U}) = untap one Field (net +1). Chain these to reach Ultimatum mana ({3BBBGGG} = 10 mana total).
  5. 05Always know which Emergent Ultimatum pile wins regardless of what they exile. Before casting Ultimatum, construct your pile so that any one card they exile still results in a win. The standard pile: Omniscience + Behold the Beyond + Dark Petition is constructed so each subset of two wins. Know this before casting — don't figure it out mid-resolution.
  6. 06Damping Sphere prevents Lotus from tapping for more than one mana — answer it immediately. With Damping Sphere in play, Lotus taps for {G} or {U} (one mana, not three). Your engine stops completely. Granted for Boseiju before attempting any combo is mandatory. Never try to combo through Damping Sphere — kill it first.
  7. 07High Noon prevents untapping — answer it before starting the loop. High Noon says players can't untap more lands than they had at the start of their turn. Hidden Strings no longer works. Granted for Boseiju or Blast Zone before starting your combo turn. Check for High Noon every time before you initiate the loop.
  8. 08Lier, Disciple of the Drowned makes your spells uncounterable AND gives them flashback. When Lier is in play, opponents cannot counter your spells (your spells gain "can't be countered"). Against UW Control: resolve Lier, then everything is free. The flashback clause also lets you cast Hidden Strings and cantrips from GY if you have mana. Protect Lier with counterspells or execute immediately after it resolves.
  9. 09Fae of Wishes is a 1/4 blocker — use it to buy time in aggro matchups. Don't always use Fae purely for Granted. In aggro matchups, a 1/4 blocker stops multiple attacks from small creatures.
