Player Profile

It's Enough

"Thoughtseize on one, Fatal Push on two. That's the whole plan. It's enough." — Troubleshot

Caleb — Troubleshot — is a mono-black control mage through and through. The game plan starts with Thoughtseize stripping the opponent's best card and Fatal Push answering their best threat. Simple, clean, and deeply effective in the right field. Standard is his preferred format and where his results live. The deliberate, reactive nature of black control rewards the kind of disciplined decision-making he brings to every match — knowing when to strip a hand, when to push a threat, and when to let the opponent stumble on their own.

The Philosophy

  • DisruptEvery Thoughtseize is a read on their hand. Every Push is precision removal. The disruption isn't random — it's targeted at the card that breaks the matchup if it resolves.
  • DismantleHe doesn't race opponents. He dismantles them. One-for-one trading backed by inevitability. Card advantage in mono-black comes from grinding the opponent into bad decisions.
  • DeliberateStandard is a format where the deliberate player wins. Black control is a deck where the deliberate player gets paid for it. The pet deck is the correct deck when the meta rewards patience over speed.
