Background

What's the deck?
Mono-black control, through and through. The game plan starts with Thoughtseize stripping the opponent's best card and Fatal Push answering their best threat. Simple, clean, and deeply effective in the right field.
Why mono-black?
Standard is the preferred format and where the results live. The deliberate, reactive nature of black control rewards the kind of disciplined decision-making Caleb brings to every match — knowing when to strip a hand, when to push a threat, and when to let the opponent stumble on their own.

Identity

Caleb doesn't race opponents — he dismantles them. Every Thoughtseize is a read on their hand. Every Push is precision removal. One-for-one disruption backed by inevitability. The pet deck is the correct deck when the meta rewards patience over speed.

"Standard is a format where the deliberate player wins. Black control is a deck where the deliberate player gets paid for it." — The Troubleshot framework

Approach

Reactive
Doesn't impose his game plan first. Lets the opponent commit, then dismantles what they put on the table.
Deliberate
No wasted Thoughtseize. No mis-timed Push. The discard target and the removal target are decided before he taps the lands.
One-for-One
Trades cleanly. Card advantage in mono-black comes from inevitability, not card draw. The plan is to grind the opponent into bad decisions.
Standard-Native
Knows the format cold. The format rotation favors players who study the new metas hardest — he does.

2026 Season
