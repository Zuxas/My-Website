Player Profile

Burn It Down

"Lightning Bolt you. Lightning Bolt you. Lightning Bolt you. Okay your turn I guess." — Foxy

John — Foxy — plays red. When in doubt, he plays red. When not in doubt, he also plays red. His natural home is aggressive red-leaning decks where the game plan is simple, the execution window is small, and the opponent doesn't get a lot of turns to figure out what's happening. The appeal of red aggro isn't just the simplicity — it's the pressure. Foxy applies it from turn one and never lets up. Every point of damage is intentional. He understands burn math, knows his outs, and plays combat steps precisely. The deck looks easy from the outside, but the margin between a good red pilot and an average one is visible in every attack declaration.

The Philosophy

  • PressureFrom turn one. The opponent's first decision is whether they can survive long enough to play their game. Red doesn't lose to bad matchups. Red loses to bad pilots.
  • MathBurn math, not vibes. He knows the outs. He counts damage three turns ahead. The reach is calculated, not hoped for. The opponent's life total is just a clock.
  • CombatAttack steps are where average red pilots leak equity. Foxy's attacks are where he gets paid. Every point of damage is intentional. Every decline-to-block is filed away for later.
