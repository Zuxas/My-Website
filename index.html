Team Resolve · Modern · Competitive Magic

who is team resolve?

Team Resolve is a competitive Magic: The Gathering team focused on Modern. We build the deepest playbooks in the format, run full gauntlet testing before every RC, and share everything we learn publicly. Led by Zuxas — five-time RC qualifier, 23 RCQ Top 8s last Standard season, 17 RCQ Top 8s last Modern season on Boros Energy.

what we do

Playbooks
Full Archetype Guides
Every Team Resolve archetype gets a 10-chapter playbook: identity, decklist, game flow, core engines, role assignment, matchup deep dives, sideboard guide, heuristics, tuning notes, and a print-ready prep sheet. Not theory — tested lines built from real tournament data.
Meta Analysis
Live DB Win Rates
We run a custom aggregator pulling from 100+ tournaments and 3,700+ players per cycle. Every matchup win rate in our guides is sourced from real match data — not guesswork, not eyeballing Top 8 results. The numbers update before each RC.
Gauntlet Process
How We Lock a 75
Before any RC registration, every candidate deck runs through our gauntlet: win rates against the expected top 8 matchups, sideboard game simulations, and matchup-specific heuristics. If the numbers aren't there, the deck doesn't go to the table.
Public Resources
Everything Open
All playbooks, meta data, and prep sheets are public. The information should be out there. Better-prepared players make better tournament fields, and sharing the process is how the community gets stronger.
