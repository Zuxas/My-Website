the qualification path

For anyone following along who is also working toward the Pro Tour, here is how the system works right now:

Step 1 — RCQs (Regional Championship Qualifiers)

RCQs are held at local game stores and large events throughout each season. Winning or finishing in the top cut earns an invitation to the Regional Championship. Volume matters — but showing up isn't enough. You need a prepared deck, sideboard plan, and the reps to execute under pressure. This is where Team Resolve's gauntlet system pays off.

Step 2 — Regional Championships

RCs are large, high-level tournaments typically run over two days. Everyone there earned their spot. Top finishers qualify for the Pro Tour. Team Resolve builds full RC prep packages — matchup guides, sideboard matrices, and play-tested lines for every relevant matchup.

Find upcoming RCs at magic.gg.

Step 3 — Pro Tour

The Pro Tour is the pinnacle of organized competitive Magic. Top RC finishers earn invitations. Getting here isn't just about talent — it's about systematic preparation, format mastery, and performing under pressure over two days of high-stakes competition.

More: Magic.gg Pro Tour path.
