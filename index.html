who is team resolve?

Team Resolve is a competitive Magic: The Gathering team focused on Modern, Standard, and Pioneer. We build the deepest playbooks in each format, run full gauntlet testing before every RC, and share everything we learn publicly. Five players. One goal. Regional Championship qualification and a path to the Pro Tour.

what we do

Playbooks
44 Full Archetype Guides
Every archetype gets an 11-chapter playbook: identity, decklist, game flow, core engines, sequencing lines, role assignment, matchup deep dives, sideboard guide, heuristics, tuning notes, and a print-ready event prep sheet. Covering Modern, Standard, and Pioneer.
Meta Analysis
Live Data, Real Tournaments
Custom aggregator pulling from 100+ tournaments per cycle. Every matchup guide is sourced from real match data — not guesswork, not eyeballing Top 8 results. Meta numbers update before each RC cycle across all three formats.
Gauntlet Process
How We Lock a 75
Before any RC registration, every candidate deck runs a structured gauntlet: win rates against the expected top 8, sideboard simulations across 3+ sessions, and full matchup heuristics written out before the event. If the numbers aren't there, the deck doesn't go to the table.
Public Resources
Everything Open
All playbooks, meta data, and prep sheets are public. Better-prepared players make better tournament fields, and sharing the process is how the community gets stronger.
