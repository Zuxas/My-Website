Chapter 01

Deck
Overview

"The core plan: play creatures that ping the opponent when other creatures enter, attach Warleader's Call for a +1/+1 anthem and extra ETB pings, and overwhelm them with Warrior tokens before they can stabilize."

Boros Aggro (also called Boros Pingers or Boros Tokens) is Tier B at 55.4% win rate across 8,800 BO3 games — the 3rd-most popular deck at 7% meta share. It is the 3rd most common opponent you'll face at Cincinnati. The plan is deceptively linear: every creature that enters the battlefield pings the opponent for 1 damage (or 4 with Ojer Axonil active), Warleader's Call amplifies both the creatures' sizes and the ping damage, and Mobilize/Start Your Engines attacks flood the board with Warrior tokens.

The deck's key insight: Ojer Axonil, Deepest Might is the game-ending card. His static ability reads "if a red source would deal noncombat damage less than Ojer's power to an opponent, deal Ojer's power instead." Ojer has 4 power. So every Sharpshooter ping (1 damage) becomes 4 damage. Every Warleader's Call trigger (1 damage) becomes 4 damage. Every Stadium Headliner Mobilize token that enters (1 damage via Sharpshooter or Call) becomes 4 damage. With enough creatures entering, Ojer turns a 1-damage-per-creature engine into 4-damage-per-creature — the entire board state is instantly lethal once Ojer resolves against a creature-heavy opponent.

The Three Threat Axes

Axis 1 — Ping Engine
ETB Damage
Shocking Sharpshooter pings opponent for 1 when any creature you control enters. Warleader's Call enchantment does the same (1 damage per creature ETB). Stack both: each creature ETB deals 2 damage (or 8 with Ojer). Fanatical Firebrand entering pings for 2. Then it hasted attacks for 2. Then it dies to trade with a blocker and you {T} sacrifice it to deal 1 more. All noncombat, all going to face.
Axis 2 — Token Flood
Mobilize + Start Your Engines
Voice of Victory Mobilize 2: attacking creates two 1/1 Warriors. Stadium Headliner Mobilize 1: attacking creates one 1/1 Warrior. Howlsquad Heavy creates a Goblin token each combat (Start Your Engines). Each token ETB triggers Sharpshooter and Call. A single attack with Voice + Stadium + Heavy can create 4 creature ETBs dealing 4–16 noncombat damage before a single point of combat damage.
Axis 3 — The Amplifier
Ojer Axonil
Ojer Axonil, Deepest Might: 4/4 Legendary God with trample. Red noncombat damage you deal becomes at least 4. One Sharpshooter ping with Ojer = 4 damage instead of 1. Warleader's Call trigger = 4 instead of 1. An attack with 4 creatures creating 4 tokens = 8 ETB triggers × 4 damage = 32 noncombat damage in one attack step. Also: when Ojer dies, it returns transformed as Temple of Power — a land that can retransform at sorcery speed once you deal 4+ noncombat damage.

Meta Position

Boros Aggro is 7% of the Cincinnati meta — expect to face it 2-3 rounds. Your Dimir Midrange rates this matchup at 44% (Unfavorable). Your removal hits their creatures but can't answer their noncombat damage cleanly — Requiting Hex kills 1-2 drop creatures, but Warleader's Call is an enchantment and Ojer Axonil has trample. The key to winning as Dimir is killing Ojer before he doubles all their ping damage and using Cecil, Dark Knight's +13.9 pts impact to trade into their board while lifelink negates some of their burn.

Card Notes

The Pinger
Shocking Sharpshooter
{1}{R} 1/3 Human Archer. Reach. Whenever another creature you control enters, this creature deals 1 damage to target opponent. The "target opponent" clause means you choose — direct to face. Every token, every creature ETB pings face. Stack Sharpshooter + Warleader's Call: each creature ETB = 2 damage. Stack with Raphael: 3 damage. Stack Ojer: each of those 1-damage instances becomes 4. Multiple Sharpshooters on board: each creature ETB pings multiple times.
The Anthem
Warleader's Call
{1}{R}{W} Enchantment. Creatures you control get +1/+1. Whenever a creature you control enters, this enchantment deals 1 damage to each opponent. The global +1/+1 makes all your tokens relevant in combat. The ETB ping amplifies with Ojer — each new creature entering with Warleader's Call + Ojer = 4 damage to face. With 2 Sharpshooters + Warleader's Call + Ojer, each creature entering = 12 noncombat damage.
The Amplifier
Ojer Axonil, Deepest Might
{3}{R} 4/4 Legendary God, Trample. Red noncombat damage sources deal at least 4 instead of less. ETB/ping damage of 1 becomes 4. His death trigger: returns transformed as Temple of Power (land that can untransform with {2}{R}{T} after dealing 4+ noncombat). He's nearly impossible to permanently kill. Even when removed, he comes back as a land that re-transforms on the next attack step where your pings trigger again.
The Token Floods
Voice of Victory / Howlsquad Heavy
Voice: {1}{W} 1/3 Human Bard. Mobilize 2: whenever attacks, create 2 tapped attacking 1/1 Warrior tokens. Opponents can't cast spells your turn. Each attack = 2 Warrior ETBs = 2 Sharpshooter pings + 2 Call pings = 4 noncombat damage before combat. Howlsquad Heavy: {2}{R} 2/3 Goblin. Start Your Engines — at beginning of combat, create a 1/1 Goblin token that attacks this combat. Other Goblins have haste. Each combat step = 1 more ETB trigger. Max Speed: {T} add {R} per Goblin.
The Support
Raphael, Tough Turtle
{1}{R} 1/3 Legendary TMNT. Alliance — whenever another creature you control enters, Raphael deals 1 damage to target opponent. Another ping trigger per creature ETB. With Sharpshooter + Call + Raphael: each creature entering = 3 damage. Stack with Ojer: each creature = 12 damage (3 × 4). The 1/3 body also blocks profitably against aggressive decks and the Alliance trigger is creatures you control (includes your own tokens).
The Removal Suite
Burst Lightning / Torch the Tower / Seam Rip
Burst Lightning: 2 damage or 4 kicked. Cheap prowess trigger and removal or reach. Torch the Tower: {R} instant, 2 damage bargained = 3 + scry 1, exile on death. Exiling matters — stops GY recursion and ensures killed creatures don't come back. Seam Rip: {1}{W} enchantment. Exile target nonland permanent with MV ≤ 2 until Seam Rip leaves. The delayed exile that returns when Seam Rip leaves — used primarily on their key 1-2 drop creature or enchantment to buy turns.
The Mobilize Land
Stadium Headliner
{1}{R} 1/1 Goblin Warrior. Mobilize 1: whenever attacks, create 1 tapped attacking 1/1 Warrior token. Sacrifice for {1}{R}: deal damage = number of creatures you control to a creature. The token creation triggers Sharpshooter, Call, and Raphael. The sacrifice ability is a significant source of noncombat damage: with 8 creatures and Ojer active, sacrifice Stadium for 32 damage to one creature (or enough to kill anything). This is an uncounterable finisher that doesn't use the combat step.
The Grind Threat
Dalkovan Encampment
Land: enters tapped unless you control Swamp or Mountain. {T}: {W}. {2}{W}{T}: whenever you attack this turn, create two 1/1 red Warrior tokens tapped and attacking. The activated land provides additional token ETBs that trigger Sharpshooter, Call, and Raphael — all without using a card from hand. In mid-game when your hand is depleted, Dalkovan Encampment generates 2 creature ETB triggers per turn cycle as pure land advantage.
Chapter 03

Game Flow

"T1 Firebrand. T2 Sharpshooter + Warleader's Call. T3 Ojer Axonil. Attack step: watch the damage pile up."

Turn-by-Turn Framework

Turns 1–2: Establish
Ping Engine Online
T1: Fanatical Firebrand (haste 1/1, pings opponent next turn). T2: Shocking Sharpshooter. When Firebrand swings on T2, it pings for 1 (Sharpshooter trigger). Add Warleader's Call on T2 instead for the +1/+1 anthem — now T3 creatures ETB for 2 noncombat damage each. The T2 decision: Sharpshooter (better value with upcoming ETBs) vs Warleader's Call (+1/+1 for faster attacks).
Turn 3: The Engine
Token Generation
Voice of Victory attacking creates 2 Warriors (2 ETBs = 2-4 pings). Howlsquad Heavy creates 1 Goblin at beginning of combat (1 more ETB). Stadium Headliner creates 1 Warrior on attack. On T3 with Sharpshooter + Call + all three attackers: 4 ETB triggers = 8 noncombat damage before combat damage. That's often lethal by T4 without Ojer.
Turn 4: Close
Ojer or Lethal Math
Ojer Axonil on T4: every subsequent ETB ping is now 4 damage minimum. T5 attack with Voice + Stadium + Heavy: 4 ETBs = 16 noncombat damage + combat. Alternatively: at 8 life or less, calculate burn to face. Burst Lightning kicked = 4. Sear = 4. Sacrifice Stadium Headliner: deal damage = creature count. With 8 creatures and Ojer: 8 × 4 = 32 to one creature, or enough to close at face in noncombat.

The Ojer Math

Ojer Axonil's static: red sources that deal noncombat damage less than Ojer's power (4) instead deal 4. This applies to ALL your red sources simultaneously. With the full board assembled:

One Creature Entering (with full board)
Potential Damage Per ETB
Sharpshooter #1: 1→4 damage. Sharpshooter #2: 1→4 damage. Warleader's Call: 1→4 damage. Raphael: 1→4 damage. Total per creature ETB with 2 Sharpshooters + Call + Raphael + Ojer: 16 noncombat damage per creature that enters. This is why Boros Aggro kills out of nowhere — tokens entering the battlefield on the attack step deal 16 damage each before any combat damage resolves.
Voice of Victory Attack
One Attack Step Math
Voice attacks → Creates 2 Warriors → Each Warrior ETB deals 4 (Sharpshooter) + 4 (Call) = 8 per token → 2 tokens = 16 damage. Simultaneously: Voice deals its combat damage. Howlsquad creates its Goblin → another 8 damage. Stadium creates its Warrior → another 8 damage. Total noncombat damage from one attack step with Ojer active: 32+ noncombat damage, all going to face.
Chapter 04

Core Engines

Engine A — The Ping Stack
Layer Your Triggers
Each trigger source stacks independently. Sharpshooter #1 + Sharpshooter #2 + Warleader's Call + Raphael = 4 separate "1 damage" triggers per creature ETB. With Ojer: 4 triggers × 4 damage = 16 per ETB. The deck plays 4 Sharpshooter and 4 Warleader's Call to ensure multiple trigger sources are in play simultaneously. Even without Ojer, 4 triggers × 1 damage = 4 per ETB is significant across an attack step with 3-4 token ETBs.
Engine B — The Token Loop
Attack-Step ETBs
Tokens trigger on ENTERING — not just on ETB from a spell. Mobilize tokens (Voice, Stadium) enter "tapped and attacking" during your attack step. They trigger all ping sources simultaneously as they enter. Howlsquad's "beginning of combat" trigger creates a Goblin before attackers are declared — that Goblin also triggers pings before attacks are declared. Dalkovan Encampment creates more tokens on the attack step via its activated ability.
Chapter 05

Role Assignment

Boros Aggro is always the beatdown deck — even against other aggro decks, your noncombat damage plan is faster than trading creatures profitably. The exception: against Mono-Green Landfall and any massive-creature deck, you are the fast aggro trying to kill before they assemble enough board to alpha-strike through you.

vs Midrange/Control
Go Wide + Ping to Face
Their interactive tools (removal, counters) trade one-for-one, but you generate tokens that are inherently two-for-one (a 1/1 token that pings for 4). Deploy the Ojer + pinger + token plan as fast as possible. Force them to spend removal on Ojer and Sharpshooter — their hand empties before you run out of ETBs.
vs Fast Aggro
Race Noncombat Damage
In aggro mirrors, your noncombat damage plan is more consistent than their attack damage plan. Don't overextend into their sweepers — keep 2-3 key pieces rather than flooding the board. A Sharpshooter + Ojer is enough damage output to win the race even if they're trading creatures with you in combat.

Chapter 08

Heuristics

  1. 01
    Ojer Axonil's static applies to ALL your red noncombat damage simultaneously. Every Sharpshooter, every Warleader's Call trigger, every Raphael Alliance trigger — all become 4 damage minimum with Ojer in play. The triggers are independent: 2 Sharpshooters + 1 Call + 1 Raphael with Ojer = 16 damage per creature ETB. Prioritize resolving Ojer when you have 2+ ping sources in play already.
  2. 02
    Mobilize tokens enter "tapped and attacking" — they trigger Sharpshooter and Call on entry, not on declare attackers. Voice of Victory's 2 Warriors, Stadium Headliner's 1 Warrior, and Howlsquad Heavy's beginning-of-combat Goblin all trigger your ping sources when they enter the battlefield during the attack step. Track: how many ETBs will happen this attack? Each is noncombat damage going to face.
  3. 03
    Howlsquad Heavy's Goblin triggers at "beginning of combat" — before attackers are declared. The Goblin enters and triggers all ping sources before you declare which creatures attack. This means you get the ETB ping before combat even starts. It also means the Goblin itself can attack (Heavy gives all Goblins haste). Stack the Goblin trigger, then declare attackers including the Goblin.
  4. 04
    Stadium Headliner's sacrifice ability deals damage = creatures you control, not just attacking creatures. {1}{R}, sacrifice Stadium: deals damage equal to the number of creatures you control to target creature. With 10 creatures on board, that's 10 damage (or 40 with Ojer) to one creature. This can clear their largest blocker or deal lethal noncombat damage to them directly. Remember: Ojer makes red noncombat damage at least 4, so this kills anything with toughness ≤ 4 × creature-count.
  5. 05
    Ojer Axonil dies → returns as Temple of Power → transforms back when you deal 4+ noncombat damage in a turn. Don't overreact when they kill Ojer. He becomes a land that adds {R}. His activation: {2}{R}{T} retransforms him at sorcery speed if your red sources dealt 4+ noncombat damage this turn. With any ping source active (Sharpshooter + incoming creature), Ojer can return to the field the same turn he's killed and they've spent a kill spell for nothing.
  6. 06
    Voice of Victory prevents opponents from casting spells your turn — protect key attacks with this. Voice of Victory's text: "Your opponents can't cast spells during your turn." When Voice attacks, they can't respond with instant-speed removal during your attack step. They must have killed your key pieces BEFORE Voice's attack. This is significant: if Ojer is in play and Voice attacks creating Warriors, they cannot flash in a blocker or cast a removal spell during that attack.
  7. 07
    Warleader's Call's +1/+1 applies to ALL creatures — including tokens at point of entry. Warleader's Call gives +1/+1 to all creatures you control. Mobilize tokens enter as 1/1s — but with Call in play, they enter as 2/2s. This means their combat damage is 2 rather than 1. In a board state where you have 6 Warriors, they're all 2/2s attacking for 12 combat damage in addition to the noncombat pings. The anthem effect scales significantly with your token count.
  8. 08
    Dalkovan Encampment's activated ability creates tokens on the attack — use it mid-combat setup. {2}{W}{T}: Whenever you attack this turn, create two tapped and attacking 1/1 Warriors. Activate this BEFORE declaring attackers but during your beginning of combat step (the activation window). Those Warriors enter and trigger Sharpshooter + Call before regular attackers deal damage. Keep {2}{W} open on lands to maximize Dalkovan Encampment triggers each turn cycle.
Chapter 09

Tuning Notes

Ojer Axonil: 3 vs 4 copies
3 is correct
4 Ojer means you draw him early too often — he's a 4-drop in an aggressive deck and dead in hand T1-T3. 3 is the tournament-proven number. The transformation clause means he's effectively immune to removal anyway, so you rarely need redundant copies. Bump to 4 only if your local meta is heavy on control with multiple Inevitable Defeats per game.
Raphael, Tough Turtle: 2 copies
Flex slot, meta dependent
Raphael is the weakest link in the main deck — his Alliance trigger only activates when other creatures enter, and his 1/3 body is underwhelming compared to Voice of Victory and Sharpshooter. 2 copies is correct for the meta that includes aggro mirrors where his pings matter. In control-heavy metas, trim to 0 and add more Battle Menu or interaction main deck.
Seam Rip: 2 main deck
Conditional inclusion
Seam Rip ({1}{W} enchantment — exile ≤ 2 MV permanent until it leaves) is in 2 copies main to answer early Sapling Nursery and Stormchaser's Talent. In metas without those threats, Seam Rip is a dead draw. Consider replacing with 2 more burn spells (Sear) or a third Battle Menu for meta versatility.
Magebane Lizard SB: 4 copies
Non-negotiable vs Prowess
4 Magebane Lizard is non-negotiable in the sideboard. Izzet Prowess is the 2nd-most popular deck at Cincinnati and Magebane single-handedly wins that matchup by punishing their entire game plan (casting cheap cantrips). Never reduce below 4 unless Prowess falls off the meta entirely.
Dalkovan Encampment vs basic lands
3 is the right count
Dalkovan Encampment enters tapped unless you control a Swamp or Mountain. With 9 basic Mountains in the main deck, the untapped clause fires frequently by T2-T3. 3 copies ensures you see it mid-game to generate tokens off lands rather than cards. Running 4 causes too many T1 tapped lands that slow your T2 Sharpshooter plan.
Rest in Peace SB: 3 copies
Hard GY hate needed
3 Rest in Peace specifically blanks Ojer's death trigger (he can't transform if he's exiled rather than dying), stops Izzet GY cost reduction for Eddymurk Crab, and answers any Reanimator strategy. 3 copies ensures you see it in G2/G3 against GY-based opponents. Note: Rest in Peace exiles your own GY too — this matters if you planned to use Dalkovan Encampment's activated ability recursively.
