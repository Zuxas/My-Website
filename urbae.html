Player Profile

Urbae

"Tell me what the best stock list is. I'll sleeve it up and beat you with it."

Garrison — Urbae — is a brick-and-mortar control player who has been grinding local stores and regional events long enough to know one thing: play the best stock list available. No pet decks, no rogue tech, no fancy brews. Find the deck that wins the most, learn it to the ground, and execute.

Format doesn't matter much to Garrison — if the best deck in the room is a blue-white control pile in Standard, that's what he's playing. If it's Dimir Midrange in Pioneer, that's the call. The skill is in reading the meta correctly, selecting the optimal 75, and then not deviating from the correct line under pressure. That's what experience at the store level builds.

Playstyle

Identity
The Best Stock List
Garrison doesn't fall in love with decks — he falls in love with correct decisions. Whatever archetype has the best overall matchup spread in the current format is the deck he brings. Control is his natural home because the archetype rewards the kind of precise, measured play he's built over years of competitive reps. But he'll play aggro, midrange, or combo if the meta calls for it.
