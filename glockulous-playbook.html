Chapter 01

Identity &
Philosophy

"The field is built to fight fair. We don't fight fair."— Core Mantra
Know the deck before you play it: Grixis Reanimator is NOT a glass-cannon one-trick. It is a midrange-combo hybrid. You don't always rush to reanimate Archon — timing depends on what the opponent can interact with. Mulligan discipline and read-dependent sequencing are the highest-skill elements.

Grixis Reanimator — Team Resolve's pet name "Glockulous" — is a midrange-combo hybrid that wins through three overlapping engines: Psychic Frog as a filtering threat, Abhorrent Oculus as a token army and Emperor of Bones combo enabler, and Archon of Cruelty as the game-winning reanimate target accessed through Persist, Unearth, and Emperor.

The meta read: Modern is stocked with creature removal — Fatal Push, Lightning Bolt, Searing Blood. None of it answers Archon of Cruelty entering off Persist at instant speed. The field's interaction is calibrated for creatures on the board. This deck punishes that calibration.

Unlike Goryo's Vengeance builds, this deck does not need to assemble a specific two-card combination on turn 2. Psychic Frog provides a real clock and discard outlet. Abhorrent Oculus creates multiple threats from one card. You can win through pressure and inevitability even when the combo is disrupted.

Axis 01
Psychic Frog
A real standalone threat that grows while filtering your hand. Discard outlet for Archon and Oculus. Protects itself from burn via the lifelink clause. Every turn it's alive it's sculpting your next kill window.
Axis 02
Abhorrent Oculus
Creates tokens immediately and chains into Emperor of Bones reanimates. A resolved Oculus generates a board that demands an answer — and if they answer it, Emperor reburies it. The engine is recursive.
Axis 03
Emperor of Bones
The combo piece. When Emperor of Bones dies or is sacrificed, it reanimates an Archon of Cruelty from the graveyard. Paired with Oculus triggers, even a single dead Oculus can chain into Archon.
Axis 04
Archon of Cruelty
The reanimate target. ETB: opponent sacrifices a permanent, discards a card, loses 3 life, you draw a card and gain 3 life. That's a 7-point life swing plus card advantage before combat. Most boards cannot recover.
Strengths
What You Do Well
Archon ETB generates immediate value even through removal.

Multiple redundant reanimation paths — Persist, Unearth, Emperor.

Psychic Frog is a real clock they must answer before combo matters.

Field's removal is calibrated for different axis than you operate on.
Weaknesses
What Beats You
Nihil Spellbomb, Rest in Peace, Leyline of the Void — graveyard hate slows all three engines.

Fast combo decks race under your setup.

Requires reads on sequencing — rushing Archon into open mana is often wrong.

Consign to Memory counters Persist at instant speed.

When to choose this for an RC: When the expected field is creature-removal heavy and light on dedicated graveyard hate. When Boros Energy and Prowess are >35% of the field. When the meta data shows low Consign to Memory density in sideboards.

Chapter 03

The Game Plan

"You don't always rush the Archon. The correct timing depends on what they can interact with."— Key Principle

The Three Engines

Engine 1
Frog into Grind
Psychic Frog applies immediate pressure and discards Archon/Oculus as needed. Don't over-rely on Frog as a discard outlet — it's a real threat that needs to attack. Manage your hand size proactively.
Engine 2
Oculus into Emperor
Abhorrent Oculus creates tokens on entry. When Emperor of Bones dies, it reanimates Archon. An Oculus trigger chains into Emperor trigger chains into Archon. One card becomes three threats.
Engine 3
Persist / Unearth
Persist reanimates Archon from graveyard as an instant at end of their turn. Unearth reanimates at sorcery speed but synergizes with Emperor of Bones. Both paths are disrupted by Consign.

When to Rush the Archon vs Wait

Rush immediately when: they have no interaction visible, they've tapped out, you have Thoughtseize confirming their hand is empty, or they're on Boros Energy (can't interact with the stack).

Wait when: they're holding up mana with blue available (Consign), they've shown Force of Negation, or you have a Frog or Oculus building value — let those threats force interaction before committing the Persist.

Never cast Faithless Looting on turn 1 into Boros Energy — they have Emperor of Bones which can exile your Archon from the graveyard. This forces you into killing their Emperor instead of developing your board, losing tempo for the rest of the game.

Mulligan Philosophy

Keep: Any hand with Psychic Frog or Faithless Looting + 2 lands + a reanimation piece (Persist/Unearth/Emperor) or a path to find one. Hands with Thoughtseize protect themselves from disruption.

Mulligan: Hands with only Archon and no way to get it in the graveyard. Hands with 0-1 lands. Hands with no pressure and no reanimation.

Chapter 04

Role Assignment

Unlike pure combo decks, Glockulous has genuine flexibility. Read the matchup before assigning your role each game.

vs Aggro
Combo Clock
Apply Frog pressure while holding Persist for the window when their mana is committed to attacking. They can't interact with the stack — reanimate at instant speed at their end step when they're tapped out.
vs Midrange
Midrange Value
Psychic Frog and Oculus are real threats that must be answered. Let them trade removal, then follow up with a Persist into Archon when they're down on interaction.
vs Control
Read Their Mana
Teferi, Time Raveler from your sideboard (G2+) is the key. Under Teferi they can't Consign at instant speed. Resolve Teferi, then resolve the Persist next turn.
vs Combo
Force Their Turn
Thoughtseize their key piece. Apply Frog pressure so they must act on schedule. Harbinger of the Seas post-board shuts off nonbasic-dependent combo (Amulet, Belcher).

Chapter 06

Sideboard Guide

Consign to Memory — 4 copies
Counter their stack interaction: Consign, Force of Negation, Counterspell, cascade triggers. Don't board against pure creature matchups where they don't interact on the stack.
Harbinger of the Seas — 2 copies
Makes all nonbasic lands enter tapped. Crushes Amulet Titan, slows Blink fetching, disrupts Belcher. A reanimated Harbinger is a continuous lock. Board in wherever nonbasic disruption matters.
Meltdown — 2 copies
X=0: Leyline of the Void. X=1: Sanctifier en-Vec, Amulet of Vigor, Nihil Spellbomb. Know the CMC of the hate piece before boarding.
Mystical Dispute — 2 copies
One-mana counter vs blue spells: Counterspell, Consign, Force of Negation. Board in against any blue deck threatening your Persist / Unearth lines.
Nihil Spellbomb — 2 copies
Graveyard hate for mirrors and Goryo's / Grinding Breach decks. Cantrips so you don't fall behind. Board in mirror matches specifically.
Chapter 07

Heuristics &
Quick Rules

  1. Never cast Faithless Looting T1 into Boros Energy — Emperor of Bones can exile your Archon before you can reanimate it.
  2. Psychic Frog is a real threat, not just a discard outlet. Let it attack. The pressure forces interaction that opens Persist windows.
  3. Don't Persist into open blue mana. Consign counters it for free. Probe with Thoughtseize or wait until they tap out.
  4. Abhorrent Oculus → tokens → Emperor of Bones → Archon is your primary non-Persist path. Know the chain before starting it.
  5. Archon ETB gains you 3 life and draws a card even if they answer it. Don't concede to a Solitude — the ETB already happened.
  6. Harbinger of the Seas is a legitimate reanimate target post-board. Emperor-reanimated Harbinger locks their nonbasic lands continuously.
  7. Rush Archon if they have no open blue mana. Develop Frog/Oculus if they do. This is the highest-skill decision in the deck.
  8. Thoughtseize on T1 is your best play in most matchups. Seeing their hand tells you which reanimation line to take.
  9. If graveyard hate appears in >30% of expected sideboards, Frog/Oculus value becomes the primary plan — not Archon combo.
  10. This deck's power level is highest when the field is Boros Energy and Prowess heavy. Against Blink + Control heavy fields, reconsider the pick.
